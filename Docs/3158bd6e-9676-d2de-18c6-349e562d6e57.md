# Energy-Saving Tips for Condo Living

Even if your home has a small footprint, you can still be using energy inefficiently.

## Try these five energy-saving tips for your condo.

### 1. Think smart
*   **Description**: A smart thermostat helps control heating and cooling by automatically learning your schedule and allowing remote temperature control. This is ideal for fluctuating schedules.

_For more details, you may want to [Learn how to get the biggest savings from your smart thermostat](https://www.saveonenergy.ca/For-Your-Home/Advice-and-Tips/Smart-Thermostat-Savings)._

### 2. Beware of your condo's vampires
*   **Description**: Electronics like gaming equipment, phone chargers, and microwaves consume energy even when plugged in but not in use (phantom load).
*   **Impact**: The average microwave uses more energy to power its clock than to heat food.
*   **Recommendation**: Use a **programmable power bar with auto on/off**.
*   **Potential Savings**: Can save **10 percent on your energy costs**.

### 3. Set the right mood
*   **Description**: Lighting significantly impacts both aesthetics and energy bills.
*   **Recommendation**: Switch to **LED lighting**.
*   **Potential Savings**: LEDs use **75 to 90 percent less energy** than traditional incandescent lighting.
*   **Advanced Option**: Consider **smart lighting** for smartphone control (on/off, color changes).

### 4. Keep it clean
*   **Description**: A dust-free environment improves efficiency and reduces costs.
*   **Air Purifiers**: Look for **ENERGY STAR-certified air purifiers**.
*   **Potential Savings (Air Purifiers)**: Use **40 percent less energy** than standard models.
*   **Ventilation**: Regularly vacuum or clean vents, including the bathroom exhaust fan, for efficient operation.
*   **Filters**: Replace ventilation filters **twice a year** (or more often with pets/allergies) if your building doesn't. Dirty filters reduce efficiency and can lead to system breakdowns.

### 5. Look for energy-saving appliances
*   **Recommendation**: When purchasing new appliances (washer, dryer, fridge), choose **ENERGY STAR-certified models**.
*   **Example (Dryer)**: An **ENERGY STAR-certified dryer** uses **20 percent less energy** than standard models and often includes built-in sensors to detect when clothes are dry, saving energy and time.
*   **Example (Ceiling Fan)**: Install an **ENERGY STAR-certified ceiling fan** to reduce air conditioning costs in summer.
*   **Potential Savings (Ceiling Fan)**: Uses **60 percent less energy** than a traditional fan.
