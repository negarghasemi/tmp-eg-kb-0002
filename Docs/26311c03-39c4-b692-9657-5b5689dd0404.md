The familiar symbol indicates a government-backed program, assuring reliability of the information provided on the label.

### EnerGuide Label Significance

- The EnerGuide label signifies that a product has been **tested and verified** to meet or exceed minimum energy performance standards set by Canadas *Energy Efficiency Regulations*.
- **Applicable Products**: Major household appliances and specific heating and cooling equipment.

## Standardized testing means assurance

- Products must be tested before they can carry an EnerGuide label.
- Tests determine energy consumption under average Canadian conditions, with different measurements for different product types.
- **Standardized procedures** ensure consistent testing criteria and comparability of EnerGuide ratings across products within the same class.
- The Government of Canada aligns its standards and testing procedures with those in the United States whenever possible.

## Energy efficiency verification mark

- Regulated energy-consuming products imported to Canada or shipped between provinces/territories must bear an **energy efficiency verification mark**.
- This mark is issued by a certification body accredited for energy efficiency by the Standards Council of Canada.
- *It is **not** a safety certification mark.*
- The verification mark can appear as a small label or as an addition to the product's nameplate.
- Detailed information, including accredited certification bodies and examples of marks, is available in the [Regulations and standards section of the NRCan website](http://oee.nrcan.gc.ca/regulations/16802#verification) and the [Standards Council of Canada (SCC) website](http://www.scc.ca/).
