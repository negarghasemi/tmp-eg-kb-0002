# Clothes Washers: Energy Efficiency Overview

An **ENERGY STAR certified clothes washer** (or washing machine) uses **25% less energy** and **33% less water** on average compared to a standard model. Pairing it with an ENERGY STAR certified clothes dryer can double your savings. This not only saves money but also reduces your carbon footprint.

## Benefits of ENERGY STAR Certified Clothes Washers

-   **Front-loaders:** Tumble clothes through a small amount of water, avoiding the use of an agitator in a full tub.
-   **Advanced top-load models:** Flip or spin clothes through a stream of water, consuming less water and energy for heating.
-   **High spin speeds:** Efficient motors spin clothes two to three times faster during the spin cycle, extracting more water and reducing dryer energy consumption.
-   **Sensors:** Monitor incoming water levels and temperature for optimal efficiency.

## The ENERGY STAR Promise

All ENERGY STAR certified products are:

-   Tested to meet strict efficiency specifications.
-   Certified by an independent third party.
-   Designed to perform the same as or better than standard products without compromising performance.

## Tips for Choosing an Energy-Efficient Clothes Washer

-   Look for a higher **Integrated Modified Energy Factor (IMEF)**. *This measure factors in energy used for water heating, washing, and standby.*
-   Consult the [EnerGuide label](/energy-efficiency/product-energy-ratings/energuide/appliances-energuide) for similar models to compare annual energy consumption. *A lower number is better.*
-   Consider models with the **[ENERGY STAR Most Efficient](/energy-efficiency/energy-star/products/energy-star-most-efficient)** designation for maximum energy savings.

## How to Use Your Clothes Washer Efficiently

-   **Fill, but dont overfill:** Washers are most efficient when full, but overloading can cause damage.
-   **Choose the high-speed or extended spin option:** This extracts more water, reducing drying time and energy use.
-   **Wash in cold water:** Saves energy used to heat water. If hot water is necessary, use the minimum level and a cold-water rinse.
-   **Pre-soak items:** For heavily soiled items, pre-soaking can prevent the need for double washing.

## Energy Efficiency Regulations

Washing machines are subject to Canada's *Energy Efficiency Regulations*, which establish minimum performance standards for energy efficiency. More details can be found in the [Guide to the Regulations](/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-regulations/guide-canada-s-energy-efficiency-regulations).
