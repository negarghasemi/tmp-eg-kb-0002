# Appliances for Residential Use - Energy Efficiency

When buying appliances, it's important to remember that the **cost of energy to operate a product over its lifetime** is just as important as its purchase price.

**Make the switch to an ENERGY STAR certified appliance** to cut your electricity bill and reduce your carbon footprint. All ENERGY STAR certified products are tested to strict efficiency standards and are certified by an independent third party, ensuring they perform as well as or better than standard products without compromising performance.

## How to Pick the Most Energy Efficient Appliances

To ensure you choose the most energy-efficient appliances:

*   **Look for the ENERGY STAR symbol**: This is the easiest way to identify an efficient appliance.
*   **Consider \"ENERGY STAR Most Efficient\"**: This designation is awarded annually to products that are \"the best of the best\"  the top energy performers in their class. [Learn more about ENERGY STAR Most Efficient.](/energy-efficiency/energy-star/products/energy-star-most-efficient)
*   **Use the ENERGY STAR Product Finder**: This tool helps you find certified products. *(Note: you will be redirected to the U.S. ENERGY STAR website. Click Canada as your market).* ([https://www.energystar.gov/productfinder/](https://www.energystar.gov/productfinder/))
*   **Compare models using the EnerGuide label**: This label on all major appliances allows you to compare a models energy consumption to similar models.
*   **Check with our list of ENERGY STAR participating [retailers](/energy-efficiency/energy-star/products/retailersdealers) and [manufacturers](/energy-efficiency/energy-star/appliance-manufacturers-energy-star-canada-participants)**.

## Maintaining the Appliances in Your Home

To maximize efficiency and lifespan:

*   **Buy the right size**: Larger models typically use more energy, so purchase one that is appropriately sized for your needs.
*   **Keep appliances clean**: For example, empty the lint trap on your dryer to increase airflow and clean the coils on your fridge to maintain airflow.

## Canadians Home Appliances

## Regulated Home Appliances

Canadas [Energy Efficiency Regulations](/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-regulations/guide-canada-s-energy-efficiency-regulations) establish minimum performance standards for energy efficiency, helping to eliminate the least efficient products from the Canadian marketplace.

Some appliances are regulated but are **not eligible for ENERGY STAR certification**:

*   [Integrated washer-dryers](/energy-efficiency/product-energy-ratings/integrated-clothes-washer-dryers)
*   [Miscellaneous refrigeration products](/energy-efficiency/energy-star/products/list-certified-products/refrigerators) (e.g., coolers and wine chillers)
*   [Cooking appliances](/energy-efficiency/product-energy-ratings/cooking-appliances) (e.g., electric and gas ranges, microwave ovens, cooktops, and ovens)

Many types of home appliances must meet minimum energy efficiency standards as regulated under Canadas Energy Efficiency Regulations. [See more information about the regulations](/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-regulations/guide-canada-s-energy-efficiency-regulations) that help eliminate the least efficient products from the Canadian market.
