# Thermal Heat Pumps

*Research is conducted in collaboration with LTE/Hydro-Qubec and Universit de Sherbrooke.*

## System Overview

Small absorption cooling systems (**3 tonne capacity**) that use **steam or hot water as a heat source** are available in Europe and Asia. However, these systems currently have a significant cost disadvantage, being **almost 7 times more expensive** than those using vapor compression, making them uncompetitive.

## Research Focus

Extensive research is underway to address the current limitations and improve these systems. Key areas of investigation include:

*   **Thermodynamics**: Exploring the fundamental principles governing system operation.
*   **New Fluids**: Developing and testing innovative working fluids.
*   **Mass and Heat Transfer Optimization**: Enhancing the efficiency of energy exchange within the systems.
*   **Hybrid Cycles**: Investigating combined cycles for improved performance.

### Core Objectives:

All research efforts are geared towards:
*   **Improving performance** with low-temperature heating.
*   **Reducing the cost** and **physical size** of these systems.

## Current Research Activities

The ongoing research activities are focused on two main areas:

*   **Comparative Study**: Conducting an analysis of absorption heat pumps and ejector coolers to evaluate their respective benefits. This comparison aims to identify the most suitable system for utilizing **excess summer heat from a cogeneration unit** for air conditioning purposes.
*   **Residential Application Analysis**: Analyzing an initial application within a residential setting, specifically a **6 kWe cogeneration system with an internal combustion engine**. This involves developing simulation models and monitoring the system's behavior to assess its performance.

## Management
