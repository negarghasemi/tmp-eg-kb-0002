# Electric Vehicle Chargers

## Buy ENERGY STAR to save you money!

Driving an electric vehicle in Canada can save a lot of energy, and with ENERGY STAR certified electric vehicle supply equipment (EV chargers), you can save even more. On average, certified models **use 40% less energy than standard models** in standby mode, which typically accounts for 85% of the product's lifetime. This not only saves money but also reduces your carbon footprint.

Electric vehicle chargers provide energy to both all-electric vehicles (EVs) and plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEVs). The ENERGY STAR specification covers both Level 1 and Level 2 AC chargers.

### Key Features

*   **Basic Functionality**: Include features like auto-restart and LED status indicators.
*   **Advanced Features**: Offer programming and timers for scheduling delayed starts.
*   **Installation**: Can be installed indoors and outdoors, including home garages and parking lots. Over 10,000 Level 1 and Level 2 EV chargers are available across Canada.
*   **Smart-Grid Ready**: Some models feature Wi-Fi technology for remote power monitoring, enabling users to benefit from energy bill savings programs offered by local electric utilities.

### Helpful Tips

*   **Incentive Programs**: Contact your local electricity provider to inquire about special EV charger incentive programs. You can start your search using the [ENERGY STAR rebates and incentives finder](http://oee.nrcan.gc.ca/energy/products/energystar/why-buy/programs.cfm?attr=0).
*   **Time-of-Use Plans**: Check if your utility offers time-of-use plans. Charging overnight or on weekends when electricity costs are lower can maximize savings. Look for an EV charger with the ability to control charging times if your vehicle doesn't already have this feature.
*   **Outdoor Installation**: If your parking spot is outdoors (not in a garage), an outdoor rating for the charger is essential.
*   **Charger Levels**: Most plug-in vehicles include a Level 1 charging station, suitable for charging from a standard household outlet. This may be sufficient for plug-in hybrids with limited electric range or low driving frequency. Ask your dealer for an ENERGY STAR certified Level 1 EV charger. Upgrading to a Level 2 charger offers faster charging, which might be necessary for larger battery EVs overnight.
*   **Professional Installation**: Consider having a certified electrician install your charger and opt for one that carries at least 50 amps. This might incur a slightly higher initial cost but can lead to significant long-term savings if you acquire an electric car with a higher charging rate in the future.

### The ENERGY STAR Promise

All ENERGY STAR certified products undergo rigorous testing to meet strict efficiency specifications and are certified by an independent third party. They are guaranteed to perform as well as or better than standard products without any compromise on performance.
