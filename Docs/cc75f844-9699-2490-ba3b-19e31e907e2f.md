# Recovery Ventilators: Energy Efficiency Regulations

## Technical Bulletin on Amending the Standards

This technical bulletin, released in May 2024, initiates the consultation process for amending Canadas *Energy Efficiency Regulations* to introduce energy efficiency standards for energy and heat recovery ventilators (E/HRVs). Input from stakeholders will inform the development of these amendments. A cost-benefit analysis will be conducted, and a regulatory proposal is intended for pre-publication in the *Canada Gazette*, Part I, in 2025.

## Background

As of January 1, 2020, E/HRVs in Canada are subject to reporting and verification requirements, but currently lack energy efficiency standards. While the U.S. Department of Energy does not regulate E/HRVs, the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency's ENERGY STAR Program has efficiency requirements for the heating mode in Canada.

The Government of Canada aims to reduce energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions by improving energy efficiency standards, supporting a goal of net-zero emissions by 2050. NRCan is considering introducing energy efficiency standards for E/HRVs, potentially exceeding U.S. stringency where appropriate for climatic and market reasons.

## Product Description

-   **Energy-recovery ventilator**: A factory-built packaged unit with fans or blowers, a maximum rated airflow of not more than **142 L/s (300 ft/min) at 0C**, that transfers heat and moisture between two isolated airstreams.
-   **Heat-recovery ventilator**: A factory-built packaged unit with fans or blowers, a maximum rated airflow of not more than **142 L/s (300 ft/min) at 0C**, that transfers heat between two isolated airstreams.

## Testing Standard

NRCan is considering referencing the **CSA-C439-18** (*Laboratory methods of test for rating the performance of heat/energy-recovery ventilators*) testing standard (version 2018), as amended from time to time, to demonstrate compliance. A new edition of this standard, if published in 2024, will also be considered. All net supply airflows in tests for Sensible Heat-Recovery Efficiency (SRE) and fan efficacy requirements must be within 10% of each other.

*CSA testing standards can be ordered from the CSA website or accessed for free by creating an account on the CSA Communities of Interest portal.*

## Energy Efficiency Standard

The following energy efficiency standards are under consideration for E/HRVs:

| Operation mode | Minimum SRE at 32F (0C) | Minimum SRE at -13F (-25C) | Minimum Fan Efficacy at 32F (0C) |
| :------------- | :------------------------- | :----------------------------- | :--------------------------------- |
| Heating        | 65%                        | 60%                            | 0.57 L/s/W (1.2 cfm/W)             |

*Notes:*
 *SRE means sensible heat-recovery efficiency.*
 *Only if the unit is not marked for use only where the outdoor design temperature is greater than or equal to -10C.*

## Important Dates

-   **Coming into force**: These modifications would come into force **six months** after the date of publication in the *Canada Gazette*, Part II.
-   **Application of new standards**: NRCan is considering applying the new standards to E/HRVs manufactured on or after **January 1, 2026**.
-   **Regulatory pathway**: NRCan is considering amending the Regulations through Governor in Council amendment. The changes would be pre-published in the *Canada Gazette*, Part I, followed by a public comment period. Final publication is typically within a year of the initial public comment period.

## Labelling Requirements

NRCan is **not considering any changes** for the labelling requirements for this product.

## Verification Requirements

NRCan is **not considering any changes** for the verification requirements for E/HRVs. Products will continue to carry a third-party verification mark from a Standards Council of Canada accredited certification body, indicating compliance with energy efficiency standards and verification of reported information.

## Reporting Requirements

### Energy Efficiency Report

The energy efficiency report for E/HRVs would include the following product-specific information:

-   The **maximum rated airflow at 0C**.
-   The **sensible heat recovery efficiency (SRE) at 0C**, the associated net supply airflow (expressed in L/s), and the associated fan efficacy (expressed in L/s/W).
-   Unless the unit is marked for use only where the outdoor design temperature is greater than or equal to -10C, the **sensible heat recovery efficiency at -25C** and the associated net supply airflow (expressed in L/s).

*These regulations apply to products imported or shipped inter-provincially for sale or lease in Canada. The report must be submitted by the dealer to NRCan **before** the product is first imported into Canada or traded inter-provincially.*

### Import Report

There is **no change** to import reporting requirements.
