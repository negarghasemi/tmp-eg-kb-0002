# Eligible Products for Home Retrofit - Canada Greener Homes Initiative

## Program Status Updates

- **Canada Greener Homes Grant:** Applications closed on **December 31, 2025**. Requests submitted by this deadline will continue to be processed.
- **Canada Greener Homes Loan:** Applications closed on **October 1, 2025**. You can no longer apply for a loan.

## Overview of Eligible Products

Some retrofits under the Canada Greener Homes Grant required choosing eligible products from an approved list. The chosen equipment needed to be selected from the applicable eligible product list for the following categories:

-   **Windows and doors**
-   **Ground source heat pumps**
-   **Air source heat pumps**
-   **Cold climate air source heat pumps**
-   **Heat pump water heaters**
-   **Gas-fired boilers** *(for northern and off-grid communities only)*
-   **Gas-fired furnaces** *(for northern and off-grid communities only)*
-   **Oil-fired boilers** *(for northern and off-grid communities only)*
-   **Oil-fired furnaces** *(for northern and off-grid communities only)*

_It was recommended to learn about product and equipment eligibility criteria and consult the list of eligible products before starting upgrades._

**Important Eligibility Criteria:**

-   All products must be **purchased in Canada**.
-   Online purchases are eligible only if they were ordered from a **distributor located in Canada**.
-   Regular quarterly updates to the Canada Greener Homes Grant searchable product list occurred in **March, June, September, and December of each year**.

## Windows and Doors

To receive a grant, windows and doors had to be **ENERGY STAR certified** and appear on specific lists. ENERGY STAR certified models meet or exceed certification levels and come with a temporary label.

_**Do not remove the ENERGY STAR labels until after your post-retrofit evaluation.** These labels help your energy advisor confirm product certification._

**Eligible ENERGY STAR Certified Models and Grant Amounts:**

-   [ENERGY STAR certified windows](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=WINDOWS_GH): **$125 per rough opening**
-   [ENERGY STAR certified sliding glass doors](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=DOORS_SGD_GH): **$125 per rough opening**
-   [ENERGY STAR certified hinged doors, sidelites and transoms](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=DOORS_GH): **$125 per rough opening**
-   [ENERGY STAR MOST EFFICIENT windows](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=WINDOWS_ME_GH): **$250 per rough opening**
-   [ENERGY STAR MOST EFFICIENT sliding glass doors](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=DOORS_SGD_ME_GH): **$250 per rough opening**

## Mechanical Systems

Mechanical systems include various heat pumps, furnaces, and boilers.

_It was recommended to consult the following lists to ensure product eligibility. Search by **AHRI number** (for heat pumps) or **ENERGY STAR Unique ID** (for heat pump water heaters, furnaces, and boilers) for accurate results._

**Eligible Mechanical Systems:**

-   [Eligible ground source heat pumps  full system and pump replacement](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=HP_GS_GH)
-   [Eligible air source heat pumps and cold climate air source heat pumps](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=ASHP1_GH)
-   [Eligible cold climate air source heat pumps  Quebec and Nova Scotia only](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=ASHP2_GH)
-   [Eligible heat pump water heaters](https://spl-lrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=HP_WH_GH)

**Furnaces and boilers** were available exclusively for [northern and off-grid communities](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/canada-greener-homes-initiative/grants-canadian-homeowners-living-north-grid-communities) not connected to the North American electricity grid or natural gas network:

-   [Eligible residential gas-fired boilers](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=BOILERS_G_GH)
-   [Eligible residential gas-fired furnaces](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=FURNACES_G_GH)
-   [Eligible gas-fired furnaces for mobile homes on permanent foundations](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=FURNACES_G_RB_GH)
-   [Eligible residential oil-fired boilers](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=BOILERS_O_GH)
-   [Eligible residential oil-fired furnaces](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=FURNACES_O_GH)

## Related Canada Greener Homes Initiative Programs

-   **Oil to Heat Pump Affordability program:** Helps low- to median-income homeowners switch from oil heating to an eligible electric heat pump system. *Details vary by province and territory.*
-   **Canada Greener Homes Affordability Program:** Helps low- to median-income homeowners and tenants reduce energy bills and greenhouse gas emissions through no-cost home retrofits, delivered through provincial and territorial partners, with additional support for Indigenous organizations.
