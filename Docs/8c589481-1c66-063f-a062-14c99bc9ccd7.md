# Humidity LevelsCooling and Ventilating Equipment

**Introduction:**
Homes today are more airtight than houses of the past. This can lead to moisture buildup inside, especially during winter when windows are closed. Signs of excessive moisture include dampness, mold growth on walls, and heavy condensation on windows. Over time, this can degrade air quality and damage the home's structure.

## Suggestions to Reduce Moisture

### 1. Check your humidifier
*   If your furnace has a humidifier, **set it lower** to reduce humidity levels.

### 2. Ventilate
*   Ensure your **clothes dryer vents outside**.
*   Install **exhaust fans in bathrooms and kitchens** that vent directly to the outside.
*   Consider installing a **heat recovery ventilator** to boost ventilation and lower humidity.

### 3. Create less moisture
*   Use **lids on pots** when cooking.
*   Keep your **showers short**.
*   **Store wood outdoors**.

### 4. Keep moisture out
*   Cover exposed earth floors in basements and crawl spaces with **moisture barriers**.
*   Install a **sump pump** to remove excess moisture under the foundation.
*   **Fix water leaks** in the basement.
*   Ensure the **grading slopes away from your house** to prevent water flow towards the foundation.
*   Ensure **eaves troughs adequately divert water away** from your home.
