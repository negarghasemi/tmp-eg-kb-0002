# Energy Saving DIY Tips

Take a look below and start saving energy now!

## Begin With Simple Steps

### Air Filters
*   **Description:** Check Your Furnace Filter Frequently. Learn how and why its an energy saver.
    *   Replace all furnace filters regularly per manufacturer's recommendation; dirty filters make your furnace work harder.
    *   Hot water radiators need bleeding at least once or twice a year for efficient operation.
    *   Oil furnaces need annual maintenance; natural gas and propane furnaces need servicing every two years.
    *   Keep heat flowing by not blocking vents and air grills with objects or furniture.

### Cold Air
*   **Description:** Drafts and cold air finds the smallest places to get in the house.
    *   Cold air enters the home from unexpected places, including electrical outlets on outside walls. Use inexpensive foam insulators to stop these drafts.
    *   Weather stripping is an easy way to stop cold air from entering and warm air from leaving your house.

### LED Light Bulbs
*   **Description:** Energy saving light bulbs can save you money and reduce emissions.
    *   Switching to LED light bulbs can save significant energy and money, as they last up to 100,000 hours or 14 years.

### Window Coverings
*   **Description:** Whether you have hefty curtains or insulated blinds, they will save energy.
    *   Curtains made from heavy fabric can significantly cut down on cold, drafty air entering through windows.

### Water Heater
*   **Description:** Blankets arent just for people! Wrap your water heater and save.
    *   Consider wrapping your water heater with a blanket, especially if it's an older model or located in a cold area of the house/basement. These are inexpensive ($30-$40) and offer good cost savings.

### Drying
*   **Description:** Skip the dryer and let the clothes dry in the breeze and sunshine.

## Programs & Incentives

### Free or Low Cost Income-Qualified Programs

*   **Federal Oil to Heat Pump Affordability Program**
    *   **Payment:** Up to **$10,000** for eligible Canadian homeowners to switch from oil heating to a new energy-efficient heat pump.
    *   **Eligibility:** Home is heated by oil, household income is at or below the median household after-tax income level.
    *   **Eligible Measures:** Necessary electrical and mechanical upgrades for a new heat pump system, installation of outdoor piping loops for ground source heat pump, safe removal of oil tank, installation of backup electrical heating (as required), switching over other oil-using household systems (e.g., water heater).

*   **100% Free Cold Climate Air Source Heat Pumps (Save on Energy  Energy Affordability Program)**
    *   **Offer:** Free cold climate air source heat pumps for qualifying Ontario households living in electrically heated homes.
    *   **Warranty:** 2-year free maintenance, 10-year equipment and labor warranty.
    *   **Additional Eligibility:** May also be eligible for other free energy efficiency upgrades including professionally installed replacement ENERGY STAR appliances (refrigerators, freezers, window air conditioners, lighting), weatherstripping, attic/basement insulation, and smart thermostats/power strips.

|*   **Home Winterproofing Program (HWP) | Enbridge Gas**
    *   **Offer:** Free home energy upgrades (insulation, draft proofing, smart thermostat, Energy Star appliances and more).
    *   **Process:** Streamlined with the Energy Affordability Program for a single application.
    *   **Partner:** CLEAResult is the authorized delivery partner; CCET is a community distribution partner.
    *   **Action:** Contact CCET to receive a free Energy Saving Kit.

*   **Hydro One Low Income Energy Assistance Program (LEAP)**
    *   **Payment:** Pays up to **$1,000** of the amount owing on your bill (and up to **$1,200** if your home is electrically heated).

*   **Save on Energy  Energy Affordability Program**
    *   **Offer:** Expert advice and energy-saving home upgrades completely free of charge for income-eligible households.
    *   **Eligibility:** Responsible for paying monthly electricity bill (homeowner or renter). Qualification depends on household income from the previous year and household size, or receipt of a qualifying government or energy-support benefit. Residents of social housing may also be eligible.
    *   **Types of Support:** Comprehensive upgrades and Energy Saving Kits.

*   **Ontario Electricity Support Program (OESP)**
    *   **Offer:** Lowers electricity bills for lower-income households with a monthly credit applied directly to bills.
    *   **Credit Amount:** Varies based on household income and size.

### General Rebates & Incentives

*   **Hydro One - Ontario Electricity Rebate (OER)**
    *   **Rebate:** 13.1% reduction on the amount of your bill before HST (as of November 1, 2024).
    *   **Automatic Eligibility (no self-declaration needed):** Residential and Small Commercial customers (billed under a Residential or General Service Energy rate class) using less than 50 kW; Commercial accounts greater than 50 kW but using less than 250,000 kWh annually.

*   **Enbridge Fixed Incentive Program**
    *   **Offer:** Offsets the cost of upgrading to energy-efficient natural gas technology.
    *   **Support:** Energy Solutions Advisor assists in identifying opportunities, quantifying potential savings, and providing incentives.

*   **Enbridge Business Programs and Incentives**
    *   **Offer:** Rebates and incentives for equipment upgrades, new construction, retrofits, and energy assessments.

*   **Home Renovation Savings Program (Enbridge Gas & Save on Energy)**
    *   **Goal:** Helps Ontarians improve home energy efficiency and comfort.
    *   **Rebates for Two or More Upgrades (require a Home Energy Assessment):**
        *   **Heat Pump Water Heaters:** $500
        *   **Windows and Doors:** $100 per rough opening
        *   **Insulation (attic wall, foundation, exposed floor):** Up to $8,900
        *   **Air Sealing:** Up to $250
    *   **Rebates for One or More Upgrades (do not require a home energy assessment):**
        *   **Heat Pumps:** Up to $12,000
        *   **ASHP (Air Source Heat Pump):** Up to $7,500
        *   **Solar Panels and Battery Storage:** Up to $10,000
        *   **Smart Thermostats:** $75
    *   **ASHP Install Requirements:** Must be an Enbridge Gas customer (primarily natural gas furnace/boiler) OR connected to the Ontario electricity grid (electrically heated home). The installed heat pump must be on Natural Resources Canadas qualified products list.

### Loans & Financing

*   **Canada Greener Homes Loan (CMHC)**
    *   **Offer:** Up to **$40,000** in interest-free loans for terms up to 10 years.
    *   **Details:** More information on eligible retrofits and application process available on the Natural Resources Canada site.

*   **Clean Energy Loan  RBC Energy Saver Loan**
    *   **Offer:** Available for RBC customers for purchases like hybrid cars, ENERGY STAR appliances, and renewable energy projects.
    *   **Benefits:** Choose between a 1% interest rate reduction or a $100 rebate on a clean energy audit with your loan.

*   **Mortgage Insurance Loan Rebate (CMHC, Sagen, Canada Guaranty)**
    *   **Rebate:** 25% mortgage loan insurance rebate if you use a mortgage to buy a certified green home (e.g., R-2000, ENERGY STAR, Built Green, LEED).

*   **Canada Green Affordable Housing Program (CMHC)**
    *   **Offer:** Funding up to 100% of eligible retrofit costs to a maximum of **$170,000 per unit**; forgivable loans will be either **$85,000 per unit** or 80% of eligible costs.
    *   **Eligibility:** Homeowners meeting applicable affordability criteria.
    *   **Process:** Must apply for funding and carry out pre-retrofit activities (such as energy audits and energy modeling studies) first.
