# For applicants in participating provinces and territories

*This information is for homeowners in participating provinces and territories who are applying for the Canada Greener Homes Initiative.* 

## Oil to Heat Pump Affordability (OHPA) Program Overview

The Oil to Heat Pump Affordability (OHPA) program assists **eligible low-to-median income households** in switching from oil heating to electric heat pump systems. This includes covering the costs of purchasing and installing heat pumps, as well as additional related expenses like the safe removal of oil tanks.

**Benefits:**
- Save thousands of dollars on heating bills annually.
- Reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

## Available Funding & Incentives

### Increased Funding
- **Federal Funding:** Up to **$15,000** is available.
- **Additional Provincial/Territorial Funding:** An additional **up to $5,000** may be available for eligible homeowners in participating provinces or territories where the province/territory matches the increased funding.

### Ontario Specific Offer
- **No-cost energy-saving home upgrades:** Eligible Ontario homeowners can receive a heat pump system valued at **up to $25,000** (combining $15,000 federal funding and up to $10,000 provincial funding).
- **Note:** *No funds will be paid directly to applicants in Ontario for these upgrades, except for a bonus payment.*
- **Application:** For details and to apply, visit [IESOs Save on Energy program](https://saveonenergy.ca/en/For-Your-Home/Energy-Affordability-Program).

### $250 Bonus Payment
- A one-time payment of **$250** is offered to eligible homeowners in co-delivering provinces/territories.
- **Eligibility:** Must sign up for the OHPA program through your province or territory and get pre-approved for an eligible heat pump system.
- **Disbursement:** Once eligibility is verified, a $250 cheque will be mailed to the applicant.

## Important Alert: Be Wary of Scams

### High-Pressure Sales Tactics
- **Warning:** Be cautious of high-pressure sales tactics that claim to have ENERGY STAR or Natural Resources Canada (NRCan) backing.
- **Official Stance:** NRCan has **not approved any third parties** to make unsolicited contact with homeowners to register or participate in the Oil to Heat Pump Affordability program.
- **Key Point:** The Government of Canada, NRCan, and its associated brands (ENERGY STAR, EnerGuide) will **never solicit** over the telephone, by email, or go door-to-door to inspect, sell, or rent heating/cooling products.
- **EnerGuide Evaluations:** Home energy evaluations by licensed service organizations are only performed at the homeowner's request.
- [Learn how to recognize energy scams](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/stories/spotlight-energy-efficiency/recognize-energy-scams).

### Third Parties Cannot Apply on Your Behalf
- **Crucial Rule:** Homeowners **must apply to the OHPA program themselves**.
- **Prohibition:** Third parties (including service organizations, energy advisors, and contractors) are **not permitted** to apply for the homeowner or offer to do so under any circumstances.
