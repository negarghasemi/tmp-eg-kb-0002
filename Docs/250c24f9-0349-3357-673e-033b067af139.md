The Canada Greener Homes Loan (CGHL) application intake is now closed due to funding being fully allocated.

### EnerGuide Evaluation Requirement
*   Pre-retrofit EnerGuide evaluation results must have been submitted to Natural Resources Canada by your service organization *before applying*.
*   Submitting an application does *not* guarantee approval or funding. Funds are subject to availability.
*   Applications are processed in the order received until all funding is allocated.
*   Loan status can be checked by logging into your account through the loan portal.
*   Homeowners with existing loan approval can continue with projects and funding requests.

### Important Reminders
*   The **cost of an EnerGuide evaluation is the responsibility of the homeowner** and is not covered by the CGHL program.
*   It is strongly recommended that homeowners **do not begin retrofit work or sign binding contracts until a loan decision has been communicated**.

## Loan Details
*   **Maximum Loan**: $40,000
*   **Minimum Loan**: $5,000
*   **Repayment Term**: 10 years, interest-free
*   **Loan Type**: Unsecured personal loan on approved credit

A maximum of one loan is available per eligible property and homeowner.

*The maximum eligible loan amount is calculated based on selected retrofits and quotes, capped by industry standards and market norms. It may be less than your quoted cost, requiring you to cover the difference.*

Once approved, a portion of the loan can be provided upfront for contractor deposits. The balance is delivered after retrofits are completed and verified by a post-retrofit evaluation.

## Eligibility

### All Loan Applicants

#### Eligibility Requirements for the Loan
*   Must be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or non-permanent resident legally authorized to work in Canada.
*   Must own the home, and it must be your primary residence.
*   Must have a pre-retrofit evaluation and not yet have had a post-retrofit evaluation.
*   Pre-retrofit evaluation must have been completed on or after April 1, 2020.
*   Must have a good credit history and not be in a consumer proposal, orderly payment of debt program, bankruptcy, or equivalent insolvency proceeding.

#### Not Eligible
Loan applications are not accepted for:
*   Work that has already been started or completed.
*   Retrofits not recommended in your pre-retrofit evaluation.
*   Retrofits not included in your application (cannot add additional retrofits after approval).
*   Retrofits not eligible for a Canada Greener Homes Grant.

*Applicants will be assessed on their ability to repay the loan. Income documents will be required. Total monthly housing costs and other debt payments should not exceed 44% of gross monthly income.*

### Low-rise Multi-unit Residential Buildings
Individual homeowners in MURBs must meet [additional eligibility criteria for MURBs](/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/canada-greener-homes-initiative/multi-unit-residential-buildings-murbs).

### Indigenous Group Applicants
*   Must be eligible to register multiple homes.
*   Must own the home(s) or have a formal partnership to represent the owners; homes do not need to be the owners primary residence.
*   Home must be occupied by an Indigenous household.
*   Must have selected at least one eligible retrofit for each property subject to the loan.

### Northern and Off-grid Communities
Retrofit amounts are adjusted to provide an **additional 30%** for northern and off-grid communities due to higher costs. Homeowners in these communities are also eligible for replacements of existing fossil-fuel burning equipment and additional insulation measures (attic, ceiling, exposed floor).

[More information for northern and off-grid communities](/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/canada-greener-homes-initiative/grants-canadian-homeowners-living-north-grid-communities)

## Loan Application Information
1.  If you included retrofits in your application that you later decide not to complete, these will be subtracted from your loan before final funding. The adjusted loan amount must be at least $5,000 to remain eligible.
2.  Retrofits not included in your loan application cannot be added later.
3.  Quotes from contractors must be clear and show the cost of eligible energy-efficient retrofits.
4.  Applications will be processed in the order they are received until all funding has been allocated.
5.  It is strongly recommended that homeowners **do not begin retrofit work or sign binding contracts until a loan decision has been communicated**.

### Upfront Costs
Up to **15%** of the loan can be delivered upfront if a deposit is required by your contractor. *This initial advance is only for retrofits requiring a deposit.*

*The initial advance is increased to **25%** if your property is located in the North or off-grid.*

### Required Documents for the Loan Application
Submitting an application with incomplete or inaccurate information will cause delays. A complete application includes:
*   Pre-retrofit evaluation file number
*   Government issued photo identification (ID) with your address (e.g., drivers license). If no address, provide a utility bill.
*   Property tax bill or assessment
*   Detailed quotes from contractors or a complete material and rental estimate from suppliers
*   Proof of income and employment (two of the following):
    *   Most recent pay stub(s) dated within 60 days
    *   Letter of employment dated within 60 days
    *   Bank statement(s) showing direct deposit for the most recent two-month period
    *   Most recent T4 or Notice of Assessment

## Next Steps After Loan Approval

### 1. Complete Your Retrofits
*   Keep copies of all receipts and invoices.
*   Only costs directly related to retrofits recommended in your pre-retrofit evaluation are eligible. *The maximum eligible loan amount may be less than your quoted costs; you are responsible for any difference.*
*   **Important**: Be aware of high-pressure sales tactics. Take time to plan, gather quotes, and interview contractors.

#### Do-it-yourself (DIY) Retrofits
*   [Know which retrofits require a licensed and trained professional](/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/canada-greener-homes-initiative/step-3-retrofit-your-home#s1).
*   You are responsible for ensuring retrofits are eligible. Professional work is recommended.
*   For DIY, provide a detailed summary of materials (excluding labor costs). All products must be purchased in Canada; online purchases are eligible only from Canadian distributors. Receipts will be required.

### 2. Get a Post-retrofit Evaluation
*   Once all planned retrofits are complete, contact your service organization to request a post-retrofit evaluation.
*   Find service organizations:
    *   [For existing homes](/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/find-service-organizations-existing-homes)
    *   [Residents of Nova Scotia](https://www.efficiencyns.ca/residential/services-rebates/home-energy-assessment/#bookHea)
    *   [Residents of Quebec](https://www.quebec.ca/en/housing-territory/heating-energy-consumption/financial-assistance-retrofits/renoclimat/application/steps-to-follow)
*   An energy advisor will visit and provide a new report showing the impact of your retrofits.
*   Your service organization will upload the results, which can take up to 30 days.

### 3. Request Final Loan Advance
*   After post-retrofit evaluation results are submitted, sign back into the loan application portal and choose Request Funding.
*   Upload all final invoices for the completed work.
*   The loan will be reviewed based on actual completed retrofits. *If final costs are higher than the initial estimate, the loan amount will not increase.*

### 4. Receive Your Loan Amount
*   You will receive an email confirming the final loan amount after your funding request is approved.
*   The final approved loan amount (less any initial advance) will be deposited into your bank account *within approximately 10 days of funding request approval*.

### 5. Repay Your Loan Over Time
*   Detailed repayment information over the 10-year period will be sent shortly after your loan is funded.
