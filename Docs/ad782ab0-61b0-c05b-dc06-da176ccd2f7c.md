# Air Source Heat Pumps

The Energy Affordability Program is offering **free cold climate air source heat pumps** to **income-eligible households** with **electric or oil heating**.

## Why should you get a heat pump?

### **It's 100 percent free!**
If you qualify for the Energy Affordability Program, theres **no cost**and no catch. Everything is free, from assessment to installation.

### **Lower energy costs**
With a heat pump, youll use less energy to heat and cool your home. That means you can count on **lower energy costs** all year long.

### **More comfortable home**
Heat pumps do more than just heat and cool. They also **dehumidify** and **filter dust and allergens** from the air. All this means more comfort for you.

## What you need to know about heat pumps

### What is a cold climate air source heat pump?
A cold climate air source heat pump is a machine that runs on **electricity**, instead of burning natural gas, oil or propane, to create heat. Heat pumps are an efficient and affordable way to heat and cool spaces year-round.

### How do they work?
-   **Summer:** They transfer hot, humid air from your home to the outdoors.
-   **Winter:** They transfer heat from the outside air into your home.

_Other types of heat pumps include water source (transferring heat from water) and geothermal (transferring heat from the ground)._

### Do they work when its cold outside?
Yes, even in winter, theres enough warmth in the air for the heat pump to transfer heat to your home. Cold climate air source heat pumps are designed to work in **below-freezing temperatures**.

### Is a heat pump right for your home?
If you have **electric heating or oil heating** in your home and qualify for the Energy Affordability Program, a cold climate air source heat pump may be right for you.

### How much energy will you save?
-   Switching from an electric furnace or electric baseboard heating to a cold climate air source heat pump could save you **up to 50%** on the heating portion of your electricity bills.
-   Switching from an oil furnace to a cold climate heat pump could save you **up to 50%** on your home heating costs.

## More energy-saving upgrades available
The Energy Affordability Program offers energy-saving upgrades tailored to the specific needs of your home, all at **no cost**. You could qualify for:

### Upgrades for all homes
-   LED light bulbs
-   Energy-efficient refrigerators
-   Energy-efficient freezers
-   Energy-efficient dehumidifiers
-   Energy-efficient window air conditioner
-   Drying rack for clothes
-   Smart power strip

### Additional upgrades for homes with electric hot water heating
-   High-efficiency showerheads (standard and handheld)
-   Faucet aerators (kitchen and bathroom)

### Additional upgrades for electrically heated and oil-heated homes
-   Cold climate air source heat pump
-   Additional attic or basement insulation
-   Draft proofing
-   Programmable or Smart thermostat(s)
