# External Power Supplies: Energy Efficiency Overview

## Looking for an energy-efficient external power supply (EPS)?

**Key Points:**

*   In Canada, External Power Supplies (EPS) are required to meet **minimum performance standards** established by Canadas Energy Efficiency Regulations.
*   An EPS is a separate component that converts AC power to a lower AC or DC voltage for various electronic devices (e.g., printers, game consoles).
*   EPS units must adhere to **international energy consumption requirements**.

### Regulations and Standards

Most external power supplies fall under Canadas *Energy Efficiency Regulations*. These regulations aim to remove the least efficient products from the Canadian market by setting energy efficiency standards.

*   **Performance Standard**: View the [performance standard for external power supplies](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=ExternalPowerSupplies&page=1).
*   **Understanding Regulations**: Learn more about how Canadas [*Energy Efficiency Regulations*](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-regulations/guide-canada-s-energy-efficiency-regulations) work.
*   **Energy Savings Handbook**: Consult [*Bring Energy Savings to your Home Entertainment System*](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/sites/nrcan/files/oee/files/residential/personal/documents/ENERGY-STAR-Home-Ent-2012-E.pdf) (PDF - 1.3 MB) for information on energy efficiency in electronics.

### International Efficiency Marking Protocol

The ENERGY STAR initiative in Canada does not directly endorse External Power Supplies (EPS) or Adaptors. However, for ENERGY STAR certified products that require an EPS, the EPS must meet **Level VI or higher performance requirements** under the International Efficiency Marking Protocol. This is indicated by the Level VI Roman numeral marking.

*   **Increased Stringency**: The U.S. Department of Energy, which manages the Marking Protocol, increased the stringency of these requirements in Version 3, published September 2013.
*   **Protocol Details**: Full information on the [Marking Protocol](http://www.regulations.gov/#!documentDetail;D=EERE-2008-BT-STD-0005-0218) is available (*in English only*).
