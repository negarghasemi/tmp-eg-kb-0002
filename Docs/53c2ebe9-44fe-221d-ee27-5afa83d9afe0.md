# FAQs Electricity Rates

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How are prices set?

*The Regulated Price Plan is a forecast of the regulated prices for Ontario Power Generations (OPG) nuclear and base load hydro facilities, existing contract prices for supply from non-utility generators, and the Ontario Energy Boards forecast of electricity prices in the open electricity market over the next 12 months.*

The Ontario Energy Board (OEB) has expanded the ratio between on and off-peak prices from **2:1 to 2.1:1**. This change aims to encourage customers to shift their electricity use to off-peak times (evenings and weekends) for greater savings.

### Why does the Ontario Energy Board adjust electricity prices every 12 months?

*The Ontario Energy Board reviews and may readjust prices every 12 months to ensure what you pay for electricity accurately reflects the cost paid to generators. The Regulated Price Plan smooths these prices for you, so that you pay the true cost of electricity over time, and are not subject to day-to-day market volatility.*

### I just received my first bill and I am on a fixed income. What can I do if I cant pay my bill?

You may qualify for financial assistance programs:

*   **Ontario Electricity Support Program**: Provides a monthly on-bill credit to eligible low-income consumers. The credit amount depends on household size and combined income. Visit the [Ontario Electricity Support website](http://www.ontarioelectricitysupport.ca/) for more information.
*   **Low Income Energy Assistance Plan**: An emergency financial assistance program. Contact the [Oak Park Neighbourhood Centre](http://opnc.ca/) at [info@opnc.ca](mailto:info@opnc.ca) or Michelle Knoll at 905-257-6029.

**Payment Options:**

*   **Equal Payment Plan (EPP)**: A fixed amount is withdrawn monthly from your bank account, calculated using your average consumption over the previous 12 months. The EPP amount is reviewed annually and may adjust based on billing history.
*   Contact Oakville Hydro directly at **905-825-9400 extension 4463** to discuss payment terms.

### Is there anything I can do to decrease my bill?

Oakville Hydro provides tools to help customers manage energy usage and costs:

*   **MyOakvilleHydro online portal**: Allows customers to view account details, manage electricity usage, and review personal time-of-use dashboards. Features include:
    *   Mobile access
    *   eBilling (paperless bills)
    *   Enhanced time-of-use charts
    *   Data downloads
    *   Price plan comparison tool
    *   Alerts for predicted time-of-use charges
    *   \"Pay My Bill\" option
*   **Conservation Initiatives**: Oakville Hydro is committed to building a culture of conservation through its Conservation and Demand Management initiatives, offering customers opportunities to actively save energy.

### Does Oakville Hydro keep all of the money it collects?

No. Oakville Hydro serves as the billing agent and collects money on behalf of other electricity market participants and the government. **Oakville Hydro's portion of a residential customers electricity bill is approximately 20%**, which is part of the delivery charge line item on electricity bills.

### What is the role of a local distribution company, such as Oakville Hydro?

Electricity is transmitted over high-voltage transmission lines (mostly owned and operated by Hydro One in Ontario) between generating stations and local distribution areas. Oakville Hydro delivers electricity directly to the homes and businesses within its service area through its wires.
