# Step 2A. Pre-retrofit EnerGuide evaluation

**Note**: *The Canada Greener Homes Grant is now closed. December 31, 2025, was the last day to submit documents. Requests submitted by the deadline will continue to be processed.*

Canada Greener Homes Grant applicants were required to complete the entire retrofit journey to be eligible for funding. This included arranging two EnerGuide evaluations: a pre-retrofit evaluation and a post-retrofit evaluation, conducted by an energy advisor registered with Natural Resources Canada (NRCan).

### Purpose of the Pre-retrofit EnerGuide Evaluation
The pre-retrofit EnerGuide evaluation provides expert advice on:
- What retrofits make the most sense for a home.
- How to improve the energy performance of a home.
- Example: For solar panels, the evaluation explains how to conserve more of the renewable energy produced at home.

*It was recommended that homeowners ensure their energy advisor evaluated potential energy efficiency improvements listed on the eligible retrofits page.*

## 1. Schedule your pre-retrofit EnerGuide evaluation

Homeowners selected a **service organization** (companies that deliver EnerGuide evaluations and work with energy advisors registered with NRCan) to conduct the evaluation. A representative from the selected organization would then contact the homeowner to schedule it.

### Cost of your EnerGuide evaluation
- Homeowners paid upfront for their pre- and post-retrofit EnerGuide evaluations.
- Prices varied across Canada based on factors like location and home size.
- Homeowners could receive up to **$600** as a maximum contribution toward the total cost of both evaluations. This amount might not cover the full cost.
- **Keep all documents and receipts until March 31, 2028.**
- **Reminder**: Homeowners were required to complete at least one eligible retrofit and the full grant process to receive the contribution towards the evaluation fees.

### What happens if I do not hear from my service organization?
Due to high demand, response times varied. Homeowners were advised to follow up with their service organization or contact other organizations in their area if the evaluation wasnt scheduled within a few weeks.

## 2. Review the results of the pre-retrofit EnerGuide evaluation

Upon completion, the energy advisor provided:
- An **EnerGuide home rating and a label**.
- A **Homeowner Information Sheet**, detailing assessed building components and energy usage.
- A **Renovation Upgrade Report** with recommended retrofits.

This information helped homeowners decide which retrofits to undertake.

### Report Delivery
- Energy advisors were required to send reports (mail or email) within a minimum of **14 days** after the evaluation.
- Service organizations were required to submit reports to the online Canada Greener Homes portal within a minimum of **30 days**.
- Homeowners could access their reports by logging into the portal.

Learn how to interpret the results of your home evaluation at: [After your EnerGuide home evaluation](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/product-energy-ratings/energuide/after-your-energuide-home-evaluation).

## Canada Greener Homes Initiative
- **Oil to Heat Pump Affordability program**: helps low- to median-income homeowners switch from oil heating to an eligible electric heat pump system. Details vary by province and territory.
- **Canada Greener Homes Affordability Program**: helps low- to median-income homeowners and tenants reduce their energy bills and greenhouse gas emissions through no-cost home retrofits, delivered through provincial and territorial partners, with additional support for Indigenous organizations.
