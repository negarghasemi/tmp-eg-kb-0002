# Canada Greener Homes Grant at a Glance (as of June 2024)

## National Overview

*   **Households completed retrofits and received a grant**: 239,696
*   **Applications being processed**: 220,932
*   **Average grant received per household**: $4,413
*   **Total grants issued to date**: $1.06B
*   **Homeowners choosing to install a heat pump**: 1 out of 2
*   **Heat pumps installed to date**: 135.9K
*   **Heat pumps expected by program end**: 250K

## Top 5 Retrofits (Canada-wide)

*   **Heat pumps**: 135,900
*   **Windows and doors**: 88,000
*   **Home insulation**: 45,000
*   **Air sealing**: 41,000
*   **Solar panels**: 27,617

## Environmental and Economic Highlights

*   **Greenhouse gas reductions**: 282,841 metric tonnes
*   _Equivalent to_: 86,770 cars off the road
*   **Average annual energy cost savings per household**: $386
*   **Average annual greenhouse gas reductions per household**: 1.18 tonnes
