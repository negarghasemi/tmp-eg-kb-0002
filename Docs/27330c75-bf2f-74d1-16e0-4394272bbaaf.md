# Peak Perks Program

**Peak Perks** is a program designed to help Ontario residents and small businesses reduce energy use during peak electricity demand periods, especially on hot summer days, by automatically adjusting smart thermostats. Participants receive financial incentives for their enrollment and continued participation, while also contributing to grid stability and reliability.

## Key Benefits & Incentives

*   **Enrollment Incentive**: **$75** virtual prepaid Mastercard when you sign up.
*   **Annual Participation Incentive**: **$20** virtual prepaid Mastercard each year you stay enrolled.
    *   _You will receive the prepaid card within 60 days of being accepted into the program._
    *   _The virtual prepaid Mastercard expires within six months after it is issued._
*   **Impact**: Contributes to Canadas biggest virtual power plant, which has saved enough energy to supply an entire city the size of **Oshawa**.
*   **Personal Savings**: Reduce your energy use at home or at work.
*   **Community Benefit**: Help reduce demand on Ontarios grid and support reliable, affordable energy for all.

## How Peak Perks Works

Peak Perks utilizes eligible smart thermostats to make brief, time-limited adjustments during periods of high electricity demand.

1.  **Before an adjustment**: Your thermostat may be adjusted down slightly to pre-cool your home or business, ensuring comfort during the event.
2.  **During an adjustment**: Your thermostat will be automatically adjusted up to **two degrees Celsius** above the current temperature setting, for a maximum of **three hours** on non-holiday weekday afternoons.
3.  **After an adjustment**: Your thermostat will automatically return to its original temperature setting.

*You are always in control*. You will be notified before every adjustment and can opt out at any time by manually adjusting your thermostat or using your providers mobile app.

## Eligibility Criteria

To participate in the Peak Perks program, you must meet the following requirements:

*   **Residential Customers**:
    *   Be a residential electricity customer in Ontario (excluding Cornwall, which is connected to Hydro Quebec).
    *   Have central air conditioning that is controlled by your smart thermostat. Heat pumps that are part of central air conditioning are also eligible.
    *   Use an eligible, Internet-connected smart thermostat.
    *   Not be participating in another demand response program in Ontario that automatically adjusts your thermostat to maintain grid reliability.
        *   _Exception_: Residents of Sarnia participating in the Enbridge Gas Sarnia Saves program can also participate in Peak Perks.
    *   _For Condos/Apartments_: Low-rise single-family units with dedicated independent AC equipment are eligible. Units in high-rise buildings using large central HVAC plants are **not eligible**.
    *   _Renters_: Eligible if all other program requirements are met.
    *   _Multiple Properties_: You may enroll multiple properties if all program requirements are satisfied, with each device controlling a separate AC system.
    *   _Device Cap_: The incentive is capped at **three devices per eligible residential address**. A fourth device can enroll but won't receive incentives.
*   **Small Business Customers**:
    *   Be a business with **50 or fewer employees** on site at any point in time, under the full operating capacity of that business.
    *   Have central air conditioning that is controlled by your smart thermostat. Heat pumps that are part of central air conditioning are also eligible.
    *   Use an eligible, Internet-connected smart thermostat.
    *   Not be participating in another demand response program in Ontario that automatically adjusts your thermostat to maintain grid reliability.
    *   _Device Cap_: The incentive cap is **not applicable for small businesses**. All eligible smart thermostats in a small business facility connected to a separate AC system are eligible for an incentive.

## Incentive Details and Management

*   **Enrollment Incentive**: **$75** virtual prepaid Mastercard sent via email within 60 days of acceptance.
*   **Annual Incentive**: **$20** virtual prepaid Mastercard sent in 2025 (if enrolled before Sept 30, 2024) and in 2026 (if enrolled before Sept 30, 2025) for continued participation.
*   **Email Address**: Incentives are sent to the email used for registration from `peakperks@redeem.energyhub.com`. Add this to your whitelist.
*   **Virtual Card Usage**: Can be used online or in stores by adding it to your mobile digital wallet (Apple Pay, Google Wallet) via the My Card Wallet app or manual entry.
*   **Physical Card**: A physical prepaid Mastercard can be requested by contacting `support@cdco.io`.
*   **Thermostat Replacement**: If you replace your thermostat, you will not receive a new enrollment incentive, but you remain eligible for the annual participation incentive.

## Program Support and Privacy

*   **Contact for Enrollment/Program Questions**: Email `info@peakperks.ca` or call (877) 793-7375.
*   **Contact for My Prepaid Center Issues**: Call 1-888-371-2109.
*   **Unenrollment**: To unenroll, email `info@peakperks.ca`. You will not receive the annual participation incentive if you unenroll.
*   **Data Collection**: Yes, the program collects smart thermostat data and energy use in accordance with the terms and conditions. Your informed consent is obtained before collecting any information.
*   **Privacy Protection**: The Independent Electricity System Operator (IESO) and its authorized partners (EnergyHub, Choice Digital, Blackhawk Network) are committed to protecting personal information in accordance with privacy laws. Robust safeguards are maintained.
*   **Fraud Alert**: Save on Energy does not solicit participation via direct calls or home visits. Report any suspicious contact to `saveonenergy@ieso.ca`.

## Program Administration

Save on Energy works with authorized third parties, EnergyHub and its service providers Choice Digital and Blackhawk Network, to administer elements of the Peak Perks program, including incentive fulfillment.
