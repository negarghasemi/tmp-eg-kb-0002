# Drain water heat recovery

## Recycling heated waste water

![Drain water heat recovery pipe](/sites/nrcan/files/energy/energystar/drain%20water%20heat%20recovery%20pipes.png)

**Dont let your energy dollars go down the drain.**

A **drain water heat recovery (DWHR) pipe** is a simple technology to save on energy used to heat water.

### How it works:
*   This pipe takes advantage of the warm water flowing down the drains to preheat the water going into the hot water tank.
*   Preheating the water reduces the amount of energy needed to heat the water to the set temperature.
*   Cold water coming into your house runs through a series of coils that are tightly wrapped around your drain stack.
*   The heat from the drain water is transferred through the copper walls of the heat recovery unit to warm the cold water before it goes into the water heater.
*   The heat is transferred but the drain water never mixes with the fresh water.

### Benefits:
*   This simple technology has no moving parts and is long-lasting.

### Effectiveness:
*   A study by the Canadian Centre of Housing Technology stipulates that DWHR units are most effective when you have large drain water events, where a lot of warm water is being drained while simultaneously drawing potable (domestic) cold water through the faucet.
*   This mostly occurs with showers.
