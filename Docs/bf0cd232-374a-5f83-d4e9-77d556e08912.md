# Refrigeration Energy Efficiency Report

This report focuses on energy efficiency in **commercial and institutional buildings** with refrigeration systems.

## Property Types Addressed

-   Arenas
-   Curling rinks
-   Supermarkets
-   Refrigerated warehouses

## Key Energy Management Concerns

These types of buildings are identified as a **primary source of greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions** due to:

-   **High energy consumption**
-   **Use of synthetic refrigerants**

## Integrated Approach to Reduce Energy Consumption and GHG Emissions

CanmetENERGY recommends the **CoolSolution approach** to optimize energy management and reduce GHG emissions. This approach combines advanced practices and technologies in the design and operation of refrigeration systems.

### Demonstrated Achievements (CoolSolution Projects)

-   **Energy Consumption Reduction:**
    -   Almost **50%** in arenas and curling rinks
    -   **25%** in supermarkets
-   **Synthetic Refrigerant Leak Reduction:**
    -   **75%** reduction

## Development and Optimization of Emerging Refrigeration Technologies

CanmetENERGY conducts research and development (R&D) to contribute to more efficient refrigeration systems. The R&D focuses on:

-   Applications and components of **heat pump systems**
-   **Ground-source heat pumps**
-   **Ejectors**
-   **Thermal storage using ice slurry**
