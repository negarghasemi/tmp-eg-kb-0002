# Make the most of your energy-efficient backyard this summer

## Save energy with these tips to help make your backyard comfortable and energy efficient.

Whether you have a backyard, balcony, courtyard, or garden, make the most of the long days of summer with some simple upgrades that will help your outdoor living space become more energy efficient.

### 1. Swap your outdoor light bulbs for LEDs

**Recommendation:** Upgrade your light bulbs to **ENERGY STAR certified LEDs**.

**Benefits:**
- Last up to **25 times longer** than traditional incandescent bulbs.
- Use about **70 to 90 percent less energy**.
- Available in various shapes, sizes, and colours to suit personal style.

*Go further: Keep light bulb colour temperature in mind to create the right ambience. Choose warm hues. Look for ENERGY STAR certified fixtures, which use a quarter of the electricity that standard fixtures use.*

### 2. No Plug? Use solar-powered lights

**Recommendation:** Utilize **solar-powered lights** in areas lacking electrical plugs.

**Benefits:**
- Automatically charges during the day.
- Discharges at night, lasting anywhere between eight and 10 hours.
- **No added costs** to your electricity bill.

### 3. Set timers for lighting and water features

**Recommendation:** Use **heavy-duty outdoor timers** for patio lights and outdoor water features (ponds, fountains).

**Benefits (Lighting):**
- Set patio lights to turn on just before sundown and turn off before sunrise to **avoid wasting energy**.

**Benefits (Water Features):**
- Installing a timer on your pump can help **save on your monthly electricity bill**.
- Ensure your pump is off during daytime peak energy hours and circulate water in the evening.

*Go further: Use smart plugs outdoors to control and monitor plug-in lights or equipment. These easy, inexpensive plugs can be configured using an app to monitor and optimize energy consumption.*

### 4. Seek out battery-powered lawn care equipment

**Recommendation:** Opt for **electric lawn care tools**, including mowers, trimmers, and leaf blowers.

**Benefits:**
- Helps the environment and **saves money** in the long run.
- Replacing half the gas-powered lawn mowers with electric counterparts would reduce emissions equivalent to **removing two million vehicles from the road**.
- Eliminates the need for winter storage.
- Produces **far less noise pollution**.

*Go further: When buying electric seasonal equipment, look for tools that use the same batteries for easy swapping.*

### 5. Use a solar blanket to prevent your pool from losing heat

**Recommendation:** Incorporate a **solar blanket** into your regular pool heating routine.

**Problem:** A pool can lose up to **50 percent or more of the heat** that accumulates during the day due to evaporation, especially when the weather is cool and windy.

**Benefits:**
- Minimize water loss.
- Raise pool temperatures.
- **Reduce energy costs**.

### 6. Maintain foliage around your AC unit

**Recommendation:** Trim any foliage around your air conditioning unit back at least **two feet**.

**Reason:** To optimize air flow and keep your AC unit running efficiently.
