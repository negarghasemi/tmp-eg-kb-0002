# Electric Vehicle Supply Equipment

**Non-Residential EV Supply Equipment Connections**

This document outlines the process for connecting Electric Vehicle Supply Equipment (EVSE) that are non-residential. This includes, but is not limited to, Level 2 and Level 3 charging stations such as:

*   Publicly accessible direct current fast charging stations
*   Workplace charging facilities
*   Charging stations for commercial EV fleets
*   Charging installations for multi-unit residential or commercial buildings (where EV chargers are owned or operated by the building owner or a third-party charging provider)

For detailed information regarding the Electric Vehicle Charging Connection Procedure (EVCCP), please refer to **Appendix 1 of the Conditions of Service**.

### Preliminary Consultation (Optional)

For non-residential EV charging connections (Level 2 and above), typically servicing fleet connections or multiple charging facilities, an optional preliminary consultation is available.

*   **Process**: Fill out the [EVPCIR Form](https://www.oakvillehydro.com/sites/oakvillehydro/files/2024-05/Oakville%20Hydro_EVCCP_EVPCIR%20and%20EVPCR%20Form_0.XLSX) and submit it to [engineering@oakvillehydro.com](mailto:engineering@oakvillehydro.com).
*   **Response Time**: Oakville Hydro will respond within **15 calendar days** to confirm the form's completeness or request additional information.

### Connection Request

To connect an EVSE to Oakville Hydros grid, a commercial connection request application must be submitted by following these steps:

1.  Visit the [forms and information page](https://www.oakvillehydro.com/accounts-customer-services/forms-and-information) and navigate to the new connections and upgrades heading.
2.  Complete and submit the appropriate form for either a **primary** or **secondary new connection**.

*   **Response Time**: Oakville Hydro will respond within **15 calendar days** to confirm the connection request's completeness or request additional information.
*   **Offer to Connect**: Once the connection request submission is deemed complete, Oakville Hydro will provide an offer to connect within **45 calendar days**.

### Important Note

This process is **NOT applicable** to EV chargers installed by individual residential customers or unit owners/tenants of a multi-unit residential building.
