# EnerGuide Rating System for Builders

## A respected mark of energy performance

Natural Resources Canadas (NRCan) [EnerGuide Rating System](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/energuide-rated-new-homes) helps homebuilders evaluate their house plans for energy performance and provide customers with energy-efficiency upgrades.

Finished homes receive an **EnerGuide label** that is recognized across Canada. This label adds value for customers by informing them about their homes energy performance.

NRCans **ENERGY STAR for New Homes** and **R-2000** initiatives utilize the EnerGuide Rating System to measure a home's energy efficiency.

## Become a Registered Builder

Follow these simple steps to become an EnerGuide registered builder:

1.  [Contact a service organization](http://www2.nrcan.gc.ca/oee/nh-mn/f-t/index.cfm?fuseaction=s.ssf&language=eng) in your area.
2.  Discuss how to register.
3.  Register with NRCan by email and sign the Terms and Conditions for using the EnerGuide Rating System.

## How to Get Your Homes Rated with EnerGuide

1.  An **energy advisor** will collaborate with a [service organization](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/learn-about-licensed-service-organizations) to evaluate your house plans using energy simulation software to estimate future energy consumption.
2.  Following the evaluation, the energy advisor will assist in developing **cost-effective energy efficiency upgrades** to offer customers.
3.  Once the home is built, the energy advisor performs an on-site visit to verify the incorporation of energy upgrades and conduct a **blower door test**.
4.  After this final evaluation, you will receive an **EnerGuide label** for the house and a report for the homeowner.

## Benefits of Being an EnerGuide Registered Builder

*   **Government backed**: Enhance your business by offering a nationally recognized label for energy efficiency.
*   **Customer satisfaction**: Provide valuable information about home energy performance, helping homeowners reduce energy consumption and utility bills.
*   **Savings**: EnerGuide rating assists in selecting the most realistic and **cost-effective energy improvements** for your house plans.
*   **Maximum flexibility**: Choose to include energy-efficiency upgrades as a standard package or offer them as additional options to homebuyers.
*   **Ongoing support and learning opportunities**: Gain access to NRCans tools and standards to integrate energy efficiency into new home designs.
