# ENERGY STAR for New Homes Standard

## Overview

*   **Promoted by**: Office of Energy Efficiency of Natural Resources Canada
*   **Purpose**: An initiative designed to encourage energy-efficient construction in new housing.
*   **Goal**: Helps reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

## Standard Requirements and Benefits

*   **Standard**: The ENERGY STAR for New Homes Standard sets out specific requirements.
*   **Key Requirement**: Enables new homes to be approximately **20% more energy efficient** than those built to the Provincial or National Building Code.
*   **Benefit for Homeowners**: The increased energy efficiency of these homes translates into **reduced energy costs**.

## Opaque Assemblies

*   **Purpose**: The *Tables for Calculating Effective Thermal Resistance of Opaque Assemblies* provides a method for calculating the effective thermal resistance of opaque assemblies.
*   **Usage**: 
    *   Currently used to demonstrate compliance with the prescriptive approach under the ENERGY STAR for New Homes Standard.
    *   Referenced in the National Building Code of Canada for calculating additional assemblies and configurations.
*   **Content**: This document provides easy-to-use look-up tables for the effective thermal resistance of assemblies containing both framing member and cavity insulation.
