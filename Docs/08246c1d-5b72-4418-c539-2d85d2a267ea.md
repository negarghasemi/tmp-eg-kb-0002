# Home Renovation Savings Program: Help and Support

Find answers to common questions about the program categories. 

## Upgrades Requiring a Home Energy Assessment

*Heat pump water heaters, insulation, windows/doors and air sealing*

### Which upgrades require a home energy assessment?

**Heat pump water heaters, windows, doors, insulation** (attic, wall, foundation and exposed floor), and **air sealing** require a home energy assessment before starting any work. You must install at least **two** of these upgrades to qualify for rebates.

### Why do I need to install at least two upgrades?

Your home functions as a system, where different parts affect each other's performance. For example, without proper air sealing, new insulation may not be fully effective. A home energy assessment helps identify optimal energy-efficiency solutions. It is recommended to follow your registered energy advisor's advice.

### What is a home energy assessment and how long does it take?

A home energy assessment helps understand your home's energy consumption and identifies upgrades to improve efficiency. A registered energy advisor assesses your home from basement to attic, providing an **EnerGuide rating** and an **energy-efficiency report**. An initial assessment takes about **two hours**, and a follow-up assessment takes about **one hour**.

### Do I have to pay for the home energy assessment?

Homeowners are responsible for the upfront cost of both initial and follow-up assessments. To offset these costs, you will receive a **$600 rebate** after installing at least two recommended upgrades from the Home Renovation Savings program.

## Appliances

### How can I apply for a rebate?

1.  **Purchase** an eligible appliance from any retailer, online or in-store.
2.  **Apply** for your rebate by clicking the \"Apply for rebate\" button on the website. Upload your receipt within **60 days** of purchase.
3.  **Install** your new appliance and recycle/properly dispose of your old one.

### What is the purpose of this rebate program?

The program encourages **energy efficiency** by offering financial incentives for purchasing qualifying appliances. These appliances use less energy, leading to **electricity bill savings** and reduced environmental impact.

### Who is eligible to apply for a rebate?

To qualify, you must:

*   Be a **residential customer** connected to the IESO-grid by a local distribution company (LDC).
*   Purchase and install the appliance at a residence connected to the IESO-grid.
*   Submit the rebate application within **60 days** of purchase.

### How do I know what appliances qualify?

Qualifying fridges, freezers, heat pump clothes dryers, clothes washers, and induction cooktops/ranges are eligible. Always check the list of eligible models or visit the **ENERGY STAR Most Efficient website**. *Note: induction stovetops only need to be ENERGY STAR certified.*

### Can I receive multiple rebates?

Yes, you may qualify for multiple rebates if you purchase and install more than one eligible appliance. Rebates are limited to **two rebates per appliance category per home**.

### How long does it take to receive my rebate?

Rebate processing typically takes **4-8 weeks** from the time a complete application is received. Delays may occur if information is missing or during high-volume periods.

### Do used or refurbished appliances qualify?

No. Only **new, unused qualified appliances** are eligible. Participants can purchase appliances from any store.

### What is ENERGY STAR?

The ENERGY STAR symbol is an internationally recognized and trusted mark of high efficiency. It certifies a product, new home, building, or industrial facility as energy efficient, helping Canadians save money on energy bills, increase competitiveness, and fight climate change. The **ENERGY STAR Most Efficient** designation recognizes the most efficient ENERGY STAR certified products. *For more information, visit [ENERGY STAR Canada's website](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/energy-star-canada).* 

### Why are only select appliances eligible?

The program focuses on incentivizing **top-performing, most energy-efficient products** to ensure greater energy savings for participants and the electricity grid. This approach maximizes the return on investment for both participants and the grid.

### Why are the incentives set at these amounts?

Incentive amounts are designed to help bridge the cost gap between eligible ENERGY STAR (for induction stoves) or ENERGY STAR Most Efficient appliances and the cost of average appliances typically purchased by residential customers.

## Attic Insulation

*Available for attics, cathedral ceilings and flat roofs*

### If I own multiple properties, can I participate in the program for each property, even for a home that is not my primary residence?

Yes, you can participate for each property you own, provided all eligibility criteria are met for each application. If there are tenants, the owner must apply.

### What if I am a tenant, can I participate in the program?

No, only the **owner** of the property is permitted to participate.

### Can I receive additional rebates from other programs for attic insulation (attic/cathedral ceiling/flat roof)?

A participant can stack incentives from other funding organizations with some limitations. You **may not stack** with other incentives offered by Enbridge Gas, IESO's Save on Energy, or the Government of Ontario. If you have received or expect to receive rebates from other utilities, municipal, or government programs, you must advise your participating contractor. The combined rebate total (from this program plus any additional rebates) must **not exceed 50% of the total cost** (after tax) spent on the measure.

### How is the attic insulation incentive determined?

The rebate is calculated at **50% of the total attic insulation cost** (after tax), up to the maximum rebate amounts listed below. The total rebate for attic is up to **$1,250** and up to **$750** for the flat roof attic and/or cathedral ceiling.

#### Attic:

| Level of Insulation at the time of the in-home visit | Minimum insulation level to reach | Maximum rebate |
| :--------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------- | :------------- |
| Less than or equal to R-12                           | R-50                              | **$1,250**     |
| Greater than R-12 to R-25                            | R-50                              | **$1,000**     |
| Greater than R-25 to R-35                            | R-50                              | **$800**       |

#### Cathedral ceiling/flat roof:

| Level of Insulation at the time of the in-home visit | Minimum insulation level to reach | Maximum rebate |
| :--------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------- | :------------- |
| Less than or equal to R-12                           | R-20                              | **$650**       |
| Less than or equal to R-25                           | R-28                              | **$750**       |

#### More than one roof assembly example:

| Roof assembly type | Level of insulation at the time of the in-home visit | Minimum insulation level to reach | Total insulation cost (after tax) | Maximum rebate                                   |
| :----------------- | :--------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------- | :-------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- |
| Attic              | Greater than R-12 to R-25                            | R-50                              | $1,400                            | $700 *($1,400 x 50%)*                            |
| Cathedral Ceiling  | Less than or equal to R-12                           | R-20                              | $1,400                            | $650 * (50% of $1,400 exceeds Maximum Rebate)* |

### Can I work with any contractor to add insulation to my roof assembly (attic/cathedral ceiling/flat roof)?

No, only **participating contractors** may be used to complete the work.

### Can I self-install the insulation in the attic/cathedral ceiling/flat roof and apply for the rebate?

No, only upgrades installed by a **participating contractor** are eligible for a rebate.

### Does Enbridge Gas or the IESO solicit participation for the program by calling or coming door to door?

No, neither Enbridge Gas nor the IESO will solicit customers over the phone or door to door.

## Heat Pumps

*Cold climate air source heat pumps and ground source heat pumps*

### What are the eligibility requirements to receive a heat pump rebate?

To receive a heat pump rebate:

*   You must be an **Enbridge Gas customer** with an active account and primarily heat your home with a natural gas furnace or boiler, **OR** be connected to the **Ontario electricity grid** and heat your home with electricity, oil, propane, or wood. (Cornwall Electric customers are generally not eligible unless primarily heated by Enbridge Gas natural gas).
*   You must live in a **single detached, semi-detached, row house, townhome, or mobile home on a permanent foundation**. New build homes do not qualify, unless the new home has achieved at least six months of occupancy.
*   You must **own the home** where the heat pump will be installed and be responsible for its purchase and installation. (Where an owner has tenants, the owner must apply).
*   Eligible heat pumps must be the **first-time installation** of a space heating heat pump system. Existing heat pump systems for space heating are not eligible.
*   The installed heat pump must be on **Natural Resources Canadas qualified products list** as either a cold climate air source heat pump or ground source heat pump.
*   The heat pump can work as a standalone system or with a backup heating system (e.g., natural gas furnace, electric resistance), but only the heat pump system qualifies for the rebate.
*   For natural gas and propane-heated homes, a new or existing furnace can be paired with the heat pump.
*   For electrically heated homes, you may keep your existing electrical backup.
*   For mini- or multi-split units, **both indoor and outdoor units must be installed**. Replacing only one component or adding a new indoor unit is not eligible.
*   Oil-heated homes must size their electric heating system (heat pump and electric resistance backup, if needed) to **fully offset their oil heating load**.

*For the full list of eligibility requirements, please visit the Terms and Conditions page.*

### Can I work with any contractor to complete the installation of my heat pump?

You must work with a **participating contractor**. To get the list, fill out the short form on the website, and you will receive an email with a link to access it. If your preferred contractor is not listed, encourage them to register. The contractor must be registered and listed **before** you proceed with installation to qualify for the rebate.

*Small print: Enbridge Gas and IESO do not endorse any contractors and are not liable for their performance or results. They do not guarantee work quality.* 

### Where can I find more information about heat pumps?

Natural Resources Canada has [information about heat pumps](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/heat-pumps/heat-pump-basics/25589).

### Can I receive additional rebates from other programs for the installation of my heat pump?

A participant can stack incentives from other funding organizations, with some limitations. You **may not stack** with other incentives offered by Enbridge Gas, IESO's Save on Energy, or the Government of Ontario. Total incentives from all sources must **not exceed 100% of the costs** to supply and install the heat pump measure.

### I have multiple homes. Can I participate in the program multiple times, even if not all homes are my primary residence?

Yes. You can participate for each property you own, provided all eligibility criteria are met for each application.

### Im a tenant. Can I participate?

No. You must **own the home** to participate.

### Do Save on Energy or Enbridge Gas solicit participation for the program by phone or door to door?

No. Save on Energy or Enbridge Gas do not solicit residential customers over the phone or door to door. Refer to the [Scams page](https://www.enbridgegas.com/ontario/contact-us/scam-awareness) for more information. If you receive such calls/visits, exercise caution and contact [saveonenergy@ieso.ca](mailto:saveonenergy@ieso.ca).

### Why are some electricity customers not eligible for the Home Renovation Savings program?

If your home is heated with natural gas, you must be an **Enbridge Gas customer**. If heated with electricity, you must be connected to **Ontarios electricity grid**. Cornwall Electric customers are connected to the Hydro-Quebc electricity grid and are currently only eligible if they are Enbridge Gas customers.

### When will I receive my rebate?

After heat pump installation, your contractor will submit a post-installation application. Once approved, your cheque should arrive by mail within **60 days**.

### How is the heat pump incentive determined?

| Baseline Heating Fuel       | Heat Pump Measures     | Eligible Measure Rebate | Qualifiers   |
| :-------------------------- | :--------------------- | :---------------------- | :----------- |
| Electricity/oil/propane/wood | ccASHP Full Equipment  | **$1,250/ton***          | Up to $7,500 |
| Electricity/oil/propane/wood | GSHP Full System       | **$2,000/ton***          | Up to $12,000 |
| Enbridge Natural Gas        | ccASHP Full Equipment  | **$500/ton***            | Up to $2,000 |
| Enbridge Natural Gas        | GSHP Full System       | **$3,000**              | flat rate    |
| Rental                      | ccASHP Full Equipment  | **$500/ton***            | Up to $2,000 |
| Rental                      | GSHP Full System       | **$3,000**              | flat rate    |

* *Incentives for ccASHPs are calculated based on the total rated heating capacity at 8.3 (47). Incentives for GSHP are calculated based on closed loop heating capacity (electric customers only).* 

### Do I need to remove my existing oil heating system to receive a heat pump rebate?

No. For oil-heated homes, you are not required to remove your existing heating system or related equipment. However, you must transition to an electric heat pump, and where required by law, work with a TSSA registered contractor to empty and/or remove your oil storage tank and associated piping.

### What if my home is primarily heated with natural gas but I am not an Enbridge Gas customer?

At this time, if your home is primarily heated by natural gas but you are not an Enbridge Gas customer, you are **not eligible**. Inquire with your local gas utility for available rebate programs.

### Am I eligible if I heat my home with a natural gas fireplace?

No, fireplaces are not considered a primary space heating type. To be eligible, your home must be primarily heated with **Enbridge natural gas** (e.g., furnace/boiler) **OR** be connected to the **Ontario electricity grid** and heat your home electrically.

### Does a home that is replacing a very old heat pump system qualify?

No. **Heat pump replacements are not eligible** for the program.

### What is the rebate amount for 1/2 ton sizes?

Incentives are calculated based on the **unrounded heating tonnage** of the heat pump, multiplied by the $/ton incentive amount.

*   For ccASHPs, incentives are based on the minimum rated heating capacity at 8.3C (47F). E.g., for a 3.2 ton ccASHP:
    *   **$1,600** for an Enbridge natural gas heated home ($500 x 3.2)
    *   **$4,000** for an oil, propane, wood or electrically heated home ($1250 x 3.2)
*   For GSHPs (full system):
    *   **$3,000** for an Enbridge natural gas heated home (flat rate)
    *   **$2,000 per ton** for an oil, propane, wood or electrically heated home (calculated per closed loop heating ton)

### How do I become a participating contractor?

**Contractor registrations are currently paused; no new applications are being accepted.** This allows the program to focus on providing comprehensive training and support to existing enrolled contractors to maintain high standards. Opportunities may arise in the future.

### Why was I selected for a Quality Assurance audit?

To maintain high standards, a portion of heat pump installations are **randomly chosen for Quality Assurance (QA) audits**. These audits ensure installations meet program requirements and support ongoing program improvements. Third-party evaluators (Ecolighten Energy Solutions and Bowser Technical) conduct these assessments.

## Solar Panels and Battery Storage

*Rooftop solar systems with or without battery*

### What are the eligibility requirements to receive solar PV and battery storage rebates?

To receive solar PV and battery storage rebates:

*   You must live in a **single/semi-detached home or row/townhome**.
*   You must **own the home** where the system will be installed and be responsible for its purchase and installation. (Where an owner has tenants, the owner must apply).
*   The home must be connected to **Ontarios electricity grid**. (Cornwall Electric customers are not eligible).
*   **All applicable permissions and permits** from local municipality, LDC, and regulatory bodies must be completed, including a Connection Assessment with the LDC and an Electrical Safety Authority (ESA) certificate.
*   Solar PV panels must be installed on a **rooftop**. Portable or temporary structures (including RVs and trailers) are not eligible.
*   Solar PV panels must be sized for **load displacement only**. Net metering is not permitted.
*   Battery storage systems must be paired with a **new rooftop solar PV panel system**.
*   All equipment must be **new, owned by the program participant, and purchased in Canada**.
*   All equipment must be compliant with applicable ANSI/CAN/UL/CSA/IEEE/CEC standards.

### Do I need a home energy assessment to be eligible for solar PV and battery storage rebates?

No. You **do not need a home energy assessment** for these upgrades.

### Can I receive a Home Renovation Savings program rebate for my solar PV project and participate in a net-metering agreement?

No. Participants receiving an incentive for solar PV and battery storage projects through this program are **not eligible to participate in a net-metering agreement** with their LDC. These incentives are for projects designed for load displacement only. You must choose either the program incentive or net-metering.

### Are program participants able to apply for net-metering in the future?

No. Participants who receive a Home Renovation Savings incentive for a solar PV system are required to operate the system for **load displacement purposes for the life of the system**. The Participant Agreement prevents entering into a net-metering or other compensation agreement for electricity injected into the grid.

### Why is net metering not permitted for solar PV projects that receive a rebate under the Home Renovation Savings program?

According to a directive issued to the IESO, the definition of electricity demand side management (eDSM) excludes measures promoted through other government or IESO programs. This means participants of the Home Renovation Savings program are not eligible for net metering agreements.

### Can I receive rebates for replacing or adding to an existing solar PV or battery storage system?

No. The rebate is intended to encourage **new solar PV and battery storage installations**.

### Are solar PV panel rebates allocated in $1,000 increments or a lower amount?

Solar PV panel rebates are calculated at **$1,000 per kilowatt of generation**. They will not be rounded to the nearest $1,000. For example, a 4.84 kW-DC system may receive a rebate up to **$4,840**.

### Why cant I install a standalone battery storage system?

A standalone battery storage system charges from the electricity grid. The program's goal is to reduce homeowners energy use, greenhouse gas emissions, and demand on the electricity grid. To achieve this, battery storage systems must be **paired with solar PV panels**.

### Are new builds eligible for solar PV and battery storage rebates?

Yes. New builds are eligible but must be **occupied** when submitting the pre-installation application.

### Can I apply more than once, for example, if I have more than one home or move homes? Does the program apply to non-primary homes (i.e., cottages)?

Yes. A homeowner can apply for the incentive more than once if they own multiple homes or move. **Both primary and non-primary homes are eligible**.

### Can I apply for a rebate if I currently have a microFIT project?

No, the rebate is meant to encourage new solar PV and battery storage installations. **Expansions to existing systems are not eligible**.

### Can I receive additional rebates from other programs for the installation of my solar PV and battery storage project?

A participant can stack incentives from other funding organizations, with some limitations. You **may not stack** with other incentives offered by Enbridge Gas, IESO's Save on Energy, or the Government of Ontario. Total incentives from all sources must **not exceed 100% of the costs** to purchase and install the solar PV and battery storage project.

### Can I apply for the Canada Greener Homes Loan and still be eligible for rebates through this program?

Yes. You may be eligible for federal loan funding and rebates together. The Canada Greener Homes Loan is a separate application. The last day to apply for the loan is **October 1, 2025**. If approved, you have **12 months** to complete retrofits and request funding. *Contact: Phone: 1-866-292-9517, Email: [Questions@cghli.ca](mailto:Questions@cghli.ca)*.

### Is third-party ownership of the solar PV panels and battery storage equipment permitted?

No. The program participant must be the individual responsible for paying for the purchase, installation, and **ownership** of the solar PV panels and battery storage system.

### Im interested in solar PV panels and battery storage for my off-grid home. Can I receive rebates?

No. Your home must be connected to the **Ontario electricity grid and distribution system** to be eligible.

### Why are some electricity customers not eligible for the Home Renovation Savings program?

To be eligible, electricity customers must be connected to **Ontarios electricity grid**. Customers of Cornwall Electric are connected to the Hydro-Qubec electricity grid and are therefore not eligible.

### How big can the solar PV array be?

For load displacement without net-metering, the array size should ideally match the homes typical energy consumption profile to maximize solar power use without frequent excess generation. Consult the relevant LDC for technical requirements.

### What is the typical size of a solar PV project applying for the rebate?

The typical size for load displacement ranges from **5 to 10 kW**, but varies by home load. When paired with storage, the system should primarily be for load displacement, not just backup power. Larger systems may require additional information during application review.

### Are there any restrictions on where the solar PV systems can be installed?

Solar PV equipment must be installed on a **rooftop of the Eligible Home** or on the rooftop of a permanent existing structure fixed to the property and connected to the hydro meter serving the Eligible Home (e.g., detached garages, outbuildings). Ground-mounted solar is not eligible.

### Why are ground-mounted solar PV systems excluded?

Ground-mounted systems involve additional permitting, environmental impacts, and design requirements. Limiting to rooftop solar helps address concerns of oversizing and reduces administrative burden.

### When should I install my solar PV and battery storage system?

Participants should **not purchase or install** the system before receiving **written pre-approval** from the Home Renovation Savings Program team and approval from the relevant LDC. If planning other upgrades (hot tub, heat pump, EV charge) that increase energy consumption, complete those first and then size solar panels accordingly.

### Can I work with any contractor to complete the installation of the solar PV and battery storage system?

Yes, you may work with the contractor of your choice. They must submit a [pre-installation form](https://www.homerenovationsavings.ca/without-assessment/solar/solar-contractor) on your behalf to verify eligibility. Once pre-approved, your contractor can proceed with installation and documentation submission.

### Can I work with more than one contractor (i.e., a contractor to install solar PV panels and another for battery storage)?

Yes, you may work with more than one contractor. Contact [solar@homerenovationsavings.ca](mailto:solar@homerenovationsavings.ca) for adjusted application process information.

### Where can I find a list of qualified solar PV and battery storage contractors?

The Home Renovation Savings Program does not provide a list of qualified contractors or endorse any. However, homeowners can refer to the [Canadian Renewable Energy Association (CanREA) member directory](https://renewablesassociation.ca/member-directory/) for a list of installers and service providers.

### What is the expected turnaround time for applications?

Applicants can expect to be contacted within **one week** of submitting their pre-installation application, with either approval or requests for clarification.

### Are program participants allowed to inject load back onto the grid and are participants required to install protections to ensure load is not being injected?

The IESO expects the LDC to manage technical connection requirements. Consult the relevant LDC for your systems technical requirements.

### Do Save on Energy or Enbridge Gas solicit participation for the program by phone or door to door?

No. Save on Energy or Enbridge Gas do not solicit residential customers over the phone or door to door. Refer to the [Scams page](https://www.enbridgegas.com/ontario/contact-us/scam-awareness) for more information. If you receive such calls/visits, exercise caution and contact [saveonenergy@ieso.ca](mailto:saveonenergy@ieso.ca).

## Smart Thermostats

*Get $100 off smart thermostats before or after you buy*

### What are the eligibility requirements to receive a smart thermostat rebate?

To receive a smart thermostat rebate, you must:

*   Be an **Enbridge Gas customer** with an active account and primarily heat your home with a natural gas furnace or boiler **OR** be connected to the **Ontario electricity grid** and heat or cool your home electrically. (Cornwall Electric customers are not eligible).
*   Have **not previously received a smart thermostat rebate, discount, or device** from Enbridge Gas, Save on Energy, or the Home Renovation Savings program.
*   Live in a **single/semi-detached home, row/townhome, or mobile home on a permanent foundation**.

### Whats the difference between the Before you buy rebate and the After you buy rebate?

*   **\"Before you buy\" rebate**: Provides **$100 off** the purchase of a qualifying smart thermostat at the time of checkout from participating retailers.
*   **\"After you buy\" rebate**: Provides a **$100 Enbridge Gas bill credit** (for Enbridge Gas customers) or a **rebate cheque** (for electricity customers). You must submit your receipt within **60 days** of purchasing the smart thermostat.

### I already purchased my smart thermostat. Can I still receive a $100 rebate?

Yes. If you purchased your smart thermostat **on or after Jan. 28, 2025**, you can apply for a rebate through the \"After you buy\" option within **60 days of purchase**.

### Where can I purchase my smart thermostat to receive a Before you buy rebate?

First, apply online to receive a discount code. Then, redeem the code online at participating retailers:

*   [ecobee.com](https://www.ecobee.com/en-ca/)
*   [emersoncanada.ca/store](https://www.emersoncanada.ca/store/sensi/)
*   [store.google.com](https://store.google.com/)
*   [bestbuy.ca](https://www.bestbuy.ca/en-ca)
*   **Home Depot**  *in-store purchase only*

### Where can I purchase my smart thermostat to receive an After you buy rebate?

To receive the \"After you buy\" rebate, you can purchase your smart thermostat from **any retailer** (online, in-store, or through a contractor) and upload your receipt showing proof of purchase.

### Can I receive a smart thermostat Before you buy rebate from Home Depots website?

No. The \"Before you buy\" rebate code cannot be redeemed on homedepot.ca. For instant savings at Home Depot, purchase your device in-store. If you prefer to purchase online at homedepot.ca, you can submit your receipt for an \"After you buy\" rebate.

### Can I get both a Before you buy and After you buy smart thermostat rebate?

No. You can only receive **one type** of smart thermostat rebate.

### Can I receive more than one smart thermostat rebate?

No. Only **one smart thermostat rebate is available per household per lifetime**.

### If I have an unredeemed Before you buy rebate code, can I receive an After you buy smart thermostat rebate?

An outstanding \"Before you buy\" rebate code would make you ineligible for the \"After you buy\" rebate. To switch, contact the program delivery agent, Summerhill (1-866-449-8510 or [SmartThermostats@homerenovationsavings.ca](mailto:SmartThermostats@homerenovationsavings.ca)) to cancel your code, then sign up for the \"After you buy\" offer.

### Can I buy a used or refurbished smart thermostat and receive a rebate?

No. **Used, refurbished, and open-box smart thermostats are not eligible**.

### When will I know if my After you buy smart thermostat rebate application was approved?

You should receive an email response within **two weeks** of applying.

### When will I receive my After you buy smart thermostat rebate?

Rebates are paid as a credit on your Enbridge Gas bill or by cheque. Payment will depend on processing time and your billing cycle. Allow up to **60 days** from application approval.

### Im an Enbridge Gas customer. Why are my account number and address not eligible?

You must have an active Enbridge Gas account and live in an eligible home type (single/semi-detached, row/townhouse, mobile home on permanent foundation). If new or recently moved, wait **2-3 weeks** for system updates. If you previously received a smart thermostat rebate at your current address, you are not eligible again.

### What happens if my After you buy smart thermostat rebate application is denied?

If denied, youll receive an email within two weeks. Denial reasons may include:

*   Device not purchased within **60 days** of application.
*   Device cost was **less than** the total rebate amount.
*   Device was **used, refurbished, or open-box**.
*   Device was **not a qualifying model**.
*   Receipt did not clearly show the thermostat cost as its own line item.

### I received a free smart thermostat as part of an HVAC package purchase. Am I eligible for a rebate?

No. **Free devices are not eligible**. Your receipt must clearly show the amount paid for the smart thermostat, and its cost must be more than the rebate amount.

### Will anyone but me control my smart thermostat if I participate in the program?

No. Enbridge Gas and Save on Energy **do not have access to your smart thermostat settings** and will not adjust your home's temperature. However, if you consent and enroll in Save on Energys [Peak Perks program](https://saveonenergy.ca/en/For-Your-Home/Peak-Perks) or other demand response programs, you allow administrators to control your smart thermostat during peak energy demand times (June-September) in exchange for a reward.

### Can I receive a smart thermostat rebate if I participate in this program and the Peak Perks program?

Yes. This program and Save on Energys [Peak Perks program](https://saveonenergy.ca/en/For-Your-Home/Peak-Perks) are designed to complement each other.

### Can I receive an After you buy rebate if I purchased my thermostat before Jan. 28, 2025?

The \"After you buy\" rebate is only available for purchases **on or after Jan. 28, 2025**, as this is the program launch date. You must apply within 60 days of purchase. Enbridge Gas customers referred from the Enbridge Gas smart thermostat program webpage who purchased before this date can use the Home Renovation Savings application portal.

### Why are some electricity customers not eligible for the Home Renovation Savings program?

To be eligible, electricity customers must be connected to **Ontarios electricity grid**. Customers of Cornwall Electric are connected to the Hydro-Qubec electricity grid and are therefore not eligible.
