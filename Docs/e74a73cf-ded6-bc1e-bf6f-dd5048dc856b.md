# Central Air Conditioners and Heat Pumps: Energy Efficiency Regulations Update

## Overview

This technical bulletin from Natural Resources Canada (NRCan) outlines proposed amendments to Canada's *Energy Efficiency Regulations* concerning single-phase central air conditioners and heat pumps. The goal is to align with updated standards in the United States while incorporating Canadian climate conditions.

**Publication Date**: May 2021

**Webinar**: Tuesday, May 18, 1:30-2:30 pm EST

**Comment Deadline**: June 7, 2021

**Regulatory Proposal Pre-publication**: Within approximately one year of this bulletin.

## Background

The Government of Canada is committed to enhancing energy efficiency standards and reducing regulatory burden by aligning with U.S., provincial, and territorial standards where appropriate. Existing regulations for central air conditioners and heat pumps apply to products manufactured on or after February 3, 1995.

Recent changes in 2016 and 2018 harmonized Canadian requirements with U.S. standards. The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) published a direct final rule on January 6, 2017, introducing new test standards and more stringent Minimum Energy Performance Standards (MEPS) for products manufactured on or after January 1, 2023.

NRCan is considering:
*   Aligning test standard requirements with those of the U.S. DOE.
*   Making the optional -15C (5F) test point in the U.S. DOE test procedure mandatory in Canadian regulations to better reflect Canadian operating conditions.
*   Applying minimum energy performance requirements closely aligned with U.S. DOE, with reporting incorporating the -15C (5F) test point for Climate Region V (instead of U.S. Climate Region IV).
*   Sunsetting the \"through-the-wall (TTW) air conditioner and heat pump\" product class, treating them as space-constrained products.

## Product Description

**Central air conditioners and heat pumps** are defined as air-source units with a rated capacity of less than 19 kW (65,000 Btu/h). This includes:
*   Single package systems
*   Split systems (single-split, multi-head mini-split, multi-split including variable refrigerant flow (VRF), multi-circuit systems)
*   Ducted systems
*   Ductless systems
*   Space-constrained systems
*   Small-duct, high-velocity air conditioners and heat pumps

## Energy Performance Testing Standard

NRCan is considering referencing **Appendix M1 to subpart B, Part 430 of Title 10** to the United States Code of Federal Regulations, entitled *Uniform Test Method for Measuring the Energy Consumption of Central Air Conditioners and Heat Pumps*.

**Adjustments for Canadian Climate**:
*   The optional -15C (5F) test point in the U.S. DOE test procedure would become mandatory.
*   The HSPF2 (*heating seasonal performance factor 2*) metric would be based on **Climate Region V** for calculation of the performance metric.

## Important Dates

*   **Coming into Force**: Six (6) months after the date of publication in the *Canada Gazette, Part II*.
*   **Applicability**: Regulatory requirements would apply to products manufactured after **January 1, 2023**.

## Labelling Requirements

*   No mandatory regulatory label requirements are proposed.
*   Voluntary labelling requirements will be determined through consultation with the industry.

## Verification Requirements

*   Products must carry a **third-party verification mark**.
*   The mark confirms the product meets seasonal cooling and heating MEPS (SEER2 and HSPF2), average off mode power consumption (P<sub>W,OFF</sub>) MEPS, and that reported information has been verified.
*   The verification mark must be from a Standards Council of Canada accredited certification body that operates an energy efficiency certification program for the product.

## Reporting Requirements

### Energy Efficiency Report

The report for central air conditioners and heat pumps would include the following product-specific information, obtained from the U.S. M1 test procedure with Canadian adjustments:
*   **SEER2** (*seasonal energy efficiency ratio 2*)
*   **Rated cooling capacity**
*   **HSPF2** for Region V and **rated heating capacity @-8.3C (47F)** for heat pumps
*   **P<sub>W,OFF</sub>** (average off mode power consumption)

**Additional information for heat pumps**:
*   **COP @-15C (5F) at rated capacity**
*   **Rated capacity @-15C (5F)**

This report must be submitted by the dealer to NRCan **before** the product is first imported into Canada or traded inter-provincially.

### Import Report

No changes are considered for import reporting.
