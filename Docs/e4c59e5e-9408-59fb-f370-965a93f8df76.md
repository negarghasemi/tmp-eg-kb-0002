# Pool heaters

## Technical bulletin on developing the standards
*   **Webinar**: Thursday, May 30, 2024, 1:00-2:15 pm EDT
*   Natural Resources Canada (NRCan) intends to amend Canadas *Energy Efficiency Regulations* to introduce **pool heaters** as a new energy-using product.
*   This includes energy efficiency and testing standards requirements to harmonize with the United States.
*   Input received will inform the development of amendments and a cost-benefit analysis.
*   The Government of Canada intends to pre-publish a regulatory proposal in the *Canada Gazette*, Part I, in **2025**.

## Background
*   Pool heaters are **not currently defined** as an energy-using product in the Regulations.
*   Energy efficiency standards for pool heaters could:
    *   Save energy
    *   Reduce energy costs for Canadians
    *   Reduce energy-related greenhouse gas emissions
    *   Encourage manufacturers to adopt efficient product designs
*   **Harmonization**: NRCan is considering amending the Regulations to align with the scope, energy efficiency standards, testing standards, and manufacturing dates of the U.S. Department of Energy (U.S. DOE) rule.
    *   U.S. DOE published a final rule on **May 30, 2023**, introducing energy efficiency standards for electric pool heaters and increasing stringency for gas-fired pool heaters.
    *   U.S. DOE published a final rule on **January 6, 2015**, for a testing standard to evaluate the energy performance of pool heaters.

## Product description
*   **Definition**: A **pool heater** is an appliance designed for heating the water contained in a swimming pool.

### Testing standard
*   NRCan is considering referencing: **Appendix P to subpart B, Part 430 of Title 10** to the U.S. Code of Federal Regulations, entitled *Uniform Test Method for Measuring the Energy Consumption of Pool Heaters*.

### Energy efficiency standard
*   **Gas-fired pool heaters**: The minimum integrated thermal efficiency, in percent, is **84(QIN+491)/(QIN+2536)**.
    *   *Where QIN is the certified input capacity of a gas-fired pool heater basic model, in Btu/h*.
*   **Electric pool heaters**: The minimum integrated thermal efficiency, in percent, is **600PE/(PE+1619)**.
    *   *Where PE is the certified active electrical power of an electric pool heater, in Btu/h*.

## Important dates
*   **Effective date**: Modifications would come into force **six months** after the date of publication in the *Canada Gazette*, Part II.
*   **Manufacturing date**: New standards apply to pool heaters manufactured on or after **May 30, 2028**.
*   **Regulatory pathway**: Governor in Council amendment, with pre-publication in the *Canada Gazette*, Part I, followed by a public comment period.

## Labelling requirements
*   NRCan is **not considering** labelling requirements for pool heaters.

## Verification requirements
*   Pool heaters would require a **third-party verification mark**.
*   This mark indicates that the product meets energy efficiency standards and that reported information has been verified.
*   The verification mark must be from a Standards Council of Canada accredited certification body that operates an energy efficiency certification program for the product.

## Reporting requirements
### Energy efficiency report
*   The energy efficiency report for pool heaters must include:
    *   **Name of product**: pool heater
    *   **Brand name**
    *   **Model number**
    *   **Name of the manufacturer**
    *   **Name of the certification body** whose verification mark would be affixed to the product
    *   **Whether a mathematical model was used** to generate any of the product specific information
    *   **Type of pool heater**: natural gas fired, propane fired, electric resistance, or heat pump
    *   **Heating capacity**: in kW (btu/h)
    *   **Integrated Thermal Efficiency**: in percent
*   This report must be submitted by the dealer to NRCan **before** the product is imported into Canada or traded inter-provincially for the first time.

### Import report
*   A dealer importing these products into Canada must include the following information on the customs release document:
    *   **Name of product**
    *   **Model number**
    *   **Brand name**
    *   **Address of the dealer** importing the product
    *   **Purpose for which the product is being imported** (e.g., for sale/lease without modification, for sale/lease after modification, or for use as a component in an exported product)
