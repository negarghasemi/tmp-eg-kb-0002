# Technical Assistance for the Canada Greener Homes Initiative

**Having issues with your application? Try these solutions!**

This document provides troubleshooting assistance for issues encountered with the Canada Greener Homes portal.

## I need help applying

### How do I apply for the **Oil to Heat Pump Affordability program**?

*   **Manitoba applicants**: Refer to the [Manitoba page](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/canada-greener-homes-initiative/applicants-manitoba) for information specific to your province.
*   **For Alberta, Northwest Territories, Quebec, or Saskatchewan**: Register via the [Log in to the Canada Greener Homes portal](https://greenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/idp/profile/nrcan/idpdisco?entityID=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca&return=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca%2Fidp%2Fprofile%2FSAML2%2FRedirect%2FSSO%3Fexecution%3De1s1%26_eventId_proceed%3D1) page using a Sign-In Partner or GCKey.
*   **Residents of the following provinces/territories need to apply through their provincial or territorial program**:
    *   [British Columbia](https://www.betterhomesbc.ca/energy-savings-program/)
    *   [New Brunswick](https://www.saveenergynb.ca/en/for-home/oil-to-heat-pump-affordability/)
    *   [Newfoundland and Labrador](https://takechargenl.ca/oiltoelectric)
    *   [Nova Scotia](https://www.efficiencyns.ca/programs-rebates/oil-to-heat-pump-affordability-program)
    *   [Ontario](https://saveonenergy.ca/en/For-Your-Home/Energy-Affordability-Program)
    *   [Prince Edward Island](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/en/service/free-heat-pump-program)
    *   [Yukon](https://yukon.ca/en/heating-system-rebate#affordable-heat-pump-program-)

*You will know you have successfully created an application when you have received your application number (e.g., **AP123456** or **OHPA123456**).* 

### What is the difference between having an **account** in the Canada Greener Homes portal and having an **application** live in the system?

*   Having an **account** means you have registered.
*   Having an **application** means you have applied and have an application number (e.g., **AP123456** or **OHPA123456**).
    1.  **Step one**: Create an account.
    2.  **Step two**: Create an application and receive your application number.

### How do I know if I have an application? What is an application number?

You will receive an automatic email notification confirming your application number (e.g., **AP123456** or **OHPA123456**).

**If you do not have an application number, you have two options:**

*   **Option 1: Complete your application online.**
    1.  Access the [Canada Greener Homes portal](https://greenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/idp/profile/nrcan/idpdisco?entityID=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca&return=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca%2Fidp%2Fprofile%2FSAML2%2FRedirect%2FSSO%3Fexecution%3De2s1%26_eventId_proceed%3D1) by logging in.
    2.  Click **New application** and follow the prompts.
    3.  **Ensure you upload your proof of primary residence document** (valid, government-issued ID with your name and address matching the application).
    *Helpful hint: When verifying your email address, if clicking the link doesn't work, copy and paste it into a new Google Chrome window.*

*   **Option 2: Request an offline homeowner kit.**
    1.  Call the call centre at **1-833-674-8282**.
    2.  The kit and application form will be mailed to you.
    *Note: This process is longer as it requires mailing documents to NRCan. **Canada Greener Homes Grant offline applications must be signed, dated and mailed on or before February 12, 2024. Eligible offline applications received no later than March 11, 2024, will be processed.***

### Do you have helpful hints (i.e., user names and passwords, when applying, browsers)?

**User names and Passwords:**

*   **GCKey**: Take note of your username and password. Use memorable recovery questions. The GCKey for the Canada Greener Homes portal is different from the Canada Revenue Agency's.
*   **Sign-In Partner**: Note which bank you used if you have multiple accounts.
*   Keep a copy of this information in a safe place.

**Email:**

*   **Verification email**: Open the email and click the link to verify your email address. If redirected to the login page, select your GCKey or Sign-In Partner and log in.
*   **Junk mail folder**: Check your spam/junk folder if you don't receive emails. Add the email to trusted senders or mark as 'not junk' for future communications.

**Browsers:**

*   Older versions of Microsoft Edge may cause issues. Try **Google Chrome** or ensure Microsoft Edge is updated to the latest version.

### What are the GC Key and sign-in partner? Why do I have to use one of these when I apply?

Both tools are used to safeguard your information during online transactions with the Government of Canada.

*   **GCKey**: A unique credential provided by the Government of Canada for secure online business with various governmental programs. 
    *   [GCKey - FAQ](https://clegc-gckey.gc.ca/j/eng/FQ-02?ReqID=S2E96744F68C76A417B9D91FA9AD7C291E88FF8AC1)
    *   **1-855-438-1102** (TTY **1-855-438-1103**)
*   **Sign-in partner**: Allows you to sign in using your existing online Canadian banking information. Always use the same Sign-In Partner you originally selected.

*The Government protects personal information under the federal Privacy Act and ensures high cybersecurity standards for the Canada Greener Homes portal.*

### When applying for the **Oil to Heat Pump Affordability program**, why am I directed to the **Canada Greener Homes Initiative**? Am I supposed to apply for both?

The **Oil to Heat Pump Affordability program** is an **additional stream** of the broader **Canada Greener Homes Initiative (CGHI)**. Applicants use the Canada Greener Homes portal for both. You choose the appropriate program based on eligibility criteria when you arrive at the portal page.

## I am having trouble logging in

### What do I do if I forgot my Sign-In Partner username or password?

Visit your Sign-In Partners website to follow their password and username recovery procedures.
*NOTE: Always use the same username or card number your bank accepts as your login credential when accessing the Canada Greener Homes portal.*

### What do I do if I forgot my GCKey password?

1.  Visit the [sign-in page for GCKey](https://onlineservices-servicesenligne-cic.fjgc-gccf.gc.ca/mycic/gccf?lang=eng&idp=gckey&svc=/mycic/start).
2.  Click Forgot your password?
3.  Answer your password recovery questions to create a new password.
*NOTE: Continue to use the same GCKey username you originally selected to verify your email with the system.*

### What do I do if I forgot my GCKey username? How do I create a new GCKey?

To regain access, you will need to create a **new GCKey** using a **different email address** from the one previously registered.

1.  **Step 1: Register for a new GCKey.**
    *   Access the [Canada Greener Homes portal](https://greenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/idp/profile/nrcan/idpdisco?entityID=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca&return=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca%2Fidp%2Fprofile%2FSAML2%2FRedirect%2FSSO%3Fexecution%3De1s1%26_eventId_proceed%3D1).
    *   Select **Option 2: GCKey**.
    *   Click **Sign Up**.
2.  **Step 2: Create new credentials.** Choose a new username and password, and create new security questions. Keep a copy.
3.  **Step 3: Sign in to the portal with the new GCKey.** Access the [Canada Greener Homes portal](https://greenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/idp/profile/nrcan/idpdisco?entityID=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca&return=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca%2Fidp%2Fprofile%2FSAML2%2FRedirect%2FSSO%3Fexecution%3De1s1%26_eventId_proceed%3D1) and use your new GCKey.
4.  **Step 4: Re-enter personal information and verify a new email.** You will be redirected to the registration page. Enter the same personal information as your original GCKey, and verify a **new valid email address**.
5.  **Step 5: Register and verify email.** Click **Register**. Check your inbox for a validation email and click the link.
    *NOTE: If the link does not work, copy and paste it into a new Google Chrome window.*
6.  **Step 6: Continue and close browser.** Click **Continue** in the browser window that opens, then close the browser once the new page reloads.
7.  **Step 7: Email the program officers.** Complete the following table and email it to the relevant address:

| | **OLD** | | **NEW** | |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **AP# (example: AP123456)** | **Name** | **E-mail address** | **Name** | **E-mail address** |
| **OHPA# (example: OHPA123456)** | | | | |

    *   **Canada Greener Homes Grant email**: [canadagreenerhomesgrant-subventionmaisonsvertes@nrcan-rncan.gc.ca](mailto:canadagreenerhomesgrant-subventionmaisonsvertes@nrcan-rncan.gc.ca)
    *   **Oil to Heat Pump Affordability program email**: [OHPA-CAMT@nrcan-rncan.gc.ca](mailto:OHPA-CAMT@nrcan-rncan.gc.ca)

*A Program Officer will link your existing application to your new GCKey username. Do not attempt to log in until you receive confirmation.*

### What do I do if I get this message: **Always use the same log-in option and credentials to access the Canada Greener Homes Grant Initiative. If you use different credentials, you will be treated as a different user?**

This message indicates you are using a new GCKey or Sign-In Partner login different from your initial registration. Refer to [What do I do if I see a registration page after I log in](#wb-auto-17) and follow the steps.

### What do I do if I get this message: **Please provide a unique identifier?**

This error occurs if you created a GCKey login in the Service Organization portal.

*   **If you are a new applicant**:
    1.  Create a new GCKey via the [Log in to the Canada Greener Homes portal](https://greenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/idp/profile/nrcan/idpdisco?entityID=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca&return=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca%2Fidp%2Fprofile%2FSAML2%2FRedirect%2FSSO%3Fexecution%3De1s1%26_eventId_proceed%3D1) using Google Chrome (avoid saved favorites).
    2.  Clear any cookies or history.
    3.  Refer to [What do I do if I forgot my GCKey username? How do I create a new GCKey?](#login3) and complete all steps.
*   **If you already have an application but forgot your login information**:
    1.  Refer to [What do I do if I forgot my GCKey username? How do I create a new GCKey?](#login3) and complete all steps.

### What do I do if I get this message: **Your GCKey has been revoked?**

Your GCKey may be revoked due to:

*   **Expiration**: Not used at least once every two years.
*   **Compromise**: Username and password may have been compromised (avoid using the same credentials for different online accounts).

To resolve, you need to create a new GCKey for the Canada Greener Homes portal. See [What do I do if I forgot my GCKey username? How do I create a new GCKey?](#login3) and follow all steps.

## I want to edit my profile or application

### How do I change my Service Organization?

1.  Log in to the Canada Greener Homes [Portal](https://greenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/idp/profile/nrcan/idpdisco?entityID=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca&return=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca%2Fidp%2Fprofile%2FSAML2%2FRedirect%2FSSO%3Fexecution%3De2s1%26_eventId_proceed%3D1).
2.  Click on **My applications**.
3.  Click the **application number (AP#)**.
4.  Click on **Manage Application**.
5.  Select **Change Service Organization for this Application**.

### I have multiple applications. How do I delete the extra applications?

1.  Log in to the Canada Greener Homes [Portal](https://greenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/idp/profile/nrcan/idpdisco?entityID=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca&return=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca%2Fidp%2Fprofile%2FSAML2%2FRedirect%2FSSO%3Fexecution%3De2s1%26_eventId_proceed%3D1).
2.  Click on **My applications**.
3.  Click the **application number (AP#)**.
4.  Click on **Manage Application**.
5.  Select **\"Opt-out\"**.
6.  Select **I am cancelling this Application and will return or already have another Application**.

### I no longer want to participate in the Canada Greener Homes Grant Initiative. What do I do?

Log in to the Canada Greener Homes [portal](https://greenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/idp/profile/nrcan/idpdisco?entityID=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca&return=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca%2Fidp%2Fprofile%2FSAML2%2FRedirect%2FSSO%3Fexecution%3De2s1%26_eventId_proceed%3D1).

### What do I do if I do not see a link to select or selecting the link does not work?

Your email provider or browser anti-virus may be blocking links in emails. Disable any pop-up blockers before selecting the link.

### I accidentally clicked Already Evaluated and I have not had my pre-retrofit evaluation by my energy advisor yet. How do I make the change?

1.  Log in to the [Canada Greener Homes portal](https://greenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/idp/profile/nrcan/idpdisco?entityID=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca&return=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca%2Fidp%2Fprofile%2FSAML2%2FRedirect%2FSSO%3Fexecution%3De1s1%26_eventId_proceed%3D1).

4.  Click on **Edit** (top right corner).
5.  **Uncheck** the Already Evaluated box.

### I have had my pre-retrofit evaluation by my energy advisor and want to ensure it is updated. How do I change that?

1.  Log in to the [Canada Greener Homes portal](https://greenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/idp/profile/nrcan/idpdisco?entityID=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca&return=https%3A%2F%2Fgreenerhomes-maisonecologiques.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca%2Fidp%2Fprofile%2FSAML2%2FRedirect%2FSSO%3Fexecution%3De1s1%26_eventId_proceed%3D1).

3.  From the drop-down menu, click on **My Account**.
4.  Click on **Edit** (top right corner).
5.  **Check** the Already Evaluated box.
6.  **Upload** your (1) Renovation Upgrade Report and (2) EnerGuide label (see [How do I upload documents?](#wb-auto-23)).
