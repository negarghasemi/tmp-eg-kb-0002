# Clothes Dryers: Energy Efficiency Guide

An ENERGY STAR certified clothes dryer **uses 20% less energy**, on average, than a standard model. You can save even more by pairing it with an [ENERGY STAR certified clothes washer](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/energy-star/products/list-certified-products/clothes-washers). Saving energy helps save money and reduces your carbon footprint.

## Benefits of ENERGY STAR Certified Clothes Dryers

ENERGY STAR certified clothes dryers offer several advantages:

*   **Sensor Drying**: Shuts the dryer off as soon as clothes are dry, preventing over-drying and wear and tear on your clothes.
*   **Extra-Low Heat Setting**: Allows clothes to dry longer at a low temperature, which saves energy.
*   **Heat Pump Dryers**: Consume even less energy (saving 20-60% more than standard models) by using and recirculating ambient heat to dry clothes while removing moisture. These are particularly beneficial for consumers with high clothes dryer usage and high electricity rates. All heat pump dryers meet the criteria for ENERGY STAR Most Efficient.

## The ENERGY STAR Promise

All ENERGY STAR certified products are tested to meet strict [efficiency specifications](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/energy-star/technical-specifications) and are certified by an independent third party. They perform as well as, or better than, standard products without compromising performance.

Start your research using the [ENERGY STAR Product Finder tool to find and compare](https://www.energystar.gov/productfinder/product/certified-clothes-dryers/results?SetLanguage=English&NRCAN=on) certified clothes dryers. *(You will be redirected to the U.S. ENERGY STAR website.)*

## Tips for Choosing an Energy Efficient Clothes Dryer

*   When choosing between similar models, consult the [EnerGuide label](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/product-energy-ratings/energuide/appliances-energuide) to compare energy consumption. **The lower the number, the better!**
*   Clothes dryers with the [ENERGY STAR Most Efficient](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/energy-star/products/energy-star-most-efficient) designation are top-tier performers, offering even greater energy savings than standard models.

## How to Use Your Clothes Dryer Efficiently

*   **Clean the lint trap** after every load to increase airflow.
*   **Clean out the dryer vents regularly** to increase airflow and reduce strain on the motor.
*   **Avoid overfilling** to ensure proper airflow and reduce strain on the motor.
*   **Scrub the lint filter regularly** if you use dryer sheets. Dryer sheets can leave a film on the filter that reduces airflow and affects motor performance. Use a toothbrush to scrub it clean once a month.
*   **Sort items by thickness**: Dry light, quick-drying items in one load and thick items like towels or jeans in another.
*   **Avoid over-drying your clothes**: Use the sensor setting or, if using the timed drying function, experiment to minimize the drying time needed.

## Regulations Set the Energy Efficiency Minimum

Clothes dryers are subject to Canada's *Energy Efficiency Regulations*, which establish a minimum performance standard for their energy efficiency. Find more details in the [Guide to the Regulations](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-regulations/guide-canada-s-energy-efficiency-regulations).
