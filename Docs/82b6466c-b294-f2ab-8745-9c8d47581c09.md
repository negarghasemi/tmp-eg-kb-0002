# Step 4. Post-retrofit EnerGuide home evaluation

## Program Status

**Closed: Canada Greener Homes Grant.** December 31, 2025, was the last day to submit your documents. Requests submitted by the deadline will continue to be processed.

*Canada Greener Homes Grant applicants had to complete the entire retrofit journey to be eligible for funding.*

## Scheduling a Post-Retrofit EnerGuide Evaluation

After retrofits were completed, homeowners had to contact their service organization or energy advisor to schedule a post-retrofit EnerGuide evaluation.

This evaluation shows: 
- How much **energy has been saved**
- How much **greenhouse gas emissions are reduced** because of the retrofits

**Recommendations:**
- It was recommended to use the same service organization as for the pre-retrofit evaluation, if possible.
- If the original service provider was unavailable, switching to a different NRCan-registered service provider was permitted.
- Switching service organizations could cause delays.
- Homeowners were responsible for ensuring all required steps were completed by the deadline.
- If switching, the application in the Greener Homes portal had to be updated with the new service organization's information.

## Finding a Service Organization or Energy Advisor

**Service organizations** are companies registered with Natural Resources Canada (NRCan) that deliver EnerGuide evaluations and work with registered energy advisors.

*You can find registered service organizations or energy advisors in your area via the [find service organizations for existing homes](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/find-service-organizations-existing-homes) link.*

## Costs of Your Home Evaluations

- The homeowner was **responsible to pay up front** for the post-retrofit EnerGuide evaluation.
- The cost of the post-retrofit evaluation might differ from the pre-retrofit evaluation.
- **Up to $600** was the **maximum contribution** for the combined costs of both evaluations.

### Reminders

- You were required to pay up front for the evaluations.
- The maximum grant amount for the two evaluations might not cover the full cost.
- You would not receive the pre- or post-retrofit evaluation contribution unless you completed the full Canada Greener Homes Grant process, including installing at least one eligible retrofit.

## Canada Greener Homes Initiative

### Related Programs

- **Oil to Heat Pump Affordability program:** Helps low- to median-income homeowners switch from oil heating to an eligible electric heat pump system. *Details vary by province and territory.*
- **Canada Greener Homes Affordability Program:** Helps low- to median-income homeowners and tenants reduce their energy bills and greenhouse gas emissions through no-cost home retrofits, delivered through provincial and territorial partners, with additional support for Indigenous organizations.
