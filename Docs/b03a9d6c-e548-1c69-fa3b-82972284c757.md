# Energy Journey for Homeowners

This report outlines various tips and recommendations for homeowners to improve energy efficiency and save on energy costs.

## Lighting Efficiency

### Choosing the Right Light Bulb
When shopping for an **LED bulb**, look at the bulbs **lumens per watt**. Lumens measure a lightbulbs efficiency and brightness. The higher the lumen, the more efficient and brighter the bulb.

*   **Learn more**: [LED lighting buying guide](/For-Your-Home/Advice-and-Tips/Lighting-guide)

### Savings from LED Lighting
By switching your bulbs to LEDs, you could save enough for **40 cups of coffee** in just one year. _Even small changes add up to noticeable energy savings._

## Home Humidity Management

### Ideal Home Humidity Levels
Maintaining appropriate humidity levels is crucial for home comfort and energy efficiency.

*   **Winter**: Typically **30%  40%** humidity to keep your home warm and comfortable without condensation on windows.
*   **Summer**: Can be higher, between **50%  60%**.

*   **Learn more**: [Humidity at home](/For-Your-Home/Advice-and-Tips/Home-humidity-levels)

## Sealing and Insulation

### Weather Stripping Benefits
Using **weather stripping** on windows and doors helps seal cracks that let cold drafts in. An airtight home reduces energy costs and improves comfort.

## Indoor Air Quality and Energy Savings

### Reducing Allergies and Saving Energy
By taking steps to reduce allergy triggers in your home, you can also save on your energy bill.

*   **Find out how**: [Reducing Allergies](/For-Your-Home/Advice-and-Tips/Reducing-Allergies)

## Appliance Energy Consumption

### Gaming System Energy Use
Gaming systems can consume significant energy even when not in active use. Most gaming systems have a **power-saving mode** that can help cut down on energy use.

*   **Learn how to enable power-saving**: [Configure game consoles for less energy use](https://www.energystar.gov/products/configuring_todays_game_consoles_use_less_energy_0)

### Shopping for Efficient Appliances
When buying new appliances, look for the blue **ENERGY STAR label**. ENERGY STAR-certified appliances are proven to be more efficient, leading to lasting energy savings.

*   **Learn more**: [Natural Resources Canada - Energy Efficiency](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency)

## Seasonal Temperature Control

### Ceiling Fan Direction
Adjusting the direction of your ceiling fan blades seasonally can help control your home's temperature, saving on both cooling and heating costs.
