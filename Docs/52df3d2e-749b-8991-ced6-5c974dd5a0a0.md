# Canada Greener Homes Grant Eligibility

This document summarizes the eligibility criteria and key information for the Canada Greener Homes Grant, focusing on energy efficiency upgrades for residential properties.

## General Ineligibility Conditions

**You are ineligible for this grant if any of the following conditions apply:**

-   You are a landlord and rent your property.
-   You are a renter.
-   You already started or completed your retrofits without first receiving an EnerGuide home evaluation.
-   Your home was built less than 6 months ago (you can apply once your home is at least 6 months old).
-   You have not moved into the home yet because you are renovating it before making it your primary residence.

## Eligibility Overview for Homeowners

To be eligible, you must be a **Canadian homeowner with proof that your home is your primary residence** and your property must be one of the eligible types.

### Eligible Property Types

-   Single or semi-detached house
-   Mobile home on a permanent foundation
-   Permanently moored floating home
-   Row house
-   Townhome
-   Mixed-use building (residential portion only)
-   Home or building with a secondary suite or apartment
-   Multi-unit residential building (MURB) with specific criteria (three storeys or less with a footprint of 600 m or less)

### Multi-Unit Residential Building (MURB) Additional Criteria

For MURBs, the following criteria must be met:

-   The entire building, not just individual units, will receive an EnerGuide evaluation.
-   The decision-makers of the building will approve eligible and recommended retrofits.
-   At least 50% of the building is residential living space and people live in it year-round.
-   The building will meet the conditions to receive a blower door test.

### Property Ownership Type

-   For row houses, townhomes, or mixed-use buildings, the property must be **freehold** (complete use, control, and ownership of the building and land). If not freehold, approval from your condo board may be required for exterior retrofits.

## EnerGuide Evaluation Requirements

-   **Pre-retrofit EnerGuide evaluation is mandatory before starting retrofits.**
-   **If you already have an evaluation:**
    -   **Before April 1, 2020:** A new evaluation is required.
    -   **Between April 1, 2020, and November 30, 2020:** Can be used, but no grant for the evaluation itself. Upload EnerGuide Label and Renovation Upgrade Report with your application.
    -   **Between December 1, 2020, and June 30, 2021:** Can receive a grant for both evaluations and retrofits if at least one eligible retrofit is completed on or after December 1, 2020. The retrofit does not need to be recommended by your energy advisor.
    -   **On or after July 1, 2021:** Can receive a grant for both evaluations and retrofits, but only if both evaluations and at least one recommended retrofit are completed. Upload EnerGuide Label and Renovation Upgrade Report with your application.

## Grant Details

-   **EnerGuide Evaluations:** Up to $600 for two EnerGuide evaluations (pre- and post-retrofit).
-   **Retrofits:** Up to $5,000 for eligible retrofits.
-   **Thermostats:** Up to $50 for thermostats *only if* combined with at least one other eligible retrofit from your pre-retrofit EnerGuide evaluation.

**Important:** You will receive a grant only if you complete both the evaluations and at least one recommended retrofit.

## Eligible Retrofits (Examples)

-   Home insulation
-   Air-sealing
-   Windows and doors
-   Space and water heating
-   Renewable energy (e.g., solar panels)
-   Resiliency measures (e.g., moisture-proofing or roofing membrane)

## Ineligible Retrofits/Upgrades

-   Retrofits done before a pre-retrofit evaluation.
-   Retrofits done before December 1, 2020.
-   Eligible products installed in new home additions built between the pre- and post-retrofit EnerGuide evaluations.
-   Leased or rented equipment.
-   Air conditioners.
-   Tankless or storage water heaters other than heat pump water heaters.
-   Skylights.
-   Furnaces (unless in a northern or off-grid community).
-   Boilers (unless in a northern or off-grid community).
-   Roofs.

## Application Process and Considerations

-   **Timeline:** The process involves several steps (application review, eligibility confirmation, evaluations, payment processing) and can take time due to high interest.
-   **Before you apply:**
    -   Understand the grant process.
    -   Have a valid government-issued ID with your primary address.
    -   Have your tax roll number (from a property tax bill).
    -   Research service organizations in your area for pre-retrofit EnerGuide evaluations.
-   **Provincial Conditions:** Specific conditions apply to residents of Nova Scotia, Ontario, Quebec, New Brunswick, Rossland (BC), and Nelson and Regional District of Central Kootenay (BC). Residents in these areas may need to go through provincial/municipal programs first.
-   **Canada Greener Homes Loan:** This loan can provide up to $40,000 interest-free for up to 10 years for more expensive retrofits. You must be approved for the grant before applying for the loan.

## Indigenous Governments or Organizations

-   Eligible to register multiple homes.
-   Must own the home(s) or have a formal partnership to represent owners.
-   Homes do not need to be the owners primary residence but must be occupied by an Indigenous household.
-   Off-grid communities can be identified using the Remote Energy Communities Database.
-   **To apply:** Call 1-833-674-8282 (Mon-Fri, 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. ET).
-   **Indigenous homeowners (off-reserve and/or own home):** Apply online via the homeowner portal. Use your Certificate of Possession or Occupation number if no property tax bill number is available.
