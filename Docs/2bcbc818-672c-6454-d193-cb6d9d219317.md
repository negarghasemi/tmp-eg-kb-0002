# Electric Heaters: Energy Efficiency Overview

## General Information

Electric space heaters, including electric furnaces, are widely used in **Canadian homes and businesses** as either a primary or supplementary heat source. They come in various technologies and designs:

*   **Technologies**: Fan-forced, convection, and radiant.
*   **Portability**: Available as portable units or installed systems.
*   **Variety**: Range from small portable plug-ins to radiator-like units, decorative fireplaces, and powerful commercial air-curtain heaters.

## Energy Efficiency Considerations

*   **Temperature Control**: **Thermostats** can be used to control temperature and manage energy consumption.
*   **End-Use Efficiency**: Electric space heaters are technically **100% energy efficient** at the point of use, meaning they produce one unit of heat for every unit of electricity consumed.
*   **Comparative Efficiency**: Despite 100% end-use efficiency, they are not always the most efficient heating choice overall. For instance, **geothermal heat pumps** can achieve **300% energy efficiency** by delivering three units of heat for every unit of electricity used.

## Regulatory Status and Certification

*   **Energy Efficiency Regulations**: Electric space heaters are **not currently covered** by Canadas *Energy Efficiency Regulations*, with the sole exception of a technology requirement for electric boilers.
*   **ENERGY STAR Qualification**: There are **no ENERGY STAR qualified** high-efficiency models available for electric space heaters.
