# Pool pump motors

**Publication Date**: May 2024

## Technical Bulletin on Developing the Standards

*Webinar*: Thursday, May 30, 2024, 1:00-2:15 pm EDT

Natural Resources Canada (NRCan) updated its [Forward Regulatory Plan](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/node/21709#msdynttrid=gPrvBKD4QnHLNydHNDucp-nQokQOZTaBRjIp6V7eiWk) in April 2024, indicating an intention to amend Canadas *Energy Efficiency Regulations* to introduce **pool pump motors** as a new energy-using product. This includes energy efficiency and testing standards requirements to harmonize with the United States.

This bulletin initiates the consultation process to gather stakeholder views. Input will inform the development of future amendments. NRCan will conduct a cost-benefit analysis using Canadian market data to assess economic and environmental impacts.

**Regulatory Proposal**: The Government of Canada intends to pre-publish a regulatory proposal in the *Canada Gazette*, Part I, in 2025.

## Background

Pool pump motors are **not currently defined** as an energy-using product in the Regulations.

These motors consume a significant portion of residential building energy. Efficiency standards could lead to:
- Energy savings
- Reduced energy costs for Canadians
- Decreased energy-related greenhouse gas emissions
- Encouragement for manufacturers to adopt efficient product designs

**Harmonization with U.S. DOE**:
- In 2021, NRCan and the U.S. Department of Energy (U.S. DOE) signed a [Memorandum of Understanding](https://nrcan.canada.ca/energy/resources/international-energy-cooperation/memorandum-understanding/23749) for cooperation on energy efficiency standards.
- **July 29, 2021**: U.S. DOE published a [final rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2021/07/29/2021-15759/energy-conservation-program-test-procedure-for-dedicated-purpose-pool-pump-motors) for a test method for pool pump motors.
- **September 28, 2023**: U.S. DOE published a [final rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/09/28/2023-20343/energy-conservation-program-energy-conservation-standards-for-dedicated-purpose-pool-pump-motors) introducing energy efficiency standards for electric pool pump motors, including prescriptive and performance requirements. Manufacturers must comply starting in **2025 or 2027** based on motor size.
- NRCan is considering amending its Regulations to harmonize with these U.S. DOE energy efficiency standards, testing standards, and manufacturing dates.

## Product Description

A **pool pump motor** is an appliance specifically designed for driving a swimming pool pump. This includes:
- Self-priming pool filter pumps
- Non-self-priming pool filter pumps
- Waterfall pumps
- Pressure cleaner booster pumps
- Integral sand-filter pool pumps
- Integral-cartridge filter pool pumps
- Storable electric spa pumps
- Rigid electric spa pumps

A **variable speed motor** is defined as a pool pump motor that meets the definition of variable-speed control dedicated purpose pool pump motor as defined by UL 1004-10:2022.

### Testing Standard
NRCan is considering incorporating by reference one of the following testing standards:
- [**CSA C747-09**](https://www.csagroup.org/store/product/2420227/) *Energy efficiency test methods for small motors* (current U.S. Code of Federal Regulations standard).
- [**CSA C747-22**](https://www.csagroup.org/store/product/2430245/) *Energy efficiency test methods for small motors*, if incorporated into the U.S. Code of Federal Regulations before the next amendment to the Regulations.

*CSA testing standards can be ordered from the [CSA website](https://www.csagroup.org/). Many are available at no cost after creating an account on the [CSA Communities of Interest portal](https://community.csagroup.org/community/energy-efficiency/standards-access-portal).*

### Energy Efficiency Standard
NRCan intends to align with the U.S. DOE and incorporate by reference the energy efficiency standards of [section 431.485 of Subpart Z to Part 431, Title 10](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-431.485) of the U.S. Code of Federal Regulations:
- A pool pump motor with a **total horsepower less than 0.5 hp (0.373 kW)** must have a **full-load efficiency of not less than 69 percent**.
- A pool pump motor with a **total horsepower greater than or equal to 0.5 hp (0.373 kW)** must be a **variable speed control motor**.

## Important Dates

**Effective Date**: Modifications to the Regulations would come into force **six months** after publication in the *Canada Gazette*, Part II.

**Manufacturing Dates for New Standards**:
- **September 29, 2025**: for motors with **THP < 0.5**, or **1.15  THP  5**
- **September 28, 2027**: for motors with **0.5  THP < 1.15**

### Regulatory Pathway
NRCan is considering amending the Regulations through a Governor in Council amendment. Changes would be pre-published in the *Canada Gazette*, Part I, followed by a public comment period.

## Labelling Requirements
NRCan is **not considering** labelling requirements for pool pump motors.

## Verification Requirements
Pool pump motors would be required to carry a **third-party verification mark** from a Standards Council of Canada accredited certification body. This mark would indicate that the product meets energy efficiency standards and that reported information has been verified.

## Reporting Requirements

### Energy Efficiency Report
This report must be submitted by the dealer to NRCan **before** the product is imported into Canada or traded inter-provincially for the first time. It would include:
- **Name of product**: Pool pump motor
- **Brand name**
- **Model number**
- **Name of the manufacturer**
- **Pool pump nominal motor power**: in kW (hp)
- **Pool pump motor service factor**
- **Total horsepower of the pool pump motor**: in kW (hp)
- **Efficiency**: in percent (if required)
- **Name of the certification body** whose verification mark is affixed
- **Whether a mathematical model was used** to generate product specific information

### Import Report
A dealer importing these products into Canada would include the following information on the customs release document:
- **Name of product**
- **Model number**
- **Brand name**
- **Address of the dealer** importing the product
- **Purpose for import**: (e.g., for sale/lease in Canada without modification; for sale/lease in Canada after modification; for use as a component in an exported product)

## Comments Invited
NRCan is seeking feedback on the proposed requirements for pool pump motors, specifically regarding:
- **Pool pump motor products manufactured within Canada**
- **Canada-specific sales or shipment data** for pool pump motors and by motor size
