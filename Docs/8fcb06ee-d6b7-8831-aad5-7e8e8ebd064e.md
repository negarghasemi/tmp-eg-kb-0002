# Home Renovation Savings Program

**Program Overview:**
Enbridge Gas and Save on Energy, with support from the Ontario Government, have partnered to launch the new **Home Renovation Savings program**.

**Goal:** To help Ontarians improve home energy efficiency and comfort. Homeowners have flexible options to choose single or bundled upgrades for greater savings.

## Key Savings Highlights
*   **Solar & Storage:** Up to **$10,000**
*   **Insulation:** Up to **$7,700**
*   **Heat Pumps:** Up to **$12,000**

## Available Upgrades and Rebates

### Do two or more upgrades
*Rebates that require a home energy assessment*

-   **Insulation** (attic, wall, foundation, exposed floor): Up to **$7,700**
-   **Windows and doors:** **$100** per rough opening
-   **Heat pump water heaters:** **$500**
-   **Air sealing:** Up to **$250**

### Do one or more upgrades
*Rebates that do not require a home energy assessment*

-   **Heat pumps:** Up to **$12,000**
-   **Solar panels and battery storage:** Up to **$10,000**
-   **Attic insulation:** **$1,250** (*previously $1,000*)
-   **Smart thermostats:** **$100** (*previously $75*)
-   **Appliances:** Up to **$200**
