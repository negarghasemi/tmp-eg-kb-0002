# The Canadian Licensed Professionals Guide to ENERGY STAR certification

This guide provides essential information for **licensed professionals** involved in verifying applications for **ENERGY STAR certification** for commercial and institutional buildings in Canada. It outlines the step-by-step process and is crucial reading for those verifying applications.

*It has been adapted for Canada and outlines the step-by-step process and is required reading if youre verifying applications for ENERGY STAR certification.*

[Download the Canadian Licensed Professionals Guide](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/sites/nrcan/files/energy/energystar/pdf/Cdn_Licensed_Prof_Guide_august_2021.pdf) *(PDF - 1.48 MB)*

**ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager** is the primary tool for information on the application process, offering a Canadian-adapted interface to support the certification process.

## Eligibility Requirements for Licensed Professionals

To verify applications for ENERGY STAR certification, licensed professionals must meet the following criteria:

- Possess a **valid license** in any Canadian province or territory or U.S. state as a **Professional Engineer (P. Eng)** or **Registered Architect**, and be in good standing.
- Have **solid knowledge** of building equipment and systems, energy use in buildings, **ASHRAE Standards 55 and 62.1**, and the *IESNA Lighting Handbook*.
- Understand all applicable provincial or territorial engineering or architectural licensure laws, professional ethics requirements, and regulations prior to offering or performing services in a jurisdiction.

*Note: You do not need to hold a license in the same jurisdiction as the building youre verifying, and you may be an employee of the company applying for the ENERGY STAR.*

Only licensed professionals who meet all of these criteria are eligible to verify applications for ENERGY STAR certification.

## Audits

**NRCan** conducts **random audits** of applications in the review process to ensure the integrity of the **ENERGY STAR brand** and to improve the application process. The objective is to confirm the accuracy of energy data and building use details entered into Portfolio Manager and verified during the LPs site visit.

Examples of information NRCan may request during an audit include:

- Copies of all **utility bills and invoices** for fuel purchases within the period of performance.
- Documentation and/or explanation of how the **building use details were verified**.
- Documentation and/or explanation of the **measurement and calculation methods** used to assess the indoor environmental quality.

LPs and the primary contact for the application will receive audit documents and instructions, with a **two-week deadline** for submission. It is recommended to keep records related to an application for ENERGY STAR certification for **two years** from the award date.

## NRCan Takes This Seriously

Falsifying information on an application for ENERGY STAR Certification can lead to serious consequences:

- NRCan reserves the right to **rescind certifications** of buildings verified by the LP.
- **Restrictions** on the LPs signing authority under the ENERGY STAR certification program.
- Pursuit of **recourse through the engineering or architectural professional licensing authority** granting that individuals license.
