# Gas Fireplaces

## Looking for an Energy-Efficient Gas Fireplace?

In Canada, gas fireplaces:
- Must provide **certified testing and report** by regulations.
- Have a voluntary **EnerGuide label** that indicates how much energy that model uses.

## Fireplace Technology and Energy Efficiency
Gas fireplaces are easier to use, cleaner, and more efficient than traditional wood-burning fireplaces. They provide heat and a cozy ambiance. However, they are **not an efficient way to heat a whole house**.

### Consider the Second Price Tag
When purchasing, always inquire about energy efficiency. The sticker price is the first cost, but the **cost of energy over the appliance's lifetime is the second price tag**. Energy efficiency can significantly reduce this ongoing cost, with some models being more than twice as efficient as others.

### Use EnerGuide to Compare Energy Efficiency
The most reliable way to shop for an energy-efficient fireplace is to look for the voluntary **[EnerGuide label](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/product-energy-ratings/energuide/gas-fireplaces-energuide)** on the product literature. This label features a **Fireplace Efficiency (FE) rating** (e.g., 65%), allowing for easy comparison between different models. The EnerGuide label also assures that the product has been tested and certified to CSA standards.

### **ALERT: Carbon Monoxide Risk**
Be aware of the risk of **[carbon monoxide](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/node/15767)** spillage in your home when using a gas- or wood-burning fireplace.

### Types of Fireplaces
Three basic types of gas fireplaces are widely available:
- **Inserts**: Installed in an existing fireplace cavity to convert a wood-burning fireplace to gas or propane.
- **Factory-built (zero-clearance)**: Installed during new home construction or a renovation project.
- **Free-standing**: Resemble wood-burning stoves; all surfaces are exposed. These units tend to heat more effectively than other types.

### Types of Ignition Systems
There are two basic types of ignition systems:
- **Continuous pilot light**: Has a flame that burns constantly, even when the fireplace is not in use. This adds up to significant extra energy use. *Turning off the pilot light during infrequent or no use can save energy.*
- **Electronic or automatic starter**: Relights the pilot flame for each use, saving energy. Some models offer a battery backup to ensure heat during power failures.

## Regulations Set the Energy Efficiency Minimum
All gas fireplaces are subject to Canadas **[Energy Efficiency Regulations](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-regulations/gas-fireplaces)**, which set a testing standard for their energy efficiency. This prevents the least efficient products from entering the Canadian market.
