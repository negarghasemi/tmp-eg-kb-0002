# Energy Affordability Program (EAP) FAQs

_This document summarizes frequently asked questions about the Energy Affordability Program, designed to help income-eligible electricity customers reduce energy costs and increase home comfort._

## General FAQs

### **What is the Energy Affordability Program?**
**Answer:** The Save on Energy Energy Affordability Program (EAP) is an electricity conservation program offered by the Independent Electricity System Operator (IESO). It provides no-cost energy-efficient upgrades for homes of income-eligible electricity customers to help reduce energy costs and increase home comfort.

Two levels of support are available:
*   **Comprehensive Support:** For participants most in need, including a free in-home energy assessment by an energy expert to identify upgrades like replacement appliances and weather stripping.
*   **Energy Saving Kit:** For moderate income-level participants, a customized kit for self-installation including items like energy-saving LED lighting, timers, and a clothes drying line.

### **Is everything really offered free of charge?**
**Answer:** Yes! The Energy Affordability Program is offered by the IESO, and all energy-saving upgrades are completely free of charge. The IESO oversees energy-efficiency programs in Ontario.

### **Who can participate in the Energy Affordability Program?**
**Answer:** If you live in Ontario and are responsible for paying your electricity bill as a homeowner or renter, you may be eligible. Residents of social housing may also be eligible.

Eligibility is based on: 
*   Household income and number of people in your home.
*   Receipt of a qualifying eligible benefit.

_Note: Ontario residents served by Cornwall Electric are not eligible as they are not connected to the IESO-controlled grid. Contact [Cornwall Electric](https://www.cornwallelectric.com/residential/ontario-electricity-support-program-oesp) for other available programs._

### **How do I know if the Energy Affordability Program is right for me?**
**Answer:** You may benefit if you are income-eligible or have received a qualifying assistance benefit in the past 12 months, and have products in your home that are not energy efficient (e.g., light bulbs, old appliances).

_If you need direct financial assistance with your electricity bill, visit [www.oeb.ca/rates-and-your-bill/help-low-income-consumers](https://www.oeb.ca/consumer-information-and-protection/bill-assistance-programs) for other programs._

### **I rent my home. What information is shared with my landlord?**
**Answer:** If a home assessment identifies landlord-owned appliances or home insulation for an upgrade, your landlord will be offered the upgrade. The EAP does not share any personal financial information about your eligibility.

### **Are your delivery agents and contractors authorized?**
**Answer:** Yes, the program works with authorized delivery agents across Ontario:
*   **Postal codes starting with K:** EnviroCentre
*   **Postal codes starting with L, M or P:** CLEAResult
*   **Postal codes starting with N:** The Home Inspectors Group

### **Where can I get more information?**
**Answer:** Call **1-844-770-3148** or visit the EAP website at [SaveonEnergy.ca/EAP](https://www.saveonenergy.ca/For-Your-Home/Energy-Affordability-Program).

### **Is the Home Assistance Program still available?**
**Answer:** The Home Assistance Program has been replaced by the new Save on Energy Energy Affordability Program (EAP). EAP offers the same free in-home energy assessments and energy-saving products with no-cost installation, expanded eligibility, and Energy Saving Kits for moderate income-level participants.

### **Im in need of urgent electricity-bill support. Who can I contact?**
**Answer:** Visit the [OEB website](https://www.oeb.ca/rates-and-your-bill/help-low-income-consumers) for information on electricity-bill support programs. Income-eligible customers can still apply for the EAP; if your region is restricted, you will be placed on a waitlist.

### **I am currently participating in the Energy Affordability Program and am waiting on delivery of my appliance upgrades and/or installation of weatherization products  when will these activities be completed?**
**Answer:** Program participants waiting for deliveries will be contacted to schedule installations as regional public health measures permit. Some product deliveries have been affected by supply chain delays.

## Comprehensive Support FAQs

### **What happens during my in-home energy assessment?**
**Answer:** An energy expert will:
*   Ask you to sign a Participant Agreement and verify eligibility documents.
*   Install qualifying measures (e.g., **ENERGY STAR certified LED lightbulbs**, efficient showerheads and faucet aerators, a clothes drying rack).
*   Ask about other electricity-using appliances and equipment.
*   Provide information on reducing electricity use and energy bills.
*   Assess the age and efficiency of appliances, insulation, and draft levels.
*   Take photographs of appliances and home interior/exterior.

_If you qualify for additional equipment, an energy expert may schedule a second visit for installation._

### **What happens after my in-home energy assessment?**
**Answer:** The EAP delivery partner will:
*   Review collected information to determine if your home qualifies for replacement with new, **ENERGY STAR certified equipment**.
*   If your home is electrically heated, assess for increased insulation (attic, walls, basement) and comprehensive draft proofing.
*   Notify you of results via letter or email.
*   If qualified for upgrades, arrange an installation date. You can choose to accept or decline any offered upgrades.

### **What upgrades can I receive as a result of my in-home energy assessment?**
**Answer:** Free upgrades include:
*   **ENERGY STAR certified light bulbs (LEDs)**
*   **Energy-efficient shower heads and faucet aerators**
*   **Clothes drying rack**
*   **Power bar with a timer**

Based on the assessment, you may also qualify for:
*   **ENERGY STAR certified replacement appliances:** fridge, freezer, window air conditioner, and/or dehumidifier.
*   **Insulation:** in walls, attic, and basement (if electrically heated).
*   **Comprehensive home draft-proofing** (if electrically heated).

### **What information do you collect about my home?**
**Answer:** With your consent, the EAP collects information on your home's efficiency, age, heat-fuel type (e.g., electricity, natural gas), appliances, heating system equipment, electronics, and lighting. This helps determine appropriate energy-efficient upgrades. With your permission, the delivery partner or IESO may refer you to other no-cost energy-affordability programs.

### **Will the Energy Affordability Program contact me after my assessment?**
**Answer:** Yes, you will receive communications from the IESO and the program delivery partner regarding your EAP participation, including customer surveys.

### **What happens to my old appliances?**
**Answer:** If you qualify for new appliances, you consent to the removal and disposal of your old, inefficient appliance. Old appliances are decommissioned, harmful substances are disposed of responsibly, and other materials are recycled.

## Energy Saving Kits FAQs

### **How will the Energy Affordability Program delivery partner verify that I am eligible to receive an Energy Saving Kit for self-installation?**
**Answer:** You may need to provide proof of eligibility (e.g., Notice of Assessment or proof of a qualifying support benefit). All documentation will be securely destroyed once eligibility is verified.

### **What will be included in my Energy Saving Kit?**
**Answer:** Depending on your household needs and eligibility, your kit may include free energy-saving measures for self-installation, such as:
*   **Energy-saving light bulbs**
*   **Efficient shower heads**
*   **Faucet aerators**
*   **Clothes drying line**
*   **Weather-stripping**

### **How is the Energy Saving Kit customized for me and my home?**
**Answer:** The kit is customized based on:
*   The number of inefficient light bulbs in your home.
*   The primary heating fuel source for your home and hot water.

_If your home and/or hot water are heated by electricity, you may qualify for energy-saving water measures and/or weather-stripping. You may also receive a block heater timer if you use a block heater for your vehicle._

### **What happens after I receive my Energy Saving Kit?**
**Answer:** The energy-saving measures are suitable for self-installation. After delivery, the EAP delivery partner will follow up by phone or email to verify installation and functionality. The IESO may also invite you to participate in a survey.

### **What if I have trouble installing the items in the Energy Saving Kit?**
**Answer:**
*   If you received a kit in **2021**, contact the IESO's EAP Delivery Partner, GreenSaver, at **1-888-855-3106**.
*   If you received a kit in **2022**, contact the IESO's EAP Delivery Partner, Ecofitt, at **1-888-341-9778**.
