# Energy Efficiency Reporting (EER) Templates

*This page provides information on Energy Efficiency Reporting (EER) templates for various energy-using products, developed in collaboration with Open Government.*

## Purpose of EER Templates

Dealers are required to use these product-specific **Energy Efficiency Report (EER) templates** to report regulated energy-using products to **Natural Resources Canada (NRCan)** prior to their first import.

## Submission of Reports

Completed EER templates must be submitted electronically to NRCan through the:
- **[Compliance Regulatory Energy Efficiency Database](https://energy-reporting-rapports-energetiques.canada.ca/creed/s/)**

## Available Product Categories for Reporting

Templates are available for the following product and equipment categories:
- **[Commercial Refrigeration Equipment](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/38ef4d26-b838-447b-b874-9233a0884cf0)**
- **[Electronic Products](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/4382d531-04ed-46b7-a10b-35df6fe36d56)**
- **[Heating and Air-Conditioning Equipment](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/02e85d01-ee78-4c4b-9d8f-56b61b8142ce)**
- **[Household Appliances](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/c1d860a2-e451-4df9-86d0-70c8699600e0)**
- **[Industrial/Commercial Equipment](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/fc60c829-506f-42c5-bd4e-9b72a0aac687)**
- **[Lighting Products](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/cab4826f-4a19-41f8-9041-3f02a1f6d966)**
- **[Plumbing Products](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/675d7095-4ceb-44f1-85f1-bf99bcb67950)**
- **[Water Heaters](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/78dc45c6-a4ad-4914-b51a-b4fc1e0b296d)**

## Requesting Templates for Other Products
