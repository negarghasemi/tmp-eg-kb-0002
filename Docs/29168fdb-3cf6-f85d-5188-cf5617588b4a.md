## Overview
The Heating and Cooling Series is published by Natural Resources Canada's Office of Energy Efficiency's EnerGuide programs.

**EnerGuide** is the official Government of Canada mark associated with the labelling and rating of the energy consumption or energy efficiency of:
-   Household appliances
-   Heating and ventilation equipment
-   Air conditioners
-   Houses
-   Vehicles

EnerGuide helps manufacturers and dealers promote energy-efficient equipment and provides consumers with the information they need to choose energy-efficient residential equipment.
