# Home Renovation Savings Program

## Program Overview
Save on Energy and Enbridge Gas, supported by the Ontario government, have partnered to deliver the **Home Renovation Savings** program. This program aims to help Ontarians improve home efficiency* and comfort by making home renovations more affordable.

**Eligibility:** Homeowners who heat their home with electricity, natural gas, oil, propane, or wood can access rebates for energy-saving upgrades.

## Your Upgrades, Your Way
Homeowners have flexible options for single and bundled upgrades, offering greater savings. You can choose to complete a home energy assessment and bundle upgrades, or opt for single upgrades.

### Bundle and Save!
*Start with a home energy assessment, then complete two or more upgrades to receive your rebates.*

| Upgrade                         | Rebate Amount                                   |
| :------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------- |
| **Home energy assessment**      | **$600** back                                   |
| **Insulation**                  | Up to **$7,700**                                |
| **Windows and doors**           | **$100** back per rough opening                 |
| **Air sealing**                 | Up to **$250**                                  |\
| **Heat pump water heaters**     | **$500** back on qualified heat pump water heaters |

*Windows, doors and heat pump water heaters must be an ENERGY STAR certified model.*

### Single Upgrades
*Skip the home energy assessment. Receive rebates on eligible energy-efficiency upgrades.*

| Upgrade                                 | Rebate Amount                                   |
| :-------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- |
| **Cold climate air source heat pump**   | Up to **$7,500**                               |\
| **Ground source heat pump**             | Up to **$12,000**                              |\
| **Smart thermostat**                    | **$100** (previously $75)                       |\
| **Solar panels and battery storage**    | Up to **$10,000**                               |\
| **Appliances**                          | Up to **$200** back                             |\
| **Attic insulation**                    | **$1,250** (previously $1,000)                  |

*The heat pump you choose must be on Natural Resources Canadas qualified products list.*
