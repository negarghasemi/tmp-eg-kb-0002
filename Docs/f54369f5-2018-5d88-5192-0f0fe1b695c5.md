# Save on Energy Program Information

*Supporting information for Save on Energy programs under the 2021-2024 CDM Framework.*

The **2021-2024 Conservation and Demand Management (CDM) Framework** focuses on cost-effectively meeting the needs of customers and the electricity system. The framework is on track to achieve its original energy and demand savings target. Enhancements will be made to programs to reflect changing market conditions and customer feedback.

## Business and Industry

### Retrofit Program

*Save on Energys flagship program offers businesses a more customer-friendly experience.*

- **Features**: Expanded list of energy-efficient measures, faster application review processes, quicker incentive payments.
- **Custom Applications**: Beginning Spring 2023, the program will accept custom applications from businesses, institutional, and industrial customers.
- **Measures**: Available under three streams: **lighting**, **HVAC**, and **manufacturing and other equipment**.
- **Updates**: Incentives and measures will be updated throughout the four-year framework based on marketplace feedback.
- **Suggestions**: Businesses can submit suggestions for new measures.

### Industrial Energy Efficiency Program

*Replaced the Process and Systems Upgrade (PSU) program as of December 31, 2020.*

- **Focus**: Supports participants in getting larger, more complex projects off the ground.
- **Benefit**: Reduces administrative burden and addresses the unique needs of industrial consumers.

### Energy Performance Program

*Award-winning program for continuous whole-building energy improvements under a pay-for-performance model.*

- **Eligibility**: Available for commercial, institutional facilities, and now broadened to include industrial consumers.
- **Enhancements**: Provides stability for participants and sustained energy savings.
- **New Payments**: Includes payments for achieving annual peak demand savings in addition to annual energy savings.
- **Contract Term**: Extended to **three years**.
- **Upfront Incentives**: Included to support initial capital investments.
- **Streamlined Requirements**: Technical requirements have been streamlined to improve customer experience and reduce administrative burden.
- **Incentive Information**:
    - **Energy Incentive**: **$0.04/kWh** paid annually over the contract term for metered savings against a baseline.
    - **Peak Demand Incentive**: An additional **$50/kW** paid annually over the three-year term for metered savings against the facilitys average peak demand over summer months in the baseline year.

### Energy Manager Program

*Evolving from the Embedded Energy Manager model to a strategic energy management model in 2023.*

- **Current Impact**: Instrumental in building a skilled network of energy professionals, supporting nearly 200 Energy Managers.
- **Evolution (Strategic Energy Management - SEM program)**:
    - Shifts incentive from direct salary funding to enhanced technical support and resources for companies with a dedicated energy management professional.
    - Provides greater flexibility and empowers organizations through increased training opportunities and access to industry tools.
- **Launch**: SEM program to be launched in January 2023.
- **Training**: Offers hands-on instruction and guidance to small cohorts by sector across Ontario.
- **Enabling Incentives**: Participants eligible to receive incentives for energy management tools and services upon completing milestones.

### Small Business Program

*Replaced the previous Small Business Lighting program.*

- **Purpose**: Helps small businesses make equipment improvements to reduce energy consumption.
- **Cost**: **No cost** to participants.
- **Offerings**: Direct-install incentives on an expanded range of equipment, including both **lighting and non-lighting**.

### Foodservice Distributor Discount Program

*Offers instant discounts for commercial businesses.*

- **Mechanism**: Instant discounts on eligible foodservice equipment purchased from participating distributors/dealers.
- **Partnership**: Delivered in partnership with Enbridge.

### Training and Support Opportunities

- **Availability**: Courses and workshops for energy professionals.
- **Incentives**: Training incentives available throughout the four-year framework for energy management, specialized skills training, and professional development (industry certifications).
- **Resources**: Foundational energy-efficiency educational content, sector-specific information guides, and project-based case studies will be available.

## Programs for First Nations

### First Nations Communities

*Programs assist on-reserve First Nations communities in benefiting from energy-saving upgrades.*

- **Goal**: Improve on-reserve homes and buildings and help residents and communities save on energy costs.

### First Nations Community Building Retrofit Program

- **Eligibility**: On-reserve First Nations communities in Ontario connected to the grid.
- **Funding**: Offers **up to $100,000** in funding and technical support.
- **Focus**: Undertake projects to improve the energy efficiency of band-owned commercial and institutional facilities.

### Remote First Nations Energy-Efficiency Program

- **Purpose**: Provides funding support to remote First Nations communities.
- **Goal**: Implement energy-efficient projects to manage energy use more effectively and increase home and business comfort.

### Energy Affordability Program (for First Nations Residents)

- **Availability**: Also available to eligible grid-connected First Nation residents.
- **Benefits**: Offers energy-saving upgrades tailored to specific home needs, all at **no cost**.
- **Services**: May include a free energy needs assessment by a trained professional to identify upgrades (e.g., replacement appliances, weather stripping) and free energy-saving kits (e.g., LED lighting, timers, faucet aerators, clothes drying line).
- **Resources**: Brochures available in Ojibwe, Cree, and Oji-Cree.

## Residential Programs and Resources

### Energy Affordability Program

*Expanded eligibility from the previous Home Assistance Program.*

- **Purpose**: Supports income-eligible electricity consumers in lowering monthly electricity costs and increasing home comfort.
- **Services for those most in need**:
    - **Free energy needs assessment** by a trained energy professional.
    - Identification of energy-efficient upgrades (e.g., replacement appliances, weather stripping).
    - **Free energy-saving kits** customized to meet energy needs (e.g., LED lighting, timers, faucet aerators, clothes drying line).
- **Contact**: Interested participants can contact a representative at **1-844-770-3148**.

### My Energy Action Plan

*An online tool launched in March 2022.*

- **Purpose**: Provides residential consumers with low-cost, no-cost ways to improve home energy efficiency.
- **Features**: Online questionnaire offering tailored advice and tips to manage electricity use, improve home comfort, and save on energy costs.

### Tips and Information

- **Resources**: Information and tips for all residential consumers on how to save energy and manage electricity costs.
- **Content**: Includes resources for smart buying decisions on energy-efficient equipment and tips for improving living and working spaces.
- **Updates**: Content and tools will be refreshed throughout the four-year framework to reflect seasonality and societal needs.

## Additional Resources and Opportunities

### Local Program Opportunities

- **Purpose**: Targeted local programming to maximize value to electricity ratepayers and customers.
- **Benefit**: Uses energy efficiency as a resource to manage local electricity demand and avoid or defer costly infrastructure investments.
- **Deployment**: Opportunities will be deployed in targeted areas across Ontario based on needs identified through the IESOs regional planning process.
- **Mechanism**: Leverages competitive mechanisms to provide targeted energy and demand savings, encouraging innovation.

## Coming in 2023

*Save on Energy continues to develop new programs in response to customer feedback. The following new programs are planned for launch in 2023:*

- **Strategic Energy Management program**:
    - An evolution of the Energy Manager program.
    - Provides training, resources, and enhanced technical support to companies with a dedicated energy manager.
- **Existing Building Commissioning program**:
    - Helps find opportunities to optimize operations and improve energy efficiency based on current facility requirements.
- **Commercial Midstream Lighting program**:
    - Offers lighting incentives for lighting distributors.
    - Aims to increase sales of energy-efficient lighting through point-of-sale discounts.
- **Additional local initiatives**:
    - To be implemented in targeted areas of the province where electricity constraints exist.

*New and enhanced programs announced on October 4, 2022, and launched by Spring 2023, include:*

- A new **residential demand response program** for homes with existing central air conditioning and smart thermostats to help lower energy use at peak times.
- **Targeted support for greenhouse growers** in Southwest Ontario, including incentives to install LED lighting, advanced controls, or behind-the-meter distributed energy resources (DER) such as combined solar generation and battery storage.
- Enhancements to the **Save on Energy Business Retrofit Program** for businesses, institutional, and industrial customers to include custom energy-efficiency projects.
- Enhancements to the **Save on Energy Local Initiatives Program** to reduce barriers to participation and to add flexibility for incentives for DER solutions.
