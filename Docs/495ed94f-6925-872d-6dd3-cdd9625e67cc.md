# Guide to Canada's Energy Efficiency Regulations

## Overview
Canadas *Energy Efficiency Act* (Act) and Energy Efficiency Regulations (the Regulations) mandate that all regulated products must meet federal energy efficiency standards to be imported into Canada or shipped from one province to another for the purpose of sale or lease. These regulations still apply even if the products are integrated into a larger, unregulated unit or machine.

## Provincial Regulations
Some provinces also have their own energy efficiency regulations that apply to many of the federally regulated products.
