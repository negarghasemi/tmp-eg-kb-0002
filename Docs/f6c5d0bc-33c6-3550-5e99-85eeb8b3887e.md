# Home Renovation Savings  Energy Assessment Required Upgrades

## Overview

**Bundle upgrades and save!** A home energy assessment will help you identify the best energy efficiency solutions for your home. Bundle two or more upgrades for bigger rebates, energy savings* and comfort. Bundle three and get an extra $500.

## Rebates

Start with an initial home energy assessment, then complete at least two energy-saving* upgrades to qualify for rebates.

### Insulation Rebates

- **Attic insulation:** Up to $1,500
- **Foundation insulation:** Up to $2,300
- **Exposed floor insulation:** $300
- **Exterior wall insulation:** Up to $3,600

### Other Rebates

- **Windows and doors:** $100 per rough opening
- **Heat pump water heaters:** $500
- **Air sealing:** Up to $250
  *To qualify, your home needs to meet a specific air tightness target. Since results can vary, we recommend choosing air sealing as your third upgrade or later to help maintain your eligibility.*

### Bonus Rebate

- **Bundle three upgrades, get $500!**

### Home Energy Assessments

- **Rebate:** $600
  *An initial home energy assessment is required before beginning any work. A registered energy advisor will assess your home and uncover energy-saving opportunities. To offset the cost of the initial and follow-up home energy assessments, you will receive a $600 rebate after successful completion of the program. Where an owner has tenants, the owner must apply.*

## How to Get Your Rebates

1.  **Schedule an assessment:** Before beginning any work, complete an initial home energy assessment with a registered energy advisor from an approved service organization.
2.  **Get a custom report:** See which upgrades are recommended for your home and choose at least two to complete.
3.  **Complete 2+ upgrades:** Have your preferred contractor complete the work.
4.  **Schedule a follow-up assessment:** After upgrades are installed, complete a final assessment with the same registered energy advisor.
5.  **Receive your rebates:** After your follow-up home energy assessment is completed and your registered energy advisor submits all required documents, your cheque should arrive by mail within 60 days.

## Eligibility

- **Ownership:** You must own your home.
- **Eligible Housing Types:** Single detached or semi-detached homes, row/townhomes and mobile homes on a permanent foundation.
- **Heating Source:**
    - Be an **Enbridge Gas customer** with an active account and primarily heat your home with a natural gas furnace or boiler, OR
    - Be connected to the **Ontario electricity grid** and heat your home with electricity, propane, oil or wood.
- *Cornwall Electric customers who heat their homes with electricity, propane, oil, or wood are connected to the Hydro-Qubec electricity grid and are not eligible to participate unless they are converting to or from Enbridge natural gas for home heating.*
