## Text version

### An easy guide to saving energy:
**LOOK FOR THE BLUE STAR**

-   **SAVE TIME**
    *The symbol is a short-cut to find top energy performers*
-   **SAVE ENERGY**
    *Certified products are in the top **15-30%** in their class for energy performance*
-   **SAVE MONEY**
    *Using certified products cuts utility bills*
-   **PROTECT THE PLANET**
    *Using certified products lowers greenhouse gas emissions  the GHGs saved from using ENERGY STAR products was equal to taking 100,000 cars off the road this year!*
-   **YOU HAVE THE POWER**
    *Choose from over 80 types of ENERGY STAR certified products for home and work*

### WHATS BEHIND THE SYMBOL?

-   **HIGH QUALITY**
    *They perform the same or better than standard products but are designed to save energy and money*
-   **TESTED & CERTIFIED**
    *They are tested to meet or exceed strict technical specs. Energy performance is tested by a third-party*
