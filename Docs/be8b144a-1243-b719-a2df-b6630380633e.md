# ERA - Energy Retrofit Assistance Program

**Free for Income-Qualified Households in the Peel Region**

This pilot program is designed to assist energy-burdened and income-qualified households by removing cost barriers and providing immediate greater comfort, energy, and cost-savings through retrofits.

### What does the program include?

*   **Free upgrades** that may include an air source heat pump or hybrid water heater.
*   An **EnerGuide Audit** (site visit and assessment) from a certified Natural Resource Canada Energy Advisor.
*   A comprehensive **EnerGuide report** with recommended energy efficiency upgrades.
*   Upgrades that may or may not include:
    *   Air Source Heat Pump installation
    *   Heat pump water heater
    *   LED bulbs
    *   Programmable thermostat
    *   Weather stripping
    *   Carbon monoxide monitor

### What is a home energy assessment and what is involved?

The Energy Advisor will collect data, often starting on the exterior with photos and potentially a thermal imaging camera, then moving to the interior. This data is entered into energy performance software to calculate your home's [EnerGuide rating](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/energuide-canada/energuide-rating-system-version-15/18392).

**Data collected may include:**
*   Air tightness, insulation levels, and types of windows and doors.
*   Heating system sizes and ratings, water heaters, ventilation (if any), and air conditioning.
*   Confirmation and locations of all heating and cooling systems.
*   All wood-fired and fuel-fired heating must be turned off prior to the evaluation and will be returned to pre-test settings when the site visit is complete.

### Is the program available only to owner-households, or can renters also participate?

Initially, the program is for homeowners, but it is designed to eventually expand to renters.

### Is there a dollar cap on the retrofits provided in the CCET ERA program?

The retrofits are based on the most beneficial installations. An average amount of upgrades is targeted at **$11,500 per household**, with some homes requiring more and others less.

### What do I need to do to prepare for a site visit?

*   Have any equipment and mechanical systems information available (age and model numbers are helpful but not required).
*   Ensure access to mechanical systems is clear and easy.
*   Liability and consent forms must be signed and submitted to CCET.

### Is the work warrantied?

Yes, licensed contractors who warranty their work are used.

### Do I have a choice of the contractor for the installations?

No, CCET has carefully chosen and vetted contractors for this program, working closely with experts at [Mits Air Conditioning](https://www.mitsair.com/).

### Who do I call if I have questions or concerns?

CCET energy coaches will answer program questions and assist with retrofit decisions, equipment information, and scheduling for audits and installations. Email [info@energytransform.ca](mailto:info@energytransform.ca) or call **1-289-998-0923**.

### If I meet the requirements, will I be guaranteed to receive the free retrofits?

CCET and energy advisors will determine the households most in need of retrofits. You may not qualify in all categories. Households receiving retrofits are based on four criteria:

*   Income and energy burden percentage
*   Age of house
*   Age of mechanical systems
*   Gas disconnection cost savings

### What if I dont want some of the recommended retrofits?

You may decide if you dont want any of the recommended installations.

### Are the upgrades CCET provides really free?

**Yes! There is no cost to the homeowner!**

## Additional Programs and Resources

### Free or Low Cost Income-Qualified Programs

*   **Federal Oil to Heat Pump Affordability Program**
    *   **Description**: Eligible Canadian homeowners can receive up to **$10,000** to switch from oil heating to a new energy-efficient heat pump.
    *   **Eligibility**: Home heated by oil, household income at or below median after-tax income.
    *   **Eligible Measures**: Electrical/mechanical upgrades for heat pump, outdoor piping for ground source, safe oil tank removal, backup electrical heating (if required), switching other oil-using systems (e.g., water heater).

*   **100% Free Cold Climate Air Source Heat Pumps (Save on EnergyEnergy Affordability Program)**
    *   **Description**: Free cold climate air source heat pumps for qualifying Ontario households with electrically heated homes. Includes a two-year free maintenance warranty and a 10-year equipment and labor warranty.
    *   **Additional Benefits**: May also be eligible for other free energy efficiency upgrades (e.g., ENERGY STAR appliances, weatherstripping, attic/basement insulation, smart thermostats, power strips).

|*   **Home Winterproofing Program (HWP) | Enbridge Gas**
    *   **Description**: Streamlined with the Energy Affordability Program to offer free home energy upgrades.
    *   **Eligibility**: Qualify based on home and household income levels.
    *   **Benefits**: Free insulation, draft proofing, smart thermostat, Energy Star appliances, and more. CLEAResult is the authorized delivery partner.

*   **Hydro One Low Income Energy Assistance Program (LEAP)**
    *   **Description**: Pays up to **$1,000** of the amount owing on your bill (up to **$1,200** if electrically heated).

*   **Save on Energy  Energy Affordability Program**
    *   **Description**: Provides expert advice and energy-saving home upgrades completely free of charge for income-eligible households.
    *   **Eligibility**: Responsible for paying monthly electricity bill as homeowner or renter; based on household income and size, or receipt of qualifying government/energy-support benefit. Social housing residents may also be eligible.
    *   **Types of Support**: Comprehensive upgrades or Energy Saving Kits.

*   **Ontario Electricity Support Program (OESP)**
    *   **Description**: Lowers electricity bills for lower-income households with a monthly credit based on household income and size.
    *   **Benefit**: Credits applied directly to eligible customers bills.

### More DIY Tips

*   **Water Heater**: Insulate older water heaters located in cold areas with inexpensive blankets (**$30-$40**) for significant cost savings.
*   **Curtains**: Use heavy fabric curtains to reduce cold, drafty air from windows.
*   **Cold Air Drafts**: Seal electrical outlets on outside walls with foam insulators. Use weather stripping around doors and windows.
*   **Air Filters**: Replace furnace filters regularly per manufacturer's recommendation to prevent the furnace from working harder. Bleed hot water radiators annually for efficiency. Service natural gas/propane furnaces every two years, oil furnaces annually. Keep vents and air grills clear of obstructions.
