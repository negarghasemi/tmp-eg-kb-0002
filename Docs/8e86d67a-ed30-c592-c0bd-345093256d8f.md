# Customer Choice for Electricity Pricing

If you are a residential or small business customer, you have the option to choose between **Tiered**, **Time-of-Use (TOU)**, and **Ultra-Low Overnight (ULO)** electricity pricing plans. You can switch between these plans even if you've recently made a change.

## How to Switch Price Plans

To switch your electricity price plan, you can notify Oakville Hydro through one of the following methods:

-   **Online:** Log into MyOakvilleHydro, click 'Choose your Electricity Price Plan', and then 'Switch Price Plan Now'.
-   **Email:** Complete an election [form](https://www.oakvillehydro.com/sites/oakvillehydro/files/2024-07/Customer%20Choice%20Election%20Form_September%202023_0.pdf) and email it to [customerservice@oakvillehydro.com](mailto:customerservice@oakvillehydro.com).
-   **Phone:** Contact customer service at **905-825-9400**, Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM.

*Note: A switch takes effect at the start of a service period and may be retroactive. No action is required if you wish to continue with your current pricing plan.*

## Electricity Pricing Plans Explained

### Tiered Prices

With Tiered prices, you pay a lower price for electricity up to a certain limit (threshold). Once this limit is exceeded, a higher price applies.

-   **Residential Customers:** The threshold changes seasonally:
    -   **Winter Period (November 1  April 30):** **1,000 kWh** at the lower price.
    -   **Summer Period (May 1  October 31):** **600 kWh** at the lower price.
    *This seasonal change reflects varying usage patterns, such as electric heating in winter or air conditioning in summer.*
-   **Small Business Customers:** The Tier threshold is **750 kWh** all year round.

*Tiered prices offer flexibility to use electricity at any time of day at the same price, changing only when the monthly threshold is crossed.*

### TOU and ULO Prices

With Time-of-Use (TOU) and Ultra-Low Overnight (ULO) prices, the cost of electricity depends on **when** you use it. You can manage your electricity costs by shifting usage to lower-priced periods.

#### Time-of-Use (TOU) - Three Price Periods:

-   **Off-peak:** Demand for electricity is lowest. *Nearly two-thirds of residential electricity is used during these hours.*
-   **Mid-peak:** Moderate demand for electricity. *These periods are during the daytime but avoid the busiest hours.*
-   **On-peak:** Highest demand for electricity. *These are typically busier times when people are cooking, using computers, or running heating/cooling systems.*

#### Ultra-Low Overnight (ULO) - Four Price Periods:

-   **Ultra-Low Overnight:** Lowest average demand for electricity.
-   **Weekend Off-peak:** Generally lower demand for electricity.
-   **Mid-peak:** Moderate demand for electricity.
-   **On-peak:** Highest average demand for electricity.

*ULO price periods remain consistent all year, while TOU price periods change between winter and summer.*

## Factors to Consider When Switching Rates

Before switching your electricity price plan, consider the following points, using your recent electricity bills for reference:

-   **Your current price plan:** Most residential and small business customers in Ontario are on TOU prices.
-   **Your monthly usage:** Check the 'Electricity' line on your bill and the historical usage graph for average daily usage over the past 12 months.
-   **Your consumption pattern:** When do you use electricity? Historically, TOU customers use about two-thirds of their power during off-peak times.
-   **Don't guess, calculate:** Use the [OEB bill calculator](https://www.oeb.ca/consumer-information-and-protection/bill-calculator) to compare your total bill under TOU, ULO, and Tiered prices, considering all charges.
-   **Seasonal changes and time of year:** Be aware that price periods and Tier thresholds change seasonally (Winter: Nov 1  Apr 30; Summer: May 1  Oct 31). High electricity usage for heating in winter or air conditioning in summer can significantly impact Tiered pricing.
-   **Lifestyle and daily habits:** Assess if you can shift significant electricity use (e.g., large appliances) to nighttime or weekend hours if you are often home during weekdays.
-   **Small business considerations:** For small businesses, the Tier threshold is 750 kWh year-round. Usage patterns vary greatly between different types of businesses (e.g., a restaurant vs. a salon).
