# Energy Efficiency Report: Clothes Dryers

## Energy Efficiency Regulations

### Regulatory Definition
**Clothes dryer**: A household tumble-type clothes dryer that is electrically operated.

### Compliance Date
**Manufactured on or after**: January 1, 2015
*For models manufactured prior, refer to the Regulations.*

### Compliant Product Models
**Searchable Database**: [Clothes dryers as listed on NRCans searchable database](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=CLOTHESDRYERS)

### Labelling Requirements
**Label**: EnerGuide label required

### Testing Standard
**Standard**: CAN/CSA-C361-16 or Appendix D2 to Subpart B, Part 430 of Title 10 to the United States *Code of Federal Regulations*, entitled *Uniform Test Method for Measuring the Energy Consumption of Clothes Dryers*

### Provincial Energy Efficiency Regulations
*Some provinces also have their own energy efficiency regulations for many of the federally regulated products  [find out which ones!](/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-regulations/energy-products-regulated-federally-provincially)*

### Energy Efficiency Standard

**Energy performance standard for clothes dryers**

| Product type                          | Minimum combined energy factor (kg/kWh) | Minimum combined energy factor (lb/kWh) |
| :------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------- |
| Conventional standard electric        | 1.69                                    | 3.73                                    |
| Conventional compact electric 120 V   | 1.64                                    | 3.61                                    |
| Conventional compact electric 240     | 1.48                                    | 3.27                                    |
| Ventless compact electric 240 V       | 1.16                                    | 2.55                                    |
| Ventless combination washer-dryer electric | 0.94                                    | 2.08                                    |

*Units: V = volts, kg/kWh = kilograms per kilowatt-hours, lb/kWh = pounds per kilowatt-hours*

### Energy Efficiency Report Requirements

**The report must include:**
- **Name of product**
- **Brand name**
- **Model number**
- **Manufacturer**
- **Certification body**: The name of the certification body or province whose verification mark will be on the product or its package
- **Mathematical model usage**: Whether a mathematical model as defined in the Regulations was used to generate any of the information provided
- **Annual energy consumption**: In kWh
- **Size category**: Compact or standard
- **Nominal voltage**
- **Type of drying control**: Timed, auto-temperature or auto-moisture
- **Capacity**: In L, of clothes dryers drum
- **Combined energy factor**: In kg/kWh

**Submission**: Sign in to the [Compliance Regulatory Energy Efficiency Database](https://energy-reporting-rapports-energetiques.canada.ca/creed/s/) (CREED) to download and submit an energy efficiency report for this product.
