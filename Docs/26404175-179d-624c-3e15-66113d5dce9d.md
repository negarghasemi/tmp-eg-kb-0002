# Eligible Retrofits and Grant Amounts - Canada Greener Homes Initiative

**Note:** *The Canada Greener Homes Grant and Loan programs are closed for new applications. Information below pertains to past eligibility criteria and grant amounts.*

## Program Overview

As part of the Canada Greener Homes Initiative, participants had access to:

*   **Grants from $125 to $5,000** for eligible and recommended home retrofits.
*   **Up to $600** as a maximum contribution toward the total costs of pre- and post-retrofit EnerGuide evaluations (if the grant process, including at least one eligible retrofit, was completed).
*   **Interest-free loans of up to $40,000** with a repayment term of 10 years for major home retrofits.

The **maximum grant amount** a household could receive was **$5,600**, including up to $5,000 for eligible retrofits and up to $600 for evaluations.

*Special considerations were made for homeowners in Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia, and Northern/off-grid communities, who should refer to specific provincial/territorial information.* 

## Ineligible Retrofits

The Canada Greener Homes Initiative supported improving the energy efficiency of *existing* homes. The following retrofits were **not eligible**:

*   Retrofits completed **before a pre-retrofit evaluation**.
*   Retrofits completed **before December 1, 2020**.
*   Products installed in **new home additions** built between pre- and post-retrofit evaluations (e.g., insulation for new areas, new windows/doors in additions, components requiring 100% upgrades like crawl space wall insulation).
*   **Leased or rented equipment**.
*   **Air conditioners**.
*   **Tankless or storage water heaters** (other than heat pump water heaters).
*   **Skylights**.
*   **Furnaces** (unless in a northern or off-grid community).
*   **Boilers** (unless in a northern or off-grid community).
*   **Roofs**.
*   **Windows and doors in a garage**.

## Detailed Eligible Retrofits

### Home Insulation

Insulation helps maintain desired indoor temperatures, reduces heating/cooling costs, and improves comfort. Its effectiveness is measured by **R-value** or **RSI-value** (higher value = slower heat transfer).

*   **All products and equipment must be purchased in Canada.** Online purchases are only eligible if from a Canadian distributor.

#### Insulate Your Roof Assemblies (Attic/Cathedral Ceiling/Flat Roof)

*   **Eligibility Criteria:**
    *   Add insulation to at least **20% of the total area** of your roof assembly.
    *   Increase the insulation value (R-value or RSI) as per the table below.
    *   Additional insulation must be in the same location as existing insulation.
*   **Important Notes:**
    *   Grant amount is **pro-rated** based on the percentage of the total roof assembly insulated.
    *   **Maximum total grant for any combination of roof assembly insulation is $1,800.**

| Insulation Level at Pre-retrofit | Attics (Target RSI) | Attics (Max Grant) | Cathedral Ceilings & Flat Roofs (Target RSI) | Cathedral Ceilings & Flat Roofs (Max Grant) |
| :------------------------------- | :------------------ | :----------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ |
| No insulation                    | RSI 8.81 (R-50)     | **$1,800**         | RSI 3.52 (R-20)                              | **$600**                                    |
|  RSI 2.11 (R-12)                | RSI 8.81 (R-50)     | **$1,800**         | RSI 4.93 (R-28)                              | **$600**                                    |
| > RSI 2.11 (R-12) to RSI 4.40 (R-25) | RSI 8.81 (R-50)     | **$600**           | RSI 4.93 (R-28)                              | **$250**                                    |
| > RSI 4.40 (R-25) to RSI 6.16 (R-35) | RSI 8.81 (R-50)     | **$250**           | N/A                                          | **N/A**                                     |

#### Exterior Wall Insulation

*   **Eligibility Criteria:**
    *   Add insulation to at least **20% of your exterior wall area**, excluding foundation walls.
*   **Important Notes:**
    *   Grant amount is **pro-rated** based on the total percentage of walls insulated.
    *   For semi-detached or end-unit row houses, max grant is **75%** of listed amounts.
    *   For middle-unit row houses, max grant is **50%** of listed amounts.
    *   No grants for insulating walls between individual units.

| Percentage of Wall Area Insulated | From RSI 1.32 (R-7.5) to RSI 2.11 (R-12) | > RSI 2.11 (R-12) to RSI 3.52 (R-20) | > RSI 3.52 (R-20) |
| :-------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------- | :---------------- |
| **20%**                           | $660                                     | $760                                 | $1,000            |
| **100%**                          | $3,300                                   | $3,800                               | $5,000            |

#### Exposed Floor Insulation

*   **Eligibility Criteria:**
    *   Insulate the **entire exposed floor area** (minimum 11 square meters / 120 square feet).
    *   Includes overhangs and floors above unheated spaces (e.g., garages).
    *   **Crawl spaces are excluded** (see crawl space grants).

| Option                                   | Grant Amount |
| :--------------------------------------- | :----------- |
| Minimum additional insulation, RSI 3.52 (R-20) | **$350**     |

#### Basement Insulation

*   **Eligibility Criteria:**
    *   Add insulation to at least **20% of the total foundation wall area** (sum of basement walls, pony walls, crawl space walls).
    *   Seal and insulate a minimum **80% of your entire basement header area**.
    *   Seal and insulate a minimum **50% of your entire basement slab area**.
*   **Important Notes:**
    *   Combined grants for basement walls, headers, and slabs are possible.
    *   Pro-rated for semi-detached/row houses (75% or 50% of listed amounts).
    *   No grants for insulating walls/headers between adjacent units.
    *   If both basement and crawl space are present, grants are pro-rated to a **maximum of $1,500** based on total foundation wall area.

| Action                                                                   | Grant Amount |
| :----------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------- |
| Seal and insulate 80% of basement header area to increase by min. RSI 3.52 (R-20) | **$240**     |
| Seal and insulate 50% of basement slab by min. RSI 0.62 (R-3.5)          | **$400**     |

| Percentage of Wall Area Insulated | From RSI 1.76 (R-10) to RSI 3.87 (R-22) | > RSI 3.87 (R-22) |
| :-------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------- | :---------------- |
| **20%**                           | $210                                    | $300              |

#### Crawl Space Insulation

*   **Eligibility Criteria:**
    *   Add insulation to crawl space walls and headers, or ceiling.
    *   If combined with a basement, must insulate a minimum of **20% of the total foundation wall area**.
    *   If insulating both crawl space walls/headers and ceiling, only the **larger grant amount** is awarded.
*   **Important Notes:**
    *   Pro-rated for semi-detached/row houses (75% or 50% of listed amounts).
    *   If both basement and crawl space are present, grants are pro-rated to a **maximum of $1,500** based on total foundation wall area.

### Air Sealing

Improving airtightness is a crucial retrofit. The goal is to reduce the air-change rate of your home.

*   **Eligibility Criteria:**
    *   Meet the target specified in your **Renovation Upgrade Report**.
    *   Achieve better than the target (e.g., 10% or 20% better).
*   **Important Note:** *In some cases, improvements may require a balanced mechanical ventilation system (HRV/ERV) to address ventilation or radon levels.*

| Options                                                                                              | Grant Amount |
| :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------- |
| Meet the target in your Renovation Upgrade Report                                                    | **$550**     |
| If you reach **10% better** than the target (i.e., 0.9 x target value)                             | **$810**     |
| If you reach **20% better** than the target (i.e., 0.8 x target value)                             | **$1,000**   |

### Windows and Doors

Upgrading to high-performance ENERGY STAR certified windows and doors improves comfort and saves energy.

*   **Eligibility Criteria:**
    *   All equipment must be **purchased in Canada** (online purchases only eligible from Canadian distributors).
    *   Equipment must be on one of the eligible **ENERGY STAR product lists**.
    *   **Keep ENERGY STAR labels** on windows/doors until after post-retrofit evaluation.
*   **Important Notes:**
    *   Only a **completely new window or door and frame** inserted into an existing frame are eligible.
    *   Replacements of only glass, sash, or door without a frame are **not eligible**.
    *   Each rough opening is eligible for a grant of up to $250, with a **maximum of $5,000**.
