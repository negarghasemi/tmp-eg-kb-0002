# Energy Journey for Renters

This report provides tips and programs for renters to save energy and make their homes more comfortable.

## Energy Saving Tips and Programs

### Is your gaming system costing you?
*   You'd be surprised to learn how much energy your console uses when it's not in use.
*   **Action:** Power down after gameplay to save. *Just make sure to save your progress!*
*   **Tip:** Most gaming systems have a power saving mode that can help cut down on energy use. [Learn how to enable this feature for your console.](https://www.energystar.gov/products/configuring_todays_game_consoles_use_less_energy_0)

### Your fridge could be hiding energy savings

### Conserve energy in the kitchen!
*   You can decrease energy waste everyday by:
    *   Reducing your use of lights and appliances.
    *   Decluttering your fridge.
    *   Cutting excess oven preheating time.
*   **Tip:** [Try out no-bake recipes](/For-Your-Home/Advice-and-Tips/No-bake-strawberry-cheesecake-dessert-jars) to keep you energized and help save on your homes energy at the same time.

### Looking for low-income help? Check out the Energy Affordability Program
*   **Program Description:** Eligible low-income residents can take advantage of free home upgrades, such as energy-efficient shower heads, appliances, and weatherstripping.
*   [Find out more here](/For-Your-Home/Energy-Affordability-Program)
