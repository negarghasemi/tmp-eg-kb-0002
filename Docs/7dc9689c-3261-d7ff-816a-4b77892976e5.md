# Facts and Myths about Heat Pumps

Buildings are the third largest source of greenhouse gas emissions in Canada. Electrifying buildings, particularly with technologies like heat pumps, is crucial for meeting Canada's 2050 net-zero emissions targets.

Heat pumps offer significant benefits:
-   Increased comfort
-   Often lower utility costs
-   Access to efficient cooling, which is increasingly important due to climate change.

## Canadian Heat Pump Myth Buster
This initiative aims to correct misconceptions about heat pumps and provide scientific-based research to inform policy-makers, media, and the public about their true facts. [Read the blog](https://www.efficiencycanada.org/canadian-heat-pump-myth-buster/)

## What is a heat pump?
**A heat pump is a device that moves heat.** While an air conditioner absorbs heat from inside a house and rejects it outside, a heat pump can reverse this process to bring heat inside, effectively providing both cooling and heating.

### Efficiency Comparisons:
-   **Heat Pumps:** Can be **200-540% efficient**, providing 2 to 5.4 kWh of heat for every 1 kWh of electricity consumed. They concentrate and move more energy than they consume.
-   **Natural Gas Boilers/Furnaces (Canada):** Typically **82 to 98% efficient**.
-   **Electric Boilers/Baseboards:** Maximum **100% efficient**.
-   **Performance in Cold Climates:** Advanced heat pumps can operate effectively in very cold conditions, down to around -30 C.

## Role of Heat Pumps in Net-Zero Emissions
-   **Global Impact:** According to the IEA (2022), electrifying heating with heat pumps can reduce global GHG emissions by more than **500 million tonnes by 2030**.
-   **Canadian Context:** The Canadian Climate Institute's \"The Big Switch report\" projects that electricity's share of household energy consumption will increase from 23% in 2020 to 39% in 2035 and **96% in 2050** under a net-zero emissions scenario. This electrification, paired with renewable electricity, is key to achieving climate targets.
-   **Efficiency and Clean Electricity:** As renewable electricity becomes cheaper, heat pumps offer an excellent and highly efficient way to utilize clean electricity.
-   **GHG Savings Case Study (NRCan):** A NRCan CanmetEnergy report modeled GHG savings for an archetypical post-1980s two-storey home in sixteen Canadian cities when switching to a Cold Climate Air Source Heat Pump (CC-ASHP) from electric resistance, gas, or oil heating:
    -   **Fossil Fuel Replacement:** Reduced emissions by **more than 95%** in two-thirds of scenarios.
    -   **Electric Resistance Replacement:** Reduced emissions by **4977%**, due to significantly lower electricity consumption for the same heat output.
-   **MURB Retrofit Project (Mississauga, ON):** A retrofit project installing CC-ASHPs in a 12-storey apartment building resulted in a **46% reduction in heating energy consumption**.
