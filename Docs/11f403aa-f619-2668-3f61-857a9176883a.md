# Windows, doors and skylights - Energy Efficiency

## Property Energy Loss
*   **Windows, doors and skylights** can be a significant source of energy loss in your home  **up to 25%**.

## ENERGY STAR Certified Products
Choosing ENERGY STAR certified models can significantly reduce energy use.

| Product | Energy Efficiency Improvement (compared to average) |
| :------ | :------------------------------------------------ | 
| **Windows** | **20% more energy efficient**                     |
| **Doors**   | **15% more efficient**                            |
| **Skylights** | **35% more efficient**                            |

### ENERGY STAR Most Efficient
*   **ENERGY STAR Most Efficient windows and sliding glass doors** are the best of the best - **up to 55% more energy efficient** than average models.

## Importance of Installation
*   A poorly installed window, door or skylight may cause **condensation, cold drafts or even allow water to leak into your home**. The damage caused by water is often invisible until it becomes very costly to fix.
*   **Recommendation**: Choose a company that is in the **Window Wise** certification program. This program trains installers for correct window and door installation.
