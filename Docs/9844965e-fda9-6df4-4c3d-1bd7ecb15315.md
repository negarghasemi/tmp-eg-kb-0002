# Gas Clothes Dryers - Technical Bulletin

**Publication Date**: May 2024

## Overview
This technical bulletin from Natural Resources Canada (NRCan) outlines proposed amendments to the *Energy Efficiency Regulations* concerning household gas clothes dryers. The primary intention is to harmonize Canada's energy efficiency and testing standards with those of the United States Department of Energy (U.S. DOE).

**Webinar**: Tuesday, June 4, 1:00-2:00 pm EDT (for consultation)

## Regulatory Context
*   **Notice of Intent (April 2, 2022)**: NRCan announced its intention to harmonize energy efficiency and testing standards for five major home appliances, including household clothes dryers, with the U.S. DOE.
*   **Forward Regulatory Plan (April 2024)**: Updated to include household gas clothes dryers within the scope of amendments.
*   **NRCan's Consideration**: Harmonizing energy efficiency standards, testing standards, and manufacturing dates for household vented gas clothes dryers with the U.S. DOE's recent final rulemaking.
*   **Cost-Benefit Analysis**: NRCan will conduct an analysis using Canadian market data to assess economic and environmental impacts.
*   **Regulatory Proposal**: Expected to be pre-published in the *Canada Gazette*, Part I, in 2025.
*   **Memorandum of Understanding (2021)**: Signed between NRCan and U.S. DOE for cooperation on energy efficiency and testing standards.
*   **U.S. DOE Direct Final Rule (March 12, 2024)**: Pertains to energy efficiency standards for household clothes dryers, including vented gas models.
*   **U.S. DOE Final Rule (October 8, 2021)**: Updated testing standards for household clothes dryers.
*   **Scope Expansion**: The requirements under consideration would also apply to the clothes dryer component of integrated consumer clothes washer-dryers.

## Product Description
*   **Current Definition (Regulations)**: A household tumble-type clothes dryer that is electrically operated.
*   **Proposed Expanded Definition**: A **clothes dryer** means a household tumble-type clothes dryer with forced air circulation. The heat source is electricity or gas, and the drum and blower(s) are driven by an electric motor(s).
*   **Types**: Vented gas clothes dryers exhaust evaporated moisture, while ventless dryers use a closed-loop system.
*   **Size Categories (based on drum capacity)**:
    *   **Compact**: Less than 125 litres (4.4 cubic feet)
    *   **Standard**: 125 litres (4.4 cubic feet) or more

## Technical Standards

### Testing Standard
NRCan is considering referencing the following U.S. testing standard, using ambulatory incorporation by reference (as amended from time to time). This standard is already used for household electric clothes dryers.
*   **Appendix D2 to Subpart B, Part 430 of Title 10** to the U.S. Code of Federal Regulations, entitled *Uniform Test Method for Measuring the Energy Consumption of Clothes Dryers* (10 C.F.R. Appendix D2).

### Energy Efficiency Standard
NRCan intends to harmonize with the U.S. DOE's Direct Final Rule. The proposed minimum Combined Energy Factor (CEF) standards, tested according to 10 C.F.R. Appendix D2, are:
*   **1.58 kg/kWh (3.48 lb/kWh)** for vented gas clothes dryers with a capacity of **125 L (4.4 cubic feet) or more**.
*   **0.92 kg/kWh (2.02 lb/kWh)** for vented gas clothes dryers with a capacity of **less than 125 L (4.4 cubic feet)**.

## Important Dates
*   **In-force Date**: Six months after the date of publication in the *Canada Gazette*, Part II.
*   **Application Date for Standards**: For products manufactured on or after **March 1, 2028**.
*   **Regulatory Pathway**: Governor in Council amendment.
*   **Pre-publication**: In the *Canada Gazette*, Part I, in 2025.

## Requirements

### Labelling Requirements
*   No changes are considered for existing electric clothes dryers.
*   An **EnerGuide label** must be attached to every new household clothes dryer.
*   **New requirement**: Labelling would be required for household vented gas clothes dryers.
*   Size categories for labelling will remain in NRCans EnerGuide label guidelines (administrative change).

### Verification Requirements
*   No changes are considered for existing electric clothes dryers.
*   A **third-party verification mark** (from a Standards Council of Canada accredited certification body) will continue to be required.
*   **New requirement**: Verification mark would be required for household vented gas clothes dryers.

### Reporting Requirements
*   **Energy Efficiency Report**: For household gas clothes dryers, this report must be submitted by the dealer to NRCan *before* the product is imported into Canada or traded inter-provincially for the first time. It must include:
    *   **Capacity**, in L, of clothes dryers drum
    *   **Hourly BTU rating** of the burner
    *   **Combined Energy Factor** when tested according to Appendix D2 (CEFD2)
    *   **Annual energy consumption**
*   **Import Report**: Required for dealers importing household vented gas clothes dryers into Canada.
