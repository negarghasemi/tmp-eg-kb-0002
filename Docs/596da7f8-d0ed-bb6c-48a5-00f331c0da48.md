# Commercial EV Charging Rates

If you own or operate a commercial electric vehicle (EV) charging station, you may be eligible for the new **Electric Vehicle Charging (EVC) Rate**.

The EVC Rate is an electricity delivery rate designed specifically for eligible EV charging stations in Ontario. Its purpose is to better reflect the transmission costs of serving EV chargers with low load factors, thereby **reducing the Retail Transmission Service Rates (RTSRs)** paid by participating EV charging stations.

## Eligibility for the EVC Rate

EV charging stations must meet **all** the following criteria to participate in the EVC Rate:

*   Have at least **one Direct Current Fast Charger**
*   Ensure that at least **90% of total monthly peak demand is attributable to EV charging**
*   Have a **monthly peak demand between 50 kW and 4,999 kW**
*   Have a **12-month average load factor of 20% or less**
*   Must **not primarily serve commercial/public sector EV fleets**
*   Any distributed energy resource behind the accounts meter must **not exceed the stations annual peak demand**.

## Definition of \"Customer\" under the EVC Rate

A **\"customer\"** refers to the **owner or operator** of an EV charging station whose facilities are connected to, or are intended to be connected to, an electricity distributors distribution system. *It is important to note that a customer is not the end-user of the EV charging service, such as the driver of a vehicle that uses the EV charging station.*

## How the EVC Rate is Calculated

The EVC Rate works by **reducing the RTSRs** that participating EV charging stations would otherwise pay, as RTSRs are a component of the electricity bill.

*   Participating customers will pay **17% of the RTSR** that other customers in a distributors General Service 50 kW to 4,999 kW class would pay.
*   *Example: If a customers base RTSR is **$100/kW**, they would instead pay **$17/kW** under the EVC Rate.*

This rate structure is designed to better align the RTSRs paid by participating EV charging stations with the actual transmission system costs incurred to serve them.

## How to Apply for the EVC Rate

To apply, you must submit an [opt-in form](https://www.oakvillehydro.com/sites/oakvillehydro/files/2025-10/FORM%20OH%20EVC%20Rate%20Opt-In_2025-09-18.pdf) by email to **CustomerService@oakvillehydro.com**.
