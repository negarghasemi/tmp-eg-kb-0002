# Specification Changes for ENERGY STAR

## How and Why
As manufacturers develop more energy-efficient products, the **U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)** and international partners, such as **Natural Resources Canada**, routinely update the technical specifications required for **ENERGY STAR** qualification.

*   *These updates aim to raise the efficiency bar, leading to greater energy and cost savings.*

### Conditions for ENERGY STAR Specification Changes:

*   **Market penetration of ENERGY STAR qualified models increases significantly**
*   **Federal minimum energy performance standards change**
*   **New technology allows manufacturers to produce more energy-efficient products**
*   **Availability of the product is limited nationwide**
*   **Energy savings are less than expected**
*   **Performance or quality issues arise**
*   **Issues arise regarding test procedures**

---

*The ENERGY STAR name and symbol are administered and promoted in Canada by Natural Resources Canada and are registered in Canada by the United States Environmental Protection Agency.*
