# Storage tank water heatersEnerGuide

## The voluntary EnerGuide label

The EnerGuide label allows consumers to check the energy efficiency of storage tank water heaters they intend to buy or rent.

The label provides a sliding scale from lowest to highest, indicating which models are the most energy-efficient. Water heater manufacturers will place an EnerGuide label in their product literature.

### Electric storage tank water heaters

*   The energy efficiency is measured by **standby loss** that is indicated in **Watts**. The lower the number, the more efficient the model is.

**EnerGuide Label Components for Electric Storage Tank Water Heaters:**

1.  **Standby loss:** Indicated in Watts.
2.  **Range available:** For similar models (same type and similar capacity).
3.  **Indicator:** Positions the model compared with the most efficient and least efficient models in the same class.

### Gas/propane storage tank water heaters

*   The energy efficiency is measured by **uniform energy factor (UEF)**. The higher the number, the more efficient the model is.
*   For gas storage tank water heaters, the EnerGuide label may be beside the **ENERGY STAR** symbol for certified models.

**EnerGuide Label Components for Gas or Propane Storage Tank Water Heaters:**

1.  **Uniform energy factor (UEF):** Of the water heater model.
2.  **Models capacity:** Based on the first hour rating of the model.
3.  **Indicator:** Positions the model compared with more energy use and less energy use; the more to the right, the more efficient.

### What consumers should know about the new EnerGuide label for gas storage water heaters

*   The new UEF standard divides all water heater products into **4 different capacities**. Water heaters can now be compared by their application.

**Water Heater Capacity Applications (based on Uniform Energy Factor test method):**

| Capacity      | Example Application |
| :------------ | :------------------ |
| **Very Small**  | Hand sinks          |
| **Low**         | A small cottage     |
| **Medium**      | A small house       |
| **High**        | A house             |

*   **ENERGY STAR certified products** are typically medium and high capacity. Each capacity has a minimum UEF level.

**ENERGY STAR UEF Ratings:**

*   For **ENERGY STAR medium capacity**, the UEF is greater than or equal to **0.64**.
*   For **ENERGY STAR high capacity**, the UEF is greater than or equal to **0.68**.

### Tips on how to compare products using the new EnerGuide label

*   **Compare models of the same capacity.**
    *   *Example:* A Low capacity unit with a UEF rating of **0.70** is more energy efficient than a Low capacity unit with a UEF rating of **0.60**.
*   **The values of the old label (Energy Factor - EF) cannot be compared with the values of the new label (Uniform Energy Factor - UEF).**
    *   *Explanation:* The two rating metrics, EF and UEF, are not directly comparable to each other. Therefore, water heaters with these different labels cannot be compared for energy efficiency.
*   **Only compare models of similar capacities.**
    *   *Example:* A High capacity unit with a UEF rating of **0.70** is more energy efficient than a High capacity unit with a UEF rating of **0.60**.
*   **Models of different capacities cannot be compared.**
    *   *Example:* You cannot directly compare a Low capacity UEF rating of **0.60** with a Medium capacity UEF rating of **0.60**.
