## Energy Efficiency Tips for Your Home

### Optimize Dryer Usage

*   **Recommendation**: Regularly clean your dryer's lint trap.
*   **Benefits**:
    *   Ensures home safety by preventing your dryer from overheating.
    *   Improves dryer efficiency.
    *   Helps save energy all year round.
*   **Go Further**: If you live in a condo, you may have a second lint trap as part of your dryers exhaust system. Make sure to clean both lint traps regularly.

### Upgrade to Energy-Efficient Lighting

*   **Recommendation**: Choose energy-efficient lighting solutions for your home.
*   **Key Technology**: **ENERGY STAR certified LEDs**.
*   **Benefits of LEDs**:
    *   Use **70% to 90% less energy** than traditional incandescent bulbs.
    *   Can last up to **3.5 times longer**.
*   **Go Further**: Smart light bulbs allow you to control your lighting remotely through a smartphone or tablet. You can also use the corresponding app to change the brightness and colour of your lights to stay on top of your energy usage even when youre not at home.
