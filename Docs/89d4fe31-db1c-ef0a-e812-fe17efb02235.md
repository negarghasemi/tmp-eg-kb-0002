# Stream videos smarter

## Buy ENERGY STAR certified streaming equipment to save you money!

*ENERGY STAR Symbol*

* [Infographic on streaming media](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/energy-star/infographic-streaming-media)

Did you know that how you stream your media could impact your energy bill? ENERGY STAR can help you stream smarter with certified digital media players, smart TVs, laptops, and tablets. On average, **ENERGY STAR certified streaming devices use 25% less energy** than standard models.

### Benefits of using a digital media player (DMP)

DMPs provide an inexpensive, easy-to-use, quickly installed method to view content on your television. Plus, they save energy:

*   **DMPs use four times less energy than a cable box.**
*   **DMPs use 11 times less energy than a computer.**

### Helpful tips

*   **Use a tablet:** When streaming content to a small screen, consider using a tablet instead of a laptop or desktop. Laptops use **four times more energy**, while desktops use **15 times more energy** than a tablet.
*   **Smart TVs are a smart choice:** When streaming content to a big screen, a smart TV uses the least amount of energy compared to other streaming devices. Use an [ENERGY STAR certified smart TV](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/energy-star/products/list-certified-products/televisions) to save more.
*   **Use game consoles for gaming  not streaming:** Use your game console for gaming and leave streaming to other devices. A game console uses **15 times more energy than a DMP** and **four times more energy than a traditional cable/satellite set-top box** for streaming.
