# Technical standards documents

The `Energy Efficiency Act` provides the authority to incorporate by reference a technical standards document (TSD) in the `Energy Efficiency Regulations` when the purpose is to harmonize with another jurisdiction.

A TSD is a document that is published in both official languages by the Minister of Energy and Natural Resources. It adapts, combines or reproduces, in whole or in part, documents that are produced by jurisdictions, standards development organizations or industry associations and that, for energy-using products or classes of energy-using products, set out requirements or guidance related to those requirements. The adaptations may include modifications to the content of the originating document for the Canadian context.

### Contact Information

For further information on the regulatory process related to a TSD, you can visit the [Canada's Energy Efficiency Regulations - Regulatory process](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-regulations/canada-s-energy-efficiency-regulations) webpage. For any specific questions on a TSD, you can send an e-mail to **EEregulations-reglementEE@nrcan-rncan.gc.ca**.
