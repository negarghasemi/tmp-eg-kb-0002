# Home Efficiency Rebate Plus

## Program Update: Home Efficiency Rebate Plus

Enbridge Gas and the Canada Greener Homes Grant have partnered to provide up to $10,000 in rebates towards eligible retrofits such as home insulation, windows and doors, heat pumps and renewable energy systems.

### The new Home Renovation Savings Program
Enbridge Gas and Save on Energy, with support from the Ontario Government, have partnered to launch the new Home Renovation Savings program to help Ontarians improve home energy efficiency and comfort. Homeowners now have more choice, with flexible options to choose single or bundled upgrades for greater savings.

[Find out more about the Home Renovation Savings Program](https://www.homerenovationsavings.ca/)

The Home Efficiency Rebate Plus program has been a huge success, helping over 100,000 Ontario households reduce energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions.

**Important Program Deadlines:**
*   **December 1, 2025, 11:59 p.m. (ET)**: Deadline for all post-retrofit energy assessments to be completed.
*   **December 31, 2025**: All applications (including paperwork submitted by Registered Energy Advisors) must be submitted by this date. Requests for grant payments after this deadline will not be processed.
*   **February 5, 2024**: Intake of new applicants to the HER+ program closed. All pre-retrofit energy assessments must have been completed by this date.

*Please be aware of potential misinformation from companies unaffiliated with Enbridge Gas or Canada's Greener Homes Grant. Visit the [Enbridge Gas consumer protection page](/ontario/contact-us/scam-awareness) for scam prevention tips.*

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the program ending?
To qualify for the CGHG funding, all post-retrofit energy assessments must be completed by **December 1, 2025**. This allows your Registered Energy Advisor time to submit all paperwork, so your file is fully submitted by the **December 31, 2025**, deadline.

### Why am I still seeing ads promoting rebates for home upgrades?
Please check the [Enbridge Gas Rebates & Energy Conservation section](/ontario/rebates-energy-conservation) to learn about other energy saving programs such as [Home Renovation Savings](https://www.homerenovationsavings.ca/without-assessment/heat-pumps). Companies unaffiliated with Enbridge Gas or the Canada Greener Homes Grant may promote misinformation. Visit the [Enbridge Gas consumer protection page](/ontario/contact-us/scam-awareness) for tips to protect yourself from potential scams.

### Why is HER+ ending the program early?
To effectively manage the remaining grant funding available within the program, a deadline of **December 1, 2025**, has been established for all post-retrofit energy assessments to be completed. This provides Registered Energy Advisors until **December 31, 2025**, to submit all required paperwork. No exceptions for grant payments made after this deadline.

### Who do I contact if I have a question about my application?
Please reach out to your **Registered Energy Advisor** for any questions regarding assessments, eligible rebates, or if you believe something was missed in your application.

### I have already completed my pre and post-retrofit energy assessment, does this update impact me?
If both your pre and post-retrofit energy assessments are complete, you should receive your rebate as expected. If you completed your post-retrofit assessment more than three months ago and haven't received communication, contact your service organization.

### What if my post-retrofit energy assessment is scheduled after Dec 1, 2025?
HER+ applications are only accepted if the pre-retrofit energy assessments were completed between April 1, 2020, and February 5, 2024, and the post-retrofit energy assessment was completed between January 1, 2023, and **December 1, 2025**. No exceptions will be made for grant payments after this deadline. Book your post-retrofit assessment as soon as possible.

### How long before I receive my rebate?
You should receive your rebate cheque within **16 weeks** after your Registered Energy Advisor submits your application. Ensure your advisor has the correct mailing address and email for updates.

### Who do I contact if there are problems with the retrofit contractor I used for the HER+ program?
Any issues or concerns regarding retrofits or contractors must be addressed directly with the contractor. Enbridge Gas is not affiliated with, nor responsible for, referring, selecting, or contracting with these contractors.

### I received a letter saying my file has been opted in or out of CGHG/HER+, is this a scam?
Enbridge Gas and the Canada Greener Homes Grant are updating their databases. If you believe you were inaccurately opted out, contact your registered energy advisor or the Enbridge Energy Conservation Line at 1-844-777-3149.
