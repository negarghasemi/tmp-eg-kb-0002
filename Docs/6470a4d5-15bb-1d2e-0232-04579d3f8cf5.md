# Success stories: Oil to Heat Pump Affordability program

## Homeowners who switched from oil heating to heat pumps
Thousands of Canadian homeowners have made the switch from oil home heating to eligible electric heat pump systems with the help of funding provided by the [Oil to Heat Pump Affordability (OHPA) program](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/canada-greener-homes-initiative/oil-heat-pump-affordability-program).

On this page, youll find some of their stories and discover the financial savings, enhanced comfort and environmental benefits that came from switching to more energy-efficient home heating.

Explore how the OHPA program has transformed the heating and cooling efficiency for these homeowners.

### Video: Cheryl from Ontario
Cheryl replaced oil heating with a heat pump that keeps her family comfortable all year long while reducing heating costs.

*Transcript highlights:*
- **Motivation:** Old furnace and air conditioner (over 20 years old) needing full replacement. Grant information made the switch possible.
- **Financial Impact:** Received up to **$10,000** against her purchase. Oil heating would have cost approximately **$1,000 a month** during winter. With the heat pump, she pays approximately **$50 to $100 more in hydro**, saving the cost of fuel.
- **Comfort:** The heat pump keeps her home comfortably heated for her and her two children in winter and cool in summer, always on demand.
- **Process:** Very simple application process; received the grant check in her mailbox within a week, even before installation.
- **Homeowner Tip:** Examine options, contact a contractor to determine price and needs, consider whether you need heating, cooling, or both, the age of current equipment, and available warranties.

## Important extra tips:
*   **Confirm eligibility with your contractor**: Ensure the recommended heat pump is eligible. Your contractor **must include the Air-Conditioning, Heating and Refrigeration Institute (AHRI) number** along with the brand name and model numbers of the indoor and outdoor units in the quote(s).
*   **Verify eligible products**: You can check if the heat pump recommended by your contractor is eligible under the OHPA program by searching the [eligible product lists](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/canada-greener-homes-initiative/eligibility-criteria-oil-heat-pump-affordability-program#eligibleList).
*   **Contractor attestation**: Its mandatory that your contractor signs an [attestation form](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/sites/nrcan/files/energy/pdf/AttestationForm-Oil2HeatPump_e.pdf) after your heat pump is installed, confirming it can distribute heat throughout your entire home and is eligible under the OHPA program. Ensure your contractor agrees to sign this form before hiring them.
*   **Completion timeline**: Confirm that all work can be completed within **6 months** of receiving your grant payment.
*   **Ground source heat pumps**: For ground source heat pumps, the contractor must attest that the installation complies with the [CSA C448 standard (latest version)  Design and installation of ground source heat pump systems](https://www.csagroup.org/store/product/2701471/). You must submit this form after the work is complete.
