# Windows, doors and skylights  ENERGY STAR Canada technical specification

## ENERGY STAR certified residential windows, doors and skylights, sold in Canada

### Version 5.0  January 1, 2020 FINAL

This technical specification outlines the requirements for residential windows, doors, and skylights to be certified under the ENERGY STAR program in Canada. It is issued by Natural Resources Canada (NRCan), which is authorized by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) to administer the ENERGY STAR program in Canada. Products must meet this specification to be promoted as ENERGY STAR certified.

## 1) Definitions

### Product types

- **Window:** An assembled unit with a frame/sash and glazing for light/air, designed for vertical installation in an external residential wall.
- **Door:** An assembled unit with a frame and one or more moving panels for ingress/egress, designed for vertical installation between conditioned and unconditioned residential space.
- **Skylight:** A window for sloped or horizontal application in a residential roof, for daylighting and/or ventilation. Includes Tubular Daylighting Devices (TDDs).
- **Sliding door:** A door with one or more manually operated panels that slide horizontally within a common frame.
- **Swinging door:** A door with a hinge attachment, or a single, fixed vertical axis for rotation.
- **Sidelite:** A glazed frame or sash product installed on one or both sides of a door.
- **Transom:** A glazed frame or sash product installed above a door or window. Transoms > 700 mm (27 in.) in height are windows; those  700 mm (27 in.) are transoms.
- **Tubular daylighting device (TDD) or tubular skylight:** A non-operable device transmitting daylight from a roof to an interior ceiling via a tubular conduit. Considered a skylight.
- **Dynamic glazing product:** Any fenestration product with fully reversible ability to change performance properties (U-factor, SHGC, VT, ER). Includes shading systems between glazing layers and chromogenic glazing.
    - *Chromogenic glazing:* Changeable glazings that reversibly vary optical properties (e.g., electrochromic, photochromic).
    - *Internal shading system:* Operable blinds or shades positioned between glass panes.

### Performance metrics

- **U-Factor:** Heat transfer per time per area per degree of temperature difference in W/mK (Btu/h ftF). *A U-factor in Btu/h ftF multiplied by 5.678263 converts the value to W/mK.*
- **Solar heat gain coefficient (SHGC):** Ratio of solar heat gain entering a space through the product to the incident solar radiation.
- **Air leakage:** Flow of air through fenestration products in L/s/m. *An air leakage in cfm/ft multiplied by 5.08 converts the value to L/s/m.*
- **Energy rating (ER):** A unitless value balancing heat loss (U-factor), air leakage loss, and potential passive solar gain. Applied to vertical fenestration in low-rise residential buildings.
    - **ER formula:** `ER = (57.76  SHGCw)  (21.90  Uw)  (1.97  L75) + 40`
        - `SHGCw` = fenestration system solar heat gain coefficient
        - `Uw` = fenestration system U-factor (W/mK)
        - `L75` = fenestration system air leakage rate at 75 Pa (L/sm), average of infiltration and exfiltration.
- **Decimal precision:** Values are rounded using the arithmetic method prior to calculation and to the precision defined in Table 1.

| Value                      | Decimal precision |
| :------------------------- | :---------------- |
| Air leakage (infiltration)  | 1                 |
| Air leakage (exfiltration) | 1                 |
| L                        | 1                 |
| U-factor and SHGC          | 2                 |
| Energy Rating              | 0                 |

### Other definitions

- **Residential:** Products designed for installation in buildings meeting Part 9 of the National Building Code of Canada (three stories or less, < 600 m).
- **Insulating glass unit (IGU):** Preassembled unit of glass lites, sealed at edges, separated by dehydrated space(s).
- **North American Fenestration Standard (NAFS):** Common name for AAMA/WDMA/CSA 101/I.S.2/A440 testing standard.
- **Private labeller:** Company marketing products under its own name, manufactured by another company.
- **Standards Council of Canada (SCC):** Canadian federal Crown corporation accrediting standards organizations, test labs, and certification agencies.
- **U.S. National Fenestration Rating Council (NFRC):** U.S. organization developing energy performance standards and providing third-party verification for fenestration products.

## 2) Scope

- **Included products:** Residential windows, doors, or skylights as defined herein.
- **Excluded products:** Products assembled onsite (e.g., sash packs/kits); products for non-residential buildings; attachments not included in a products certified energy performance rating.

### Energy efficiency requirements

Window and door models must meet or exceed the requirements in Table 2 or Table 3. Skylights must meet or exceed the requirements in Table 4. Dynamic glazing products must meet criteria in the minimum tinted state (chromogenic) or fully open position (internal shading systems).

### Air leakage requirements

Fenestration models must have an air infiltration rate **and** an air exfiltration rate less than or equal to **1.5 L/s/m**.

## 4) Test and certification requirements

- **Energy performance certification:** Models shall be certified using:
    - *CSA A440.2-14 or CSA A440.2-19 Fenestration Energy Performance*
    - *ANSI/NFRC 100-14 or ANSI/NFRC 100-17 Procedure for Determining Fenestration Product U-factors*
    - *ANSI/NFRC 200-14 or ANSI/NFRC 200-17 Procedure for Determining Fenestration Product Solar Heat Gain Coefficient and Visible Transmittance at Normal Incidence*
- **Minimum requirements testing:** All models shall be tested to and meet the minimum requirements of:
    - *AAMA/WDMA/CSA 101/I.S.2/A440-08 North American Fenestration Standard/Specification for windows, doors, and skylights (NAFS)-08* and its Canadian supplement *CSA A440S1-09*
    - *AAMA/WDMA/CSA 101/I.S.2/A440-11 North American Fenestration Standard/Specification for windows, doors, and skylights (NAFS)-11* and its Canadian supplement *CSA A440S1-17*
    - *Note: Certification to NAFS and the Canadian supplement is not a requirement.*
- **Insulating Glass Units (IGU) certification:** IGUs shall be certified using:
    - *ASTM E2190-10 Standard Specification for Insulating Glass Unit Performance and Evaluation*
    - *CAN/CGSB 12.8-17 Insulating Glass Units Standard*
- **Model representation:** The model selected for testing/simulation must be representative of the product marketed as ENERGY STAR.
- **Accreditation for energy performance:** Certification must be done by an organization accredited by the **SCC** or by **NFRC**.
- **Accreditation for NAFS testing:** Testing must be done by an organization accredited by the **SCC** or other signatories to **APLAC** and/or **ILAC Mutual Recognition Arrangement**.

## 5) Model registration and labelling

- **Registration:** Product models must be registered with NRCan using the designated submission form.
- **Labelling:** Registered product models must be labelled in accordance with the *Requirements for the labelling and promotion of ENERGY STAR certified windows, doors and skylights sold in Canada*.

## 6) Installation instructions

Installation instructions must be readily available online or packaged with the product. Generic instructions covering common situations are acceptable. Disclaimers limiting liability are permitted.

## 7) Effective date

The effective date for Version 5.0 is **January 1, 2020**, superseding all previous versions. Models must meet the ENERGY STAR specification in effect on their date of manufacture.

## 8) Future criteria revisions

NRCan reserves the right to change the specification, with revisions arrived at through industry discussions. ENERGY STAR certification is not automatically granted for the life of a product model.
