# Home Renovation Savings Program

## No Energy Assessment Required Upgrades

Its now easier to get rebates for a heat pump, solar panels, attic insulation, and smart thermostats as a home energy assessment is not required.

### Featured Rebates
*   **Attic Insulation**: Up to **$1,250***
*   **Smart Thermostat**: **$100***
*   **Solar**: Up to **$10,000***
*   **Appliances**: Up to **$200***
*   **Heat pumps**: Up to **$12,000***

## Available Rebates

### Cold-climate air source or ground source heat pumps
*   **Rebate**: Up to **$12,000**, depending on your homes primary space heating energy source.
*   [See rebates](/without-assessment/heat-pumps)

### Solar panels plus battery storage
*   **Rebate**: Up to **$5,000** for solar panels, plus the option to bundle with battery storage for an additional rebate, up to **$5,000**.
*   [See rebates](/without-assessment/solar)

### Attic insulation
*   **Rebate**: Up to **$1,250** for attic insulation, or up to **$750** for cathedral ceiling or flat roof insulation.
*   [See rebates](/without-assessment/attic)

### Smart thermostats
*   **Rebate**: Two ways to get **$100** off an energy-saving smart thermostat: before you buy or after.
*   [See rebates](/without-assessment/smart-thermostat)

### Appliances
*   **Rebate**: Get up to **$200** in rebates for upgrading to qualified energy-efficient appliances.
*   [See rebates](/without-assessment/appliances)

## Saving energy made easier and more affordable

For heat pumps and attic insulation, work with a program-qualified contractor to have your upgrades installed and they will take care of the rebate paperwork. For solar panels, you will need to choose your own contractor, as there is no program-qualified list. For smart thermostats and appliances, you can easily purchase and install on your own.
