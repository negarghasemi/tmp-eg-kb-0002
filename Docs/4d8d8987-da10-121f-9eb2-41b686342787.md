# ENERGY STAR Certified Homes

ENERGY STAR is a trusted symbol for identifying energy-efficient products, including homes. Choosing an ENERGY STAR certified home means opting for a property built to a higher standard, offering significant benefits in terms of comfort, cost savings, environmental impact, and resale value.

## What is an ENERGY STAR Certified Home?

An ENERGY STAR home is:
-   **Constructed by an ENERGY STAR builder**
-   **Awarded a Government of Canada ENERGY STAR label**

## Benefits of an ENERGY STAR Certified Home

### Enjoy Better Indoor Living
-   **Enhanced Comfort**: Generally more air-sealed, reducing drafts and cold spots.
-   **Improved Air Quality**: Reduces dust, pollen, and pests. Features devices that exchange indoor stale air with filtered outdoor air.
-   **Balanced Temperatures**: Energy-efficient windows and higher insulation levels contribute to more consistent indoor temperatures, beneficial in both hot and cold seasons.

### Save Energy
-   **20% More Energy Efficient**: Built to be approximately 20% more energy efficient than a typical new home.
-   **Significant Savings**: Annually saves energy equivalent to watching **17 hockey seasons on TV** or running a refrigerator for **10 years**.
-   **Environmental Impact**: Homes account for 17% of Canada's energy consumption, making energy conservation crucial.

### Save Money
-   **Annual Utility Savings**: Homeowners can save up to **$300 per year** in utility costs. These savings are immediate and continuous, providing extra funds for other goals.

### Have Peace of Mind About Energy Efficiency
-   **Third-Party Verification**: Homes are evaluated, inspected, and labelled by a **third-party energy advisor**.
-   **High Standards**: Constructed by builders dedicated to higher energy-saving standards.
-   **Government Standard**: Meets the ENERGY STAR energy efficiency standard developed by **Natural Resources Canada**.
-   *Note: The ENERGY STAR standard is an enhancement to regional building codes, specifically designed to improve energy efficiency.*

### Help the Planet
-   **Reduced GHG Emissions**: By operating an ENERGY STAR certified home, you save over **1,000 kilograms of Greenhouse Gas (GHG)** emissions annually.
-   **Equivalent to Driving**: This saving is estimated to be about the same as the emissions from a **6,000 kilometre road trip** from Toronto to Calgary and back.
-   **Resource Conservation**: Lower energy consumption conserves natural resources and protects the environment.

### Appreciate the Resale Potential
-   **Increased Appeal**: Energy-efficient homes are attractive to potential buyers who prioritize comfort and cost savings.
-   **Desirable Features**: Features like high-performing windows and efficient heating/cooling systems are valuable assets during resale.

## Typical Features of an ENERGY STAR Certified Home

### Higher Insulation Levels
-   Walls and ceilings are insulated beyond building code requirements, ensuring more even temperatures throughout the home.

### Efficient Heating and Cooling Systems
-   These systems are designed to save energy and reduce utility costs.

### Additional Air-Sealing
-   Homes are built to be sufficiently airtight to meet specific energy targets, reducing drafts, cold spots, dust, pollen, pests, and noise.

### High Performing Windows, Doors and Skylights
-   ENERGY STAR certified windows, doors, and skylights save energy and enhance comfort, even near windows.

### A Whole-Home Ventilation System
-   An air-exchanger (such as a heat or energy recovery ventilation system) improves indoor air quality by expelling stale air and replacing it with filtered outdoor air.

### ENERGY STAR Certified Products
-   Incorporates various ENERGY STAR certified products (e.g., lighting, appliances, air conditioners) that use less electricity, leading to further savings.
