# Energy Journey for Condo Dwellers

## Energy Efficiency Tips

### **Lighting: Understanding Watts, Lumens, and Kelvins**
*   **Watts**: *Traditionally used for brightness, but less relevant for LEDs due to lower energy consumption.*
*   **Lumens**: **Measure of a bulbs brightness**. *Higher lumens indicate a brighter bulb.*
*   **Kelvins**: **Measure of colour temperature**. *Ranges from warmer, yellow-toned to cooler, soft white.*
*   **Recommendation**: When purchasing LED bulbs, focus on lumens and kelvins rather than watts to determine brightness and colour temperature.
*   [Learn more in our lighting guide.](https://www.saveonenergy.ca/For-Your-Home/Advice-and-Tips/Lighting-guide)

### **Preventing Drafts with Weather Stripping**
*   **Problem**: Cold drafts entering through cracks in windows and doors.
*   **Solution**: Use **weather stripping** on windows and doors to seal these cracks.
*   **Benefits**: Improves airtightness, reduces energy costs, and enhances home comfort.

### **Natural Humidity Control with Houseplants**
*   **Concept**: Certain indoor plants can naturally add humidity to the air.
*   **Recommended Plants**: Peace lilies, Boston ferns, English ivy, and desert succulents (e.g., aloe).
*   **Benefit**: Can reduce the need for an electric humidifier during drier winter months.
*   [Learn more about how you can balance humidity at home.](https://www.saveonenergy.ca/For-Your-Home/Advice-and-Tips/How-to-manage-humidity-at-home)

### **Efficient Gaming Console Usage**
*   **Issue**: Gaming consoles consume energy even in sleep mode, a phenomenon known as **phantom power**.
*   **Recommendation**: Enable your console's energy-saving mode to reduce unnecessary energy use.
*   [Learn how to configure energy-saving mode for gaming consoles.](https://www.energystar.gov/products/configuring_todays_game_consoles_use_less_energy_0)
