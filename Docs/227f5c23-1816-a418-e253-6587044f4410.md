# R-2000: Environmentally Friendly Homes

R-2000 certified homes are among the **most energy-efficient** available, offering significant benefits in terms of energy savings, comfort, and environmental protection.

## Why Choose an R-2000 Home?

R-2000 is a **voluntary housing standard** developed by Natural Resources Canada (NRCan) over 35 years ago, continuously monitored and improved. It establishes benchmarks for high energy efficiency, clean-air features, and environmental considerations in home construction.

### Key Benefits:

*   **Energy Savings**: Reduce heating and cooling costs.
*   **Healthier Indoor Air**: Improved air quality and moisture control.
*   **Enhanced Comfort**: Consistent temperatures and reduced drafts.
*   **Environmental Protection**: Lower greenhouse gas emissions and support for recycled products.
*   **Long-term Investment**: Though initial costs may be higher, R-2000 homes offer a return on investment through energy savings.

## The Updated R-2000 Standard

The R-2000 Standard was significantly updated in **July 2012**. Homes built to this new standard are, on average, **50% more energy-efficient** than typical new homes constructed to code. This update addresses unpredictable fuel costs, the need to reduce greenhouse gases, and the growing importance of indoor air quality, especially in Canada's diverse climate.

## Construction and Certification

R-2000 homes are built using **leading-edge techniques** by innovative builders.

### Typical Features of an R-2000 Home:

*   **High insulation levels** in walls, ceilings, and basements.
*   **High-efficiency windows and doors**.
*   **High-efficiency heating and cooling systems**.
*   **Balanced, whole-house mechanical ventilation system** for consistent fresh air.
*   **Minimal air leakage** from the house.
*   **Water-conserving fixtures** (e.g., taps, showers, toilets).
*   *Optional*: Use of less toxic and environmentally impactful building materials.

### Certification Process:

Every R-2000 home undergoes a rigorous process to ensure compliance:

*   **Constructed by trained builders** who hold an R-2000 license from NRCan and complete refresher courses.
*   **Evaluated, inspected, and tested by an independent third-party energy advisor**.
*   **Certified by the Government of Canada**.

## R-2000 Certificate and Label

Upon certification, homeowners receive an **R-2000 certificate** and a **label** affixed to the electrical panel.

### R-2000 Certificate Information:

*   **Municipal address**
*   **Postal code**
*   **Lot number or legal description**
*   **Plan number or legal description**
*   **Certificate Number**
*   **Date and signature**

### R-2000 Label Statement:

_\"This home meets the R-2000 Standard. For further details, please refer to the R-2000 Home Certificate or contact your local R-2000 office or Natural Resources Canadas Office of Energy Efficiency at 1 800 387-2000 (toll-free). Please consult your R-2000 Homeowners Manual and ensure that you are familiar with how to operate and maintain your R-2000 home.\"_

(A space is provided for the home's certificate number.)

## Detailed Benefits of Choosing an R-2000 Home

### Energy Savings

*   R-2000 homes are designed for **optimal energy efficiency** based on local climate and homeowner lifestyle.
*   The combination of **high insulation** and an **airtight building envelope** directly translates into significant energy bill reductions.
*   *Actual cost savings* vary based on local fuel costs, household size, and lifestyle habits.

### Health Benefits

*   R-2000 homes set a high standard for **indoor air quality**.
*   Features include a **balanced, whole-house mechanical ventilation system** that removes stale air and supplies fresh air to every room.
*   Options for using **green-certified carpets, water-based paints/varnishes, and air filtration systems** further enhance air quality.
*   Designed to maintain **optimal indoor moisture levels** to prevent mold growth.
*   **High-efficiency sealed-combustion heating systems** prevent dangerous combustion products from entering the home.

### More Comfort

*   Certified airtightness, high-quality windows, and well-insulated walls eliminate drafts and maintain **stable indoor temperatures**.
*   Homes are designed to **maximize passive solar gain** in winter, and energy-efficient windows allow more natural light without increasing heating costs.

### Environmental Benefits

*   **Lower Greenhouse Gases**: By using significantly less energy for heating and cooling, R-2000 homes reduce the burning of fossil fuels, thereby lowering carbon dioxide (CO2) emissions and contributing to Canada's climate change goals. The average new home in Canada releases five tonnes of CO2 annually.
*   **Market for Recycled Products**: Many R-2000 homes incorporate advanced building products made from recycled materials such as drywall, wood/steel studs, insulation, roofing, concrete, and carpeting, supporting recycling efforts.
*   **Reduced Water Use**: Water-saving fixtures decrease municipal water purification and sewage treatment needs, promoting environmental sustainability and reducing utility costs.

## R-2000 vs. ENERGY STAR

*   **ENERGY STAR certified new homes** are, on average, **20% more energy-efficient** than homes built to code.
*   **R-2000 certified homes** are considered **best-in-class** and offer *higher levels of energy efficiency* than ENERGY STAR homes, along with additional clean air and environmental features.

## Buying or Building an R-2000 Home

To buy or build an R-2000 home, the first step is to **find a licensed R-2000 builder**. Look for the R-2000 mark when shopping, and verify that your builder is NRCan R-2000-licensed, as only homes built by such builders can be R-2000 certified.

### Cost Considerations

An R-2000 home will typically have a **higher upfront cost**. However, this investment leads to **immediate savings on heating and cooling bills** and provides a **long-term return on investment** through reduced operational costs and increased home value.
