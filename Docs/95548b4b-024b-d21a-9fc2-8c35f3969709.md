# Types of Ground-Source Heat Pump

There are three main types of ground-source heat pumps:

## Open Loop
*   This system utilizes a **lake, river, or other body of water** for heat exchange.
*   The heat pump **draws water directly** to a heat exchanger where heat is extracted.
*   The water is then **discharged** either to an above-ground body of water or back underground through a well.

## Closed Loop
*   Most closed-loop pumps circulate an **antifreeze solution** through a closed loop.
*   The loop is typically made of **plastic tubing** and is buried in the ground or submerged in water.
*   A **heat exchanger** facilitates heat transfer between the heat pump's refrigerant and the antifreeze solution.
*   The loop can be configured in **horizontal, vertical, or pond/lake arrangements**.

## Direct Exchange
*   In this system, a **refrigerant circulates** directly through a continuous loop of **copper tubing** placed in the ground.
*   It requires a **shorter and smaller set of buried tubing**, which can lead to **reduced installation costs**.
