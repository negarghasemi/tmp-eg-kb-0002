# Customer Choice - Q & A

## What is Customer Choice?
If youre a **residential or small business electricity customer**, you can choose to switch between **Time-of-Use (TOU), Ultra-Low Overnight (ULO)** and **Tiered prices**.

## What do electricity plan rates pay for?
*   The **Ontario Energy Board (OEB)** sets electricity plan prices based on a forecast of the cost to supply electricity for the next 12 months.
*   The **Regulated Price Plan (RPP)** is designed to provide stable pricing, encourage conservation, and ensure prices reflect generation costs.
*   **Electricity utilities are not allowed to make a profit** from the sale of electricity.

## Will I save money by opting out of a price plan?
*   There is **no guarantee you will save money** regardless of the electricity price plan selected.
*   The total bill impact depends on **how much electricity you consume** in a month and **when it is consumed** during the day.
*   *Recommendation*: Use the OEB bill calculator at [www.oeb.ca/choice](http://www.oeb.ca/choice) to compare potential bill impacts.

## What do I need to do to switch price plans?
*   **No action is required** if you wish to remain on your current price plan.
*   **To switch**: 
    *   **Fill out an election form**: Available on the Oakville Hydro website or upon request. You will need your utility account number (found on a recent electricity bill).
    *   **Submit the form**: Email the completed form to [customerservice@oakvillehydro.com](mailto:customerservice@oakvillehydro.com) or mail it.
    *   **Online option**: Log into MyOakvilleHydro, click Choose your Electricity Price Plan', then 'Switch Price Plan Now' and follow the prompts.
*   **Processing Timeframes**:
    *   **Within 10 business days**: Oakville Hydro will notify you if your form cannot be processed and explain why.
    *   **Within 10 business days**: If no issues, Oakville Hydro will inform you when your new price plan will begin.
*   **Effective Date of Switch**:
    *   A switch can only take effect at the **start of a billing period** (approximately 30 days long).
    *   If the form is received at least **10 business days before the start of your next billing period**, the switch will occur then (assuming no issues).
    *   If the form is received **less than 10 business days before the start of your next billing period**, the switch might still occur for that period, or it will be applied at the start of the *following* billing period.
    *   *Note*: There will be a time lag between submitting the form and the new plan taking effect.

## If I have opted into Tiered prices, can I switch back to TOU prices or switch to ULO prices?
**Yes**, you can switch between these price plans.

## Can all customers opt out of TOU prices?
*   Any customer currently on **TOU prices may opt out and switch to Tiered prices**.
*   If you were on Tiered prices prior to November 1, 2024, because your meter could not support TOU, you **cannot switch to TOU prices** at this time.
*   **Unit Sub-Meter Provider (USMP) customers** (e.g., in condos/apartments where bills come from a company other than the electricity utility) cannot individually switch to Tiered prices. This decision is made by the **master consumer** (property manager, landlord, or condominium board) for the entire building.

## I receive a monthly credit from the Ontario Electricity Support Program (OESP). Will I lose that credit if I switch to Tiered prices?
**No**, you will continue to receive the OESP credit.

## I receive the Ontario Electricity Rebate. Will I lose that rebate if I switch to Tiered prices?
**No**, you will continue to receive the Ontario Electricity Rebate.

## I am about to sign up with a new electricity utility. Will the utility inform me that I have a choice between TOU, ULO or Tiered prices before I sign up?
**Yes**, all electricity utilities in Ontario are required to inform new Regulated Price Plan customers about their choice between TOU, ULO, or Tiered prices when setting up an account.
