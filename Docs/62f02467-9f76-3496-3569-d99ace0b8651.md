# Residential Electric Cooking Products

ENERGY STAR certified electric cooking products are **up to 10% more efficient** than standard models.

## Benefits of Electric Kitchen Appliances

Electric cooking appliances do not burn gas, which releases pollutants into the air, thus contributing to better air quality in your home. Cooking with gas can release hazardous gases such as:
-   **Carbon monoxide**
-   **Nitrogen oxides**
-   **Formaldehyde**
-   **Other particulate matter**

Exposure to these pollutants is linked to health disorders and adverse environmental effects.

## Advantages of Induction Cooking

Induction cooktops and ranges heat cookware internally using electromagnetic energy, leading to a more efficient heating process than traditional methods. Key advantages include:
-   **Quicker heat-up times**
-   **Better precision temperature control** compared to gas, radiant, or coil cooking.
-   The cooking surface does not become hot to the touch without a pot or pan in place.

_Note: Not all cookware is compatible with induction. Pots and pans must be made of a ferromagnetic material (like cast-iron or steel) or have a magnetic layer on the bottom._

## Why ENERGY STAR Certified Electric Cooking Products Are Right for You

ENERGY STAR certified electric cooking products offer various styles, sizes, and functionalities. Here are some features to consider:

### Functionality
-   **Slide-in range**: Sits flush between existing countertops.
-   **Freestanding range**: Can occupy a separate space.
-   **Cooktop**: Fits into your countertop.
-   **Combination sets**: Some products are sold as matching cooktop and oven combinations, allowing separation.

### Range Width and Cooking Zones
-   Most cooking tops are **between 30 to 36 inches in width**, with other sizes available.
-   **Concentric cooking zones** accommodate different sized cookware and can have bridging elements for larger items like griddles.
-   Some models offer **specialty zones** for simmering or power heating.

### Additional Product Features
-   **Smart features**: Connect to mobile apps or other devices.
-   **Cookware sensors**: Generate heat based on cookware size and location.
-   **Control locks**
-   **Indicator lights** for active heating and hot surfaces.

### Capacity
-   Ovens included in electric ranges typically have a capacity of **5-6 cubic feet**.

## The ENERGY STAR Promise

All ENERGY STAR certified products are tested to meet strict efficiency specifications and are certified by an independent third party. They perform the same as or better than standard products without compromising performance.

**Tip for choosing an energy efficient electric cooking product:**
-   When comparing similar models, check the **EnerGuide label** to understand annual and lifetime energy consumption. The lower the number, the better.

## Regulations Set the Energy Efficiency Minimum

Electric ranges are subject to **Canada's Energy Efficiency Regulations**, which establish a minimum performance standard for energy efficiency. Details can be found in the Guide to the Regulations.
