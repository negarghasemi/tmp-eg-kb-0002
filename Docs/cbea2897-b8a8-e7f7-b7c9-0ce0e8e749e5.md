# Sterling and Colin: A Pet-Of-View on Energy Efficiency

## Have fun and save energy at the same time!
Being energy efficient can be an enjoyable experience, leading to long-term savings. Engaging in simple yet entertaining activities can contribute to this goal. Unplugging from electronics is not only beneficial for your energy bills but also for your health, encouraging outdoor activities and community exploration.

## Making small changes to your routines can also help you save on energy
Optimizing laundry habits can significantly reduce energy consumption.

*   **Off-peak hours**: Do laundry in the evening or overnight when electricity costs are lowest.
*   **Dryer usage**: Dryers can account for **6 per cent** of a homes total energy use. Consider hanging clothes to dry on a rack or drying line, even indoors during colder weather.
*   **Cold water wash**: Washing in cold water reduces the energy used for heating water. Utilize the cold water settings on your washing machine. If warm or hot water is necessary, set the cycle to wash in warm/hot water and rinse in cold.

## Ways to zap energy-sucking phantom power from your home
Electronic devices consume energy even when not actively in use if they remain plugged in. This phenomenon, known as **phantom power,** can impact your energy bills.

*   **Impact**: **75 per cent** of the electricity used by devices occurs when they are not even on. Phantom power can increase a home's average energy costs by **up to 10 per cent**.
*   **Examples of devices**: Laptops, gaming consoles, TVs, phones, and tablets.
*   **Recommendations to combat phantom power**:
    *   Unplug devices when not in use.
    *   Use an electricity usage monitor device to check power consumption.
    *   Invest in smart or programmable power bars with built-in timers, shut-off capabilities, motion sensors, and infrared (IR) sensors that automatically cut power to inactive devices.
