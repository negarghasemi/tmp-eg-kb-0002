# Single package heat pumps

## Energy Efficiency Regulations

### Regulatory definition

**Single package central heat pump**, a single-phase or three-phase air-to-air central heat pump that is a centrally ducted single package unit and has a cooling or heating capacity of less than 19 kW (65,000 Btu/h). It does not include a single package vertical heat pump.

### Compliance date

*   **Manufactured on or after January 1, 2025**, for single package central heat pumps (single-phase)
*   **Manufactured on or after January 1, 2026**, for single package central heat pumps (three-phase)

_For models manufactured prior, refer to the Regulations_

### Compliant product models

*   [Single package heat pumps, single-phase](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=HP.SinglePackageSingle) as listed on NRCans searchable database
*   [Single package heat pumps, three-phase](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=HP.SinglePackageThree) as listed on NRCans searchable database

### Testing standard

*   **10 C.F.R. Appendix M1** for single-phase models
*   **10 C.F.R. Appendix F1**, except that the very low temperature heating test (H4) must be conducted.

_**Energy efficiency regulations by province:** Some provinces also have their own energy efficiency regulations for many of the federally regulated products  [find out which ones!](/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-regulations/energy-products-regulated-federally-provincially)_

### Energy efficiency standard

#### Energy performance standard for single package central air conditioners and single package central heat pumps under 19 kW (65,000 Btu/h)

**Single Phase**

| Energy-using product                                     | Energy performance standard                        |
| :------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **Single package central heat pumps, other than those that are space-constrained** | SEER 2  13.4<br><br>HSPF 2 (Region V)  5.4<br><br>Off mode power consumption  33 W |
| **Single package central heat pumps that are space-constrained** | SEER 2  11.9<br><br>HSPF 2 (Region V)  5.0 <br><br>Off mode power consumption  33 W |

**Three-phase**

| Energy-using product                                     | Energy performance standard |
| :------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------- |
| **Single package central heat pumps, other than those that are space-constrained** | SEER  13.4<br><br>HSPF (Region V)  5.3 |
| **Single package central heat pumps that are space-constrained** | SEER  13.9<br><br>HSPF (Region V)  5.3 |

*   **Seasonal energy efficiency ratio (2)**  _the total cooling of a heat pump in Btu during its normal annual usage period for cooling, divided by the electric power usage in watt-hours during the same period in accordance with 10 C.F.R. Appendix M1/10 C.F.R. Appendix F1._
*   **Heating seasonal performance factor (2) (Region V)**  _the total heating output of a heat pump during its normal annual usage period for heating, divided by the total electric power input in watt-hours during the same time period in accordance with 10 C.F.R. Appendix M1/10 C.F.R. Appendix F1._

*   **kW** = kilowatts
*   **Btu/h** = British thermal units per hour

### Energy efficiency report requirements

*   name of product
*   brand name
*   model number
*   manufacturer
*   name of the certification body or province whose verification mark will be on the product or its package
*   whether a mathematical model as defined in the Regulations was used to generate any of the information provided below
*   Air-Conditioning, Heating and Refrigeration Institute (AHRI) classification type
*   type
    *   single package, other than space-constrained, or
    *   space-constrained single package
*   off mode power consumption, in watts
*   phase of electric current
*   cooling capacity in kW (Btu/h)
*   heating capacity at 8.3 (47) in kW (Btu/h)
*   seasonal energy efficiency ratio (SEER 2)
*   heating seasonal performance factor (HSPF 2) and the region for the factor
