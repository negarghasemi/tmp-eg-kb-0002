# EnerGuide Energy Efficiency Home Evaluations

*An energy-efficient home is comfortable, healthy, environmentally friendly and cost-effective. An EnerGuide home evaluation can help you understand how your home uses energy now  and identify retrofits to help improve energy efficiency.*

An energy advisor will assess your home from basement to attic. This will give you an **EnerGuide rating** for your home and an **energy efficiency report** to help you make decisions about possible upgrades.

## What to Expect from Your Home Evaluation

After you book your appointment, the service organization may contact you with a pre-evaluation phone call or email.

Key steps of the evaluation:

- The **EnerGuide evaluation** is carried out by an **energy advisor** registered with Natural Resources Canada.
- Before the energy advisor arrives, if you have a wood-burning appliance, **remove any ashes** from the fireplace/appliance. Also, **clear access** to any hard-to-reach areas indicated by the service organization.
- The energy advisor will present a **homeowner consent form**, the *Notice to Homeowner*, which you must read, understand, and sign to authorize the evaluation.
- The energy advisor will review the **scope, intent, and approximate duration** of the evaluation, along with the **data collection process**. All data and photos are protected by privacy legislation. Photos will be taken of the home's exterior, mechanical systems, attic space, and any wood-burning appliances for record-keeping and quality assurance. One photo of the front of the house will appear in your customized report.
- During the evaluation, you can discuss any **plans and priorities** for energy efficiency upgrades.
- The energy advisor will explain the tools used, including the **blower door testing equipment** used to determine the **airtightness** of your home.
- The evaluation typically starts at the exterior and moves to the interior. The energy advisor will measure and collect data on elements impacting your home's energy performance, which is then entered into an energy simulation software program to calculate your homes **EnerGuide rating**. Data collected includes:
    - The level of your homes **airtightness**, using a **blower door test**.
    - The **insulation levels** of your walls, ceilings, and basement.
    - The **number, type, and location** of all windows and exterior doors.
    - The **size and efficiency ratings** of your space heating, space cooling (air conditioning), and water heating equipment.
    - Information about any **ventilation equipment**.
    - Any other information relevant to your homes energy performance.
- The energy advisor will confirm locations of attic, basement/crawl space accesses, mechanical equipment, etc. You may be asked to assist in providing safe and unobstructed access.
- If you have fuel-fired space heating or water heating systems, these will need to be **turned down or off** during the blower door test to avoid safety issues. All equipment will be returned to pre-evaluation settings afterward.
- **Do not use fuel-fired or wood-fired appliances** during the blower door test.
- After the evaluation, the energy advisor may discuss program information and survey requests.
- Required items to prepare in advance:
    - Your **municipal property tax roll number** to confirm ownership.
    - If applicable, **paid invoices and receipts** for energy efficiency upgrades (for rebate/incentive programs requiring post-retrofit evaluation).

## Prepare Your Home for the Home Evaluation

For an energy advisor to perform an EnerGuide evaluation, your home must meet the following conditions:

- **Access**: Provide a **clear path** to all attic spaces, crawl spaces, mechanical equipment, and other hard-to-reach areas.
- **Heating**: Your home must have a space heating system capable of maintaining the interior living space at **22 degrees Celsius**.
- **Windows and doors**: All windows and doors must be in place. A maximum of one missing window or door is allowed if it is **temporarily covered and air-sealed**. Broken window panes must be air-sealed. All windows and doors must be properly closed for an accurate test.
- **Envelope**: The building envelope (ceilings below roof/attic, exterior walls, exposed floors, windows, doors) must be **intact**, including interior and exterior surfaces. No renovations should break the building envelope. If the exterior finish is not intact, the home cannot be evaluated.
- **Foundation**: Your home must rest on its **permanent foundation** or be a permanently moored float home.
- **Power**: The blower door test equipment requires a supply of **standard AC electrical power**. If not available from a utility, you must arrange for it and inform the service organization.
- **Water and sewer**: Potable water must be supplied by the municipality, private wells, or cistern systems. Sewer service must be provided by the municipality, a private septic system, or a holding tank (outhouses and bio-toilets are permissible).
- **Operating kitchen**: Kitchen space must include a **sink, an operating stove**, and a minimum of **six square feet (0.5 square metres) of counter space** (or evidence of future intent to install a permanent counter).
- **Wood-burning appliances**: **Remove all ashes** from any wood-burning appliances.

## The Day of Your Evaluation

- **Do not use fuel-fired space heating or water heating systems**, or any **wood-fired or other solid-fuel fired appliances**, as the blower door test could affect safe operation. You can return all equipment to normal settings after the test.
- Read and sign the **Notice to Homeowner consent form**.
- The energy advisor will explain the evaluation's scope, length, purpose, and data-collection process, including taking photos of your homes exterior, mechanical systems, attic space, and any wood-burning appliances. All data and photos are protected by privacy legislation.
- The data collected includes:
    - The level of your homes **airtightness**, using a **blower door test**.
    - The **insulation levels** of your walls, ceilings, and basement.
    - The **number, type, and location** of all windows and exterior doors.
    - The **size and efficiency ratings** of your space heating, space cooling, and water heating equipment.
    - Information about any **ventilation equipment**.
    - Any other information relevant to your homes energy performance.
- After the evaluation, the advisor will confirm information and may discuss related program details or survey participation.
