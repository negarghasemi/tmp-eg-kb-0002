# Energy Efficiency Report: Home Energy Saving Tips

## Simple Ways to Keep Your Home Warm, Cozy, and Energy-Efficient This Winter

To help keep your home warm and cozy while managing energy consumption during colder months, consider the following simple yet effective measures:

*   **Weather Stripping:** Apply weather stripping to your homes windows and doors. This is an **easy and cost-effective way** to reduce unwanted drafts, minimize heat loss, and ultimately **lower your energy bills**.
    *   *Further Information: [Weatherstripping](https://www.saveonenergy.ca/For-Your-Home/Advice-and-Tips/Weatherstripping)*
*   **Utilize Sunlight:** On sunny days, open the blinds and curtains on your windows. The natural sunshine will help warm your rooms throughout the day.
*   **Go Further: Energy-Efficient Windows:** Installing energy-efficient windows can significantly reduce your energy costs and maintain home comfort all year long.
    *   *Further Information: [Window Buying Guide](https://www.saveonenergy.ca/For-Your-Home/Advice-and-Tips/Windows-buying-guide)*

## Save Time and Energy by Using Your Dishwasher

Dishwashers are often more energy-efficient than handwashing, offering significant savings in water and energy.

*   **Water Consumption Comparison:**
    *   Most dishwashers use **fewer than 15 litres of water** for a full cycle.
    *   Handwashing dishes uses roughly **15 litres every two minutes**.
*   **ENERGY STAR Dishwashers:** These appliances can save an average of **26,400 litres of water a year**  which is equivalent to almost 87 standard bathtubs full of water!
*   **Usage Tips:**
    *   Load your dishwasher with all your plates, bowls, cutlery, and even pots and pans.
    *   Running your dishwasher with less than a full load can still lead to energy and water savings.
*   **Go Further: Optimize Dishwasher Settings:** Take advantage of your dishwashers settings. Many models offer options based on how dirty the dishes are, adjusting water and heat usage. Options like **no-heat air drying** can help you save even more energy.
    *   *Test your knowledge: [Dishwasher Quiz](https://www.saveonenergy.ca/For-Your-Home/Advice-and-Tips/Dishwasher-Quiz)*
