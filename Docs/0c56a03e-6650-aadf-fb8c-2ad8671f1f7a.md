# Buying and Operating Tips for Cooking Appliances

## What should you consider when buying a cooking appliance?

### Buying tips

*   **Read the EnerGuide label:** Choose a model with the **lowest energy consumption rating**. _(This indicates higher energy efficiency.)_
*   **Look for a self-cleaning oven:** These models are generally **better insulated** and maintain internal temperature more effectively, despite using heat for cleaning.
*   **Ask about ovens with a convection setting:** They can **reduce baking time by up to 30 percent**, saving energy.
*   **Consider an oven with probes for measuring internal temperatures:** These prevent overcooking and **reduce the need to open the door**, which wastes energy.
*   **Choose an induction or halogen (ceramic glass) cooktop:** This type is **more energy efficient** than conventional burners.
*   **Purchase the right size oven:** A **smaller model will use less energy**.

### Operating tips

Follow these best practices for even more energy savings:

*   **Read the owner's manual:** It provides specific tips for operating your model as efficiently as possible.
*   **Preheat the oven only for baking:** In other cases, preheating is a waste of energy.
*   **Turn off the oven a few minutes early:** The remaining heat will finish cooking the food.
*   **Keep the oven door shut whenever possible:** Every time it's opened, **as much as 20 percent of the heat escapes**.
*   **Use the right size cookware:** A pot should just cover the cooking ring for **maximum efficiency**.
*   **Use the right type of cookware:** For example, aluminum pans are unsuitable for halogen induction cooktops.
*   **Use only smooth, flat-bottomed cookware:** Food cooks faster when pots make full contact with the heating element.
*   **Use minimal heat:** Once water is boiling, use the **lowest setting required** to maintain the boil.
*   **Keep drip pans clean under conventional burners:** Avoid lining them with aluminum foil, as foil reflects heat that can damage the element.
*   **Keep oven door seals clean and tight:** Replace seals if a sheet of paper slides around or light can be seen through them, as this indicates heat loss. _(This also applies to fridge and freezer doors.)_
