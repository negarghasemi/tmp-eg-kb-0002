# Canada Greener Homes Initiative

The Canada Greener Homes Initiative aims to help homeowners make their homes more energy-efficient, save money, create jobs, and fight climate change.

## Latest News

*   [Canada Greener Homes Initiative: Highlights and program updates](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/canada-greener-homes-initiative/canada-greener-homes-initiative-highlights-program-updates)

## Programs to Support Canadians

### Canada Greener Homes Affordability Program

*   This program, delivered through partners in provinces and territories, helps **low-to-median-income homeowners and tenants** retrofit their homes to reduce monthly energy bills.
*   **The full cost of recommended retrofits will be covered** for eligible households, meaning participants will not pay out of pocket.
*   [Learn more about the Canada Greener Homes Affordability Program](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/canada-greener-homes-initiative/canada-greener-homes-affordability-program)

### Oil to Heat Pump Affordability Program

*   **Status:** **OPEN FOR APPLICATIONS**
*   Helps eligible homeowners with **median income or less who currently heat their homes with oil** transition to an eligible heat pump system.
*   **No home energy evaluation may be needed** for this program.
*   **Financial Incentives:**
    *   **Up to $10,000** grant for changing oil heating to an eligible heat pump. Available to eligible Canadian homeowners in provinces/territories where the program is delivered exclusively by the federal government.
    *   **Up to $25,000** in grants or direct heat pump installation for eligible homeowners in participating co-delivered provinces/territories. *Funding amounts and program requirements vary by region.*
    *   **$250 additional** one-time incentive payment for eligible homeowners in co-delivered provinces/territories.
*   [Learn about the program](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/home-energy-efficiency/canada-greener-homes-initiative/oil-heat-pump-affordability-program)
