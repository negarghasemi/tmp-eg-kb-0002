# Room Air Conditioners Energy Efficiency Report

## Energy Efficiency Regulations

### Regulatory Definition
*A single-phase electric air conditioner that has a cooling capacity of 10.55 kilowatts (36,000 Btu/h) or less. It does not include a packaged terminal air conditioner, a portable air conditioner or a single package vertical air conditioner.*

### Compliance Date
*Manufactured on or after* **June 1, 2014**
*(For models manufactured prior, refer to the Regulations)*

### Compliant Product Models
[Room air conditioners listed on NRCans searchable database](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/product/?product=AC.Room&page=1)

### Labelling Requirements
**EnerGuide label required**  see [Schedule 3](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-311/page-38.html#h-837657).
*The label must be printed on, or affixed to, the principal display panel of the products package.*

### Testing Standard
**CSA-C368.1-14**

### Energy Efficiency Regulations by Province
*Some provinces also have their own energy efficiency regulations for many of the federally regulated products  [find out which ones!](/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-regulations/energy-products-regulated-federally-provincially)*
