## Financial Assistance Programs

### Federal Programs

*   **Canada Greener Homes Loan**
    *   **Offers:** Up to **$40,000** in interest-free loans for terms up to 10 years.
    *   **Details:** See more details about eligible retrofits and how to apply at the Natural Resource Canada site.
|    *   **Link:** [Canada Greener Homes Loan | CMHC](https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/funding-programs/all-funding-programs/canada-greener-homes)
*   **Federal Oil to Heat Pump Affordability Program**
    *   **Payment:** Up to **$10,000** to switch from oil heating to a new energy-efficient heat pump.
    *   **Eligibility:**
        *   Home is heated by oil.
        *   Household income is at or below the median household after-tax income level.
    *   **Eligible Measures:**
        *   Necessary electrical and mechanical upgrades for a new heat pump system.
        *   Installation of outdoor piping loops for ground source heat pump.
        *   Safe removal of oil tank.
        *   Installation of backup electrical heating (as required).
        *   Switching over other oil-using household systems, such as a water heater (where necessary).
    *   **Link:** [Federal Oil to Heat Pump Affordability Program](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/homes/canada-greener-homes-initiative/oil-heat-pump-affordability-program/24775)

### Provincial & Utility Rebates & Incentives

*   **Hydro One - Ontario Electricity Rebate (OER)**
    *   **Benefit:** 13.1% reduction on the amount of your bill before HST (as of November 1, 2024).
    *   **Eligibility:**
        *   Residential and Small Commercial customers (billed under a Residential or General Service Energy rate class) using less than 50 kW.
        *   Commercial accounts greater than 50 kW but using less than 250,000 kWh annually.
        *   _Automatically eligible, no self-declaration form needed._
    *   **Link:** [Ontario Electricity Rebate](https://www.hydroone.com/business-services/commercial-industrial-generators-and-ldcs/commercial-industrial-customers/ontario-electricity-rebate)
*   **Enbridge Fixed Incentive Program**
    *   **Offers:** Offsets the cost of upgrading to energy-efficient natural gas technology.
    *   **Assistance:** Energy Solutions Advisor helps identify opportunities, quantify potential savings, and provide incentives.
    *   **Link:** [Enbridge Fixed Incentive Program](https://www.enbridgegas.com/business-industrial/incentives-conservation/programs-and-incentives/equipment-upgrades/fixed-incentive-program)
*   **Enbridge Business Programs and Incentives**
    *   **Offers:** Rebates and incentives for equipment upgrades, new construction, retrofits, and energy assessments.
    *   **Link:** [Enbridge Business Programs and Incentives](https://www.enbridgegas.com/business-industrial/incentives-conservation/programs-and-incentives)
*   **Home Renovation Savings Program (Enbridge Gas & Save on Energy)**
    *   **Offers:** Rebates to help Ontarians improve home energy efficiency and comfort.
    *   **Rebates Available:** Up to **$5,000**.
    *   **Measures:** Solar installations, insulation, heat pumps, energy efficient upgrades.
    *   **Rebates requiring a Home Energy Assessment:**
        *   **Heat Pump Water Heaters:** $500
        *   **Windows and Doors:** $100 per rough opening
        *   **Insulation (attic, wall, foundation, exposed floor):** Up to $8,900
        *   **Air Sealing:** Up to $250
    *   **Rebates not requiring a Home Energy Assessment:**
        *   **Heat Pumps:** Up to $12,000
        *   **ASHP:** Up to $7,500
        *   **Solar Panels and Battery Storage:** Up to $10,000
        *   **Smart Thermostats:** $75
    *   **ASHP Eligibility:** Must be an Enbridge Gas customer (primary heat with natural gas furnace or boiler) OR connected to the Ontario electricity grid (heat electrically). Installed heat pump must be on [Natural Resources Canadas qualified products list](https://oee.nrcan.gc.ca/pml-lmp/index.cfm?language_langue=en&action=app.search-recherche&appliance=ASHP1_GH&_gl=1*l4wgjm*_ga*MTY0NzEzMDk4LjE3MDk1ODUzNTM.*_ga_C2N57Y7DX5*MTczMDgxODU1OS40MS4xLjE3MzA4MTg1OTIuMC4wLjA.).

    *   **Eligibility:** Homeowners meeting applicable affordability criteria.
    *   **Offers:** Funding for retrofits.
    *   **Process:** Must apply for funding and carry out pre-retrofit activities (energy audits and energy modeling studies).
    *   **Funding:** Up to 100% of eligible retrofit costs to a maximum of **$170,000 per unit**; forgivable loans will be either **$85,000 per unit** or 80% of eligible costs.
    *   **Link:** [Canada Green Affordable Housing Program](https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/funding-programs/all-funding-programs/canada-greener-affordable-housing-program)

    *   Show your banker that youve done your homework.

## DIY Energy Saving Tips

*   **Water Heater:**
    *   Wrap older water heaters in cold areas (basements) with an inexpensive blanket (cost: $30-$40) for significant savings.
*   **Curtains:**
    *   Use heavy fabric curtains to reduce cold, drafty air from windows.
*   **Cold Air (Drafts):**
    *   Install inexpensive foam insulators for electrical outlets on outside walls to stop drafts.
    *   Apply weather stripping around doors and windows.
*   **Air Filters & Heating System Maintenance:**
    *   Replace furnace filters regularly as per manufacturer's recommendations; dirty filters make furnaces work harder.
    *   Bleed hot water radiators at least once or twice a year for efficiency. [Handy video guide](https://youtu.be/IG5CvZRDQ_Q?feature=shared).
    *   Oil furnaces need annual maintenance. Natural gas and propane furnaces need servicing every two years.
    *   Keep vents and air grills clear of objects or furniture to ensure proper heat flow.
