# Ground-Source Heat Pumps

This document outlines the current challenges and ongoing research by CanmetENERGY regarding ground-source heat pumps, aiming to improve their energy efficiency and environmental impact.

### **Current Challenges & Drawbacks**
- **High Initial Cost**: The primary financial barrier is the significant upfront cost, mainly attributed to the underground heat exchanger.
- **Technological Optimization Needs**:
    - **Exchanger Development**: There is a need for smaller, more efficient, and less expensive heat exchangers.
    - **Refrigerant Performance**: Detailed information on the behavior of new refrigerants is crucial for maximizing component efficiency.
    - **Environmental Refrigerants**: The development and adoption of new, less harmful refrigerants are required.
- **Lack of Standards for Community Heating**: The absence of established standards for community-based heating applications discourages their adoption by contractors.
    - *Recommendation*: Demonstration sites are necessary to gather data, develop guidelines, and validate the feasibility of ground-source heat pumps for community use.

### **CanmetENERGY's Research Activities**
CanmetENERGY is actively pursuing research in the following areas to advance ground-source heat pump technology:

- **Development of a ground-source heat pump with CO in a secondary loop and a phase change**
    - **Focus**: Investigating the performance of ground-source heat pump systems utilizing CO with a phase change in a secondary ground loop.
    - **Methodology**: Involves creating a theoretical model to predict thermal and hydrodynamic phenomena within the heat exchanger, which will then be validated through a test bed to study dynamic behavior.

- **Direct Expansion (DX) Systems**
    - **Focus**: Addressing the current lack of technical data to optimize DX systems and facilitate the replacement of synthetic refrigerants.
    - **Goal**: Studying the viability of replacing R-22 with more environmentally friendly refrigerants, such as R-410a.
    - **Methodology**: Includes conducting field tests and developing a comprehensive simulation model.

- **Ground-Source Storage for Community Heating**
    - **Focus**: Conducting follow-up assessments of existing community heating systems that integrate ground-source heat pumps with thermal energy storage.
    - **System Feature**: These systems incorporate controllers designed for efficient management of heating and air conditioning between residential buildings and buildings with high heat output (exothermic buildings), like supermarkets and arenas.
    - **Expected Outcome**: The assessment results will contribute to the development of guidelines and enhance the planning and implementation of such systems.

### **Partnership Opportunities**
CanmetENERGY is actively seeking research partnerships with universities and industries that share a commitment to improving energy efficiency in buildings and reducing greenhouse gas emissions. Interested parties are encouraged to establish contact for potential collaborations.
