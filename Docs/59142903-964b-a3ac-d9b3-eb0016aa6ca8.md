# Air-source heat pumps

## Overview
An **ENERGY STAR certified air-source heat pump** uses **5% less energy**, on average, than a standard model. Air-source heat pumps that have earned the *ENERGY STAR Most Efficient* designation are the best of the best, saving even more energy. Saving energy saves money and reduces your carbon footprint.

Start your research using the [searchable product lists for ENERGY STAR air-source heat pumps](https://spl-lpi.nrcan-rncan.gc.ca/en-US/) to find and compare product models.

## How an Air-source Heat Pump Works
During cooler months, a heat pump uses electricity to extract heat from the outside air and transfer it into your home. Its advanced technology allows it to extract heat even during Canadas cold winter days. For cooling, it transfers warm indoor air to the outside. If you have an air conditioner, you're already familiar with this technology, as they work similarly.

Heat pumps transfer heat rather than generate it, making them energy-efficient while providing comfortable temperatures year-round.

*Learn more in the booklet: [Heating and Cooling with a Heat Pump](/energy-efficiency/energy-star/heating-cooling-heat-pump)*.

## Key Efficiency Ratings
When buying a heat pump, two ratings help determine the units efficiency:

*   **Heating Seasonal Performance Factors (HSPF2 Region V)**: Used to determine efficiency during winter.
*   **Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio (SEER2)**: Used for summer efficiency.

*In both cases, the higher the number, the better the efficiency.*

## Helpful Tips

*   **Sizing**: Finding the right heat pump size for your home is critical to maximize savings on energy bills and maintain comfort year-round. Ask your mechanical contractor for more information on sizing and selection.
*   **Thermostat Settings**: Set your thermostat at one temperature. Constant adjusting can lead to higher utility costs, especially in colder seasons.
*   **Winter Maintenance**: During winter, check for excessive ice or snow build-up on or around the heat pump. It must be removed for proper operation.
*   **Annual Servicing**: The best time to service your unit is at the end of summer, before the start of the next heating season.
*   **Clean Filters and Coils**: Keep your filters and coils clean. This dramatically impacts system performance and operating life. Do this monthly, but follow the manufacturers instructions for accuracy.
*   **Professional Inspection**: Hire a qualified service contractor for an annual inspection of your unit.

## Regulations
Air-source, ground-source, and water-source heat pumps are subject to **Canada's Energy Efficiency Regulations**, which set minimum performance standards for their energy efficiency. Find details in the [Guide to the Regulations](/energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency-regulations/guide-canada-s-energy-efficiency-regulations).
