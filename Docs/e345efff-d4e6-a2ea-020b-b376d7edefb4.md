# How to set your ceiling fan direction for each season

*A simple switch to your high-efficiency ceiling fan can get you through the summer months  and the colder ones, too.* 

## Article Summary

Ceiling fans can save energy year round by adjusting blade direction with the built-in switch. In summer, set fans counterclockwise to create a cooling breeze and reduce air conditioning use. In winter, switch to clockwise at low speed to recirculate warm air, lowering heating costs. Proper blade angles improve efficiency.

## Ceiling Fan Operation for Energy Savings

Nearly all ceiling fans have a switch that allows you to change the direction of the fan blades. Adjusting this setting helps control the temperature in your home, leading to savings on both cooling and heating costs.

### Why use a ceiling fan with my air conditioning?

While air conditioning units are effective for cooling, they can be expensive if they are the sole source of cooling. Ceiling fans can complement your AC unit and reduce overall cooling costs.

*   **Power Consumption Comparison:**
    *   **Standard High-Efficiency Central AC Unit:** **3,500 watts** (on average)
    *   **Ceiling Fan:** Approximately **50 watts**

To maximize energy efficiency, consider pairing your ceiling fan with an **ENERGY STAR-certified air conditioning unit**, which uses **eight percent less energy** than standard models.

### Which way should a ceiling fan turn to keep you cool in the summer?

*   **Summer Direction:** **Counterclockwise**
*   **Effect:** When spinning quickly in this direction, the fan pushes air down, creating a cool breeze. This helps maintain a consistent room temperature and reduces the need for the air conditioner to run constantly.

### Getting the right angle (Summer)

*   **Blade Angle:** Minimum of **12 degrees** for maximum cooling. Angles above 16 degrees may cause loose objects to blow around.
*   **Room Size Consideration:** If the room is larger than **500 square feet**, consider installing more than one fan.

### How do ceiling fans work for colder weather?

Using a ceiling fan in winter can help reduce heating costs by improving air circulation.

*   **Winter Direction:** **Clockwise**
*   **Speed:** **Low speed**
*   **Effect:** Warm air naturally rises. The gentle updraft created by a clockwise spin at low speed redistributes warm air accumulated near the ceiling throughout the room. This allows you to adjust your thermostat to a lower setting while still maintaining warmth, saving on heating costs.
