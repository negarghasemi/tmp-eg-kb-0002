# Home Energy Retrofits

Home energy retrofits are designed to provide residents with the most effective energy-saving installations and upgrades that immediately deliver greater comfort and savings on utility bills while reducing GHG emissions.

## Benefits of Home Energy Retrofits

-   **Greater comfort** in both heating and cooling seasons
-   **Lower utility bills**
-   **Reduction of harmful GHG emissions**
-   **Increased home value**

## What to Expect from a Home Energy Retrofit

Energy Coaches or a **NrCan (Natural Resources Canada) Registered Energy Advisor** can suggest the most impactful retrofits. These include:

### Specific Retrofit Recommendations

-   **Insulation**: Improves warmth in winter and comfort in summer for attics, basement walls, and crawl spaces.
-   **Blower Door Test**: Uses a machine to measure and locate air leakage in homes, identifying areas where warm air escapes and cold air enters.
-   **Weather Stripping**: Reduces drafts around doors, windows, and ductwork, a simple way to start saving energy.
-   **Air Source Heat Pump (ASHP)**: Can save up to **$1,000 per year** in heating and cooling costs compared to conventional gas furnaces and standard AC units.
-   **LED Lighting**: Switching to LED bulbs can save an average household about **$225 in energy costs per year**, as lighting accounts for approximately 15% of total electric costs.
-   **New Windows**: Installing Energy Star rated windows can save as much as **45% on an energy bill**, though it's an expensive investment best considered during construction.
-   **Water Efficiency**: Installing a low-flow toilet can save up to **$200 per year**. Low-flow shower heads and sink aerators also significantly reduce water consumption.

### Tools for Assessment

-   **Thermal Imaging Camera**: Provides visual information to identify where warm air is leaving and cold air is entering your home.

## Your Energy Coach

Energy Coaches offer **FREE calls (up to 45 minutes)** to consult and guide you through the retrofit process, from start to finish. They connect you with resources and assess beneficial energy retrofits.

### Preparing for the Call with an Energy Coach

To maximize your coaching session, have the following information ready:

-   **Size of your home**
-   **Utility information**
-   **Types of heating and cooling systems**
-   **Number of people in the house**
-   **Age of your home**
-   **Budget or cost concerns**

## Deep Retrofits: Achieving Net Zero

**Net Zero** means a home produces as much energy as it consumes, ideally from clean sources like solar and wind, and is up to 80% more energy efficient than conventionally built homes. Key components include:

-   **Building Envelope**: High-quality, high R-value insulation and a tight building envelope are crucial for overall system efficiency.
-   **Conservation**: High-efficiency HVAC (like Air Source Heat Pumps) systems and appliances, along with passive heating designs (e.g., window orientation), minimize energy use.
-   **Renewables**: Integration of clean, renewable energy sources is essential for reducing carbon emissions. A Net Zero Ready house is designed for easy installation of renewables.

## Financial Assistance: Rebates, Incentives & Loans

### Income-Qualified Programs (Free or Low Cost)

-   **Federal Oil to Heat Pump Affordability Program**: 
    -   **Payment**: Up to **$10,000** for switching from oil heating to an energy-efficient heat pump.
    -   **Eligibility**: Home heated by oil, household income at or below median.
    -   **Eligible Measures**: Electrical/mechanical upgrades, ground source piping loops, oil tank removal, backup electrical heating, switching oil-using household systems (e.g., water heater).
-   **100% Free Cold Climate Air Source Heat Pumps (Save on Energy)**:
    -   **Eligibility**: Qualifying Ontario households in electrically heated homes.
    -   **Benefits**: Two-year free maintenance warranty, 10-year equipment and labor warranty. May also include free ENERGY STAR appliances (refrigerators, freezers, window AC, lighting), weatherstripping, attic/basement insulation, smart thermostats, and power strips.
|-   **Home Winterproofing Program (HWP) | Enbridge Gas**: 
    -   **Description**: Streamlined with the Energy Affordability Program for free home energy upgrades.
    -   **Eligibility**: Home and household income levels qualify.
    -   **Benefits**: Free insulation, draft proofing, smart thermostat, Energy Star appliances, and more.
-   **Hydro One Low Income Energy Assistance Program (LEAP)**:
    -   **Assistance**: Up to **$1,000** on your bill (up to **$1,200** if electrically heated).
-   **Save on Energy  Energy Affordability Program**:
    -   **Description**: Provides expert advice and free energy-saving home upgrades for income-eligible households.
    -   **Eligibility**: Responsible for monthly electricity bill (homeowner or renter), based on household income/size or qualifying government/energy-support benefit. Residents of social housing may also be eligible.
    -   **Support Types**: Comprehensive upgrades or Energy Saving Kits.
-   **Ontario Electricity Support Program (OESP)**:
    -   **Description**: Lowers electricity bills for lower-income households with a monthly credit based on income and household size.

### Rebates & Incentives (General)

-   **Hydro One- Ontario Electricity Rebate (OER)**:
    -   **Rebate**: **13.1% reduction** on the bill amount (before HST) as of November 1, 2024.
    -   **Automatic Eligibility**: Residential and small commercial customers (<50 kW), or commercial accounts >50 kW but <250,000 kWh annually.
-   **Enbridge Fixed Incentive Program**: Helps offset the cost of upgrading to energy-efficient natural gas technology.
-   **Enbridge Business Programs and Incentives**: Offers rebates and incentives for equipment upgrades, new construction, retrofits, and energy assessments.
-   **Home Renovation Savings Program (Enbridge Gas & Save on Energy)**:
    -   **Rebates for Two or More Upgrades (require Home Energy Assessment)**:
        -   **Heat Pump Water Heaters**: **$500**
        -   **Windows and Doors**: **$100** per rough opening
        -   **Insulation** (attic, wall, foundation, exposed floor): Up to **$8,900**
        -   **Air Sealing**: Up to **$250**
    -   **Rebates for One or More Upgrades (no assessment required)**:
        -   **Heat Pumps**: Up to **$12,000**
        -   **Air Source Heat Pumps (ASHP)**: Up to **$7,500** (requires Enbridge Gas customer, primary heat from natural gas furnace/boiler or electric grid, and heat pump on Natural Resources Canadas qualified products list).
        -   **Solar Panels and Battery Storage**: Up to **$10,000**
        -   **Smart Thermostats**: **$75**

### Loans & Financing

-   **Canada Greener Homes Loan**: 
    -   **Amount**: Up to **$40,000** in interest-free loans.
    -   **Term**: Up to 10 years.
    -   **Details**: Applicable for eligible retrofits; apply via Natural Resources Canada.
-   **Clean Energy Loan  RBC Energy Saver Loan**: 
    -   **Benefits**: 1% interest rate reduction or a $100 rebate on a clean energy audit for RBC customers purchasing hybrid cars, ENERGY STAR appliances, or renewable energy projects.
-   **Mortgage Insurance Loan Rebate**: 
    -   **Rebate**: **25%** mortgage loan insurance rebate offered by CMHC, Sagen, and Canada Guaranty.
    -   **Eligibility**: For buying a certified green home (R-2000, ENERGY STAR, Built Green, LEED, etc.).
-   **Canada Green Affordable Housing Program**:
    -   **Funding**: Up to 100% of eligible retrofit costs to a maximum of **$170,000 per unit**.
    -   **Loan Type**: Forgivable loans (either $85,000 per unit or 80% of eligible costs).
    -   **Eligibility**: Homeowners meeting applicable affordability criteria, after applying for funding and completing pre-retrofit activities (e.g., energy audits).

## Handy at Home? Quick Fix DIY Tips

Many quick fixes can be done at little or no cost, providing immediate comfort and cost savings:

-   **Cold Air Drafts**:
    -   **Electrical Outlets**: Inexpensive foam insulators can stop drafts from electrical outlets on outside walls.
    -   **Weather Stripping**: Easy to install around doors and windows to prevent cold air entry and warm air escape.
-   **Air Filters**:
    -   **Furnace Filters**: Replace regularly per manufacturer's recommendations; dirty filters make your furnace work harder.
    -   **Hot Water Radiators**: Bleed at least once or twice a year for efficient operation.
    -   **Furnace Maintenance**: Oil furnaces need annual maintenance; natural gas and propane furnaces need servicing every two years.
    -   **Ventilation**: Keep heat flowing by not blocking vents and air grills with objects or furniture.
-   **Water Heater**:
    -   Consider a water heater blanket ($30-$40) for older models, especially if located in cold areas like basements, for significant cost savings.
-   **Curtains**:
    -   Use heavy fabric curtains to reduce cold, drafty air from windows.
-   **Light Bulbs**:
    -   Switch to LED light bulbs; they last up to 100,000 hours (14 years) and can save hundreds of dollars annually.
